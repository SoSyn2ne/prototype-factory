### Title

- **Theme:** 건강

- **Target user:** Korean office workers and founders in repeated overtime cycles who know their body is slipping but need a calm, non-preachy way to prioritize recovery actions.
- **Key UX:** Turn scattered health guilt into a ranked recovery sequence that shows which small habit or appointment will reduce the most physical damage first.
- **Required UI sections:** body debt snapshot, sleep and stimulant load timeline, symptom severity map, recovery action priority ladder, two-week energy rebuild planner, excuse breaker note drawer.
- **Design profile:** calm-consumer
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a desktop recovery planner called Overtime Body Debt Planner for office workers and founders stuck in repeated overtime cycles. It should help users translate sleep loss, caffeine dependence, weight drift, and pain signals into a realistic two-week body damage control plan instead of vague guilt. Include a body debt snapshot, a sleep and stimulant load timeline, a symptom severity map, a recovery action priority ladder, a two-week energy rebuild planner, and an excuse breaker note drawer. Design direction: use a calm-consumer aesthetic with soft daylight tones, reassuring card groupings, generous breathing room, and gentle status cues so the product feels restorative, trustworthy, and easy to return to after an exhausting day.
