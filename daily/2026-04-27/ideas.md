## Candidate 1 — Practical / Commercial
### Title
Clause Trap Scanner

- **Theme:** 자산 방어
- **One-liner:** A plain-Korean contract red-flag desk that helps renters, freelancers, and subscription buyers spot hidden fees, unfair exits, and liability traps before signing.
- **Target user:** Korean renters, freelancers, gym members, and service buyers who are worried a contract or membership term will quietly cost them money later.
- **Key UX:** Paste or review a clause set, see ranked money-risk cards, compare exit options, and leave with a clean negotiation checklist instead of vague fear.
- **Required UI sections:** contract risk scorecard, hidden fee radar, exit penalty timeline, liability shift highlighter, plain-Korean rewrite panel, negotiation checklist drawer
- **Design profile:** financial-analyst
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a desktop contract red-flag workspace called Clause Trap Scanner for Korean renters, freelancers, and subscription buyers who need to protect their money before signing confusing terms. It should help users scan clauses, rank hidden fees and unfair exits, highlight liability shifts, and produce a plain-Korean negotiation checklist. Include a contract risk scorecard, a hidden fee radar, an exit penalty timeline, a liability shift highlighter, a plain-Korean rewrite panel, and a negotiation checklist drawer. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with disciplined comparison tables, navy and graphite surfaces, precise risk badges, and restrained red warning accents so the product feels serious, evidence-led, and built for asset-defense decisions.
