# Prototype ideas — 2026-04-01

## p001 — Parcel Dimension Fee Radar
- **One-liner:** A shipping-ops desk that flags cartons most likely to trigger dimensional-weight or parcel compliance fees before labels are purchased.
- **Target user:** Ecommerce fulfillment leads and 3PL operators shipping mixed-size parcels across major carriers.
- **Key UX:** See which outbound orders are margin-dangerous, why the surcharge risk exists, and what packaging swap fixes it fastest.
- **Required UI sections:** Margin-at-risk hero, outbound parcel watchlist, dimension variance inspector, carrier rule comparison panel, packaging swap recommender, surcharge exposure summary rail.
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a desktop web app screen called "Parcel Dimension Fee Radar" for ecommerce fulfillment leads and 3PL operators. Use a disciplined shipping-ops visual direction with deep navy, cool gray, and signal orange accents; make hierarchy obvious, tables highly scannable, and the overall product feel practical, cost-sensitive, and warehouse-ready. Include: (1) a margin-at-risk hero with today's at-risk shipments, projected surcharge exposure, and primary CTA, (2) an outbound parcel watchlist showing order, SKU mix, box size, carrier, and risk score, (3) a dimension variance inspector comparing declared vs measured dimensions, (4) a carrier rule comparison panel for DIM thresholds, oversize triggers, and fee types, (5) a packaging swap recommender suggesting smaller cartons or split shipments, and (6) a surcharge exposure summary rail with save potential by action. Use realistic mocked shipping data, desktop-first layout, concise labels, and no code blocks.

## p002 — AI Seat Offboarding Desk
- **One-liner:** A security and IT operations workspace that closes former employees' AI tool access before data leaks through forgotten seats and shared workspaces.
- **Target user:** IT admins and operations managers at small-to-mid-sized companies juggling ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Notion AI, and other SaaS tools.
- **Key UX:** Review departure-related access risk in one pass, spot unrevoked AI seats instantly, and run a clean offboarding checklist without hunting across admin consoles.
- **Required UI sections:** Offboarding risk hero, departing-user queue, AI seat inventory panel, shared workspace exposure card, revocation checklist, audit-ready timeline.
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a premium desktop admin workspace called "AI Seat Offboarding Desk" for IT admins and operations managers managing former employees' access to AI tools. Use a sober security-first design direction with graphite, off-white, and restrained crimson accents; emphasize crisp status hierarchy, calm compliance cues, and an overall product feel that is trustworthy, modern, and audit-ready. Include: (1) an offboarding risk hero with open departures, unrevoked AI seats, and urgent CTA, (2) a departing-user queue listing person, team, last day, and risk tier, (3) an AI seat inventory panel covering ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Notion AI, and shared API projects, (4) a shared workspace exposure card for team spaces, uploaded files, and external connectors, (5) a revocation checklist with completed/pending states, and (6) an audit-ready timeline showing revoke, transfer, export, and signoff events. Use realistic mocked admin data, desktop-first composition, concise labels, and no code blocks.

## p003 — Fuel Spike Route Margin Board
- **One-liner:** A field-service margin board that shows which jobs become unprofitable when fuel costs jump and how to rebalance the day before trucks roll.
- **Target user:** Owners and dispatch managers at small HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, and repair businesses running multi-stop crews.
- **Key UX:** See route-level profit erosion immediately, compare regrouping options, and choose the least painful dispatch change before the first appointment.
- **Required UI sections:** Daily margin hero, crew route timeline, fuel exposure map, job repricing or regrouping panel, customer sensitivity queue, recovered-margin footer.
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a polished desktop operations dashboard called "Fuel Spike Route Margin Board" for owners and dispatch managers at small field-service businesses. Use a pragmatic fleet-ops design direction with midnight blue, warm white, and high-visibility lime accents; keep hierarchy bold, route blocks easy to scan, and the overall product feel energetic, action-oriented, and financially aware. Include: (1) a daily margin hero with scheduled jobs, fuel cost delta, and urgent CTA, (2) a crew route timeline showing stops, drive time, and slack windows, (3) a fuel exposure map summarizing long-haul routes and high-burn crews, (4) a repricing or regrouping panel comparing route merge, reschedule, fuel surcharge, or keep-as-is actions, (5) a customer sensitivity queue showing VIPs, low-margin jobs, and cancellation risk, and (6) a recovered-margin footer with before/after totals. Use realistic mocked field-service data, desktop-first layout, crisp cards, and no code snippets.
