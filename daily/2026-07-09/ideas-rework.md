# Prototype Factory Phase A Rework - 2026-07-09

Reason: operator feedback said the first July 9 slate was weak. Diagnosis: too many evidence/claim/ops consoles, too much defensive merchant paperwork, not enough "proven pattern + sharp twist." This rework treats the earlier p001-p008 as a bad batch and proposes a replacement slate.

## What To Drop From The First Slate

- Drop or heavily demote: AI Spend Hold Music, Merchant Fee Negotiation Table, Digital Passport Supplier Chase, Chargeback Evidence Autobox, Guest Damage Claim Desk, Teacher Parent Email Shield.
- Reason: they are plausible chores, but the batch feels like six versions of "paperwork dashboard."
- Keep only as raw signal, not as product direction: small business cost pain, chargebacks, teacher conflict, travel refund evidence.
- Keep the stronger direction: Camouflage Shelf Sprint and Refund Queue Boss Fight had better interaction shape, but still need less generic framing.

## Replacement Principles

- Start from a known pattern: Prop Hunt, drawing/guessing games, gacha/trading, mini-game feeds, Splitwise, creator hook analysis, office-file QA, social voting.
- Change one or two things sharply: Korean/local context, 15-second rule, tactile browser interaction, friend/viewer participation, screenshot-share result.
- Avoid "dashboard as answer." If a candidate is practical, give it a game-like action loop or a dead-simple artifact.
- Candidate fields must lead with `reference_pattern`, `twist`, `playable_hook`, and `spread_trigger`.

## Replacement Candidate Slate

### p001 - Pocket Game Oven
- reference_pattern: Meta Pocket / TikTok mini-game feed / prompt-to-play gizmo.
- twist: Instead of "generate any app," force creators into eight proven 15-second game molds: hide, guess, stack, sort, bluff, vote, race, reveal.
- playable_hook: Pick a mold, type one weird object or niche, and get a playable rule card plus first-screen mockup.
- target user: creators, indie hackers, teachers, brand social teams.
- first screen: a vertical feed of tiny playable cards with "steal this rule" and "remix in 30 seconds."
- spread_trigger: each generated rule card is a shareable screenshot or prompt for friends to play.
- why stronger: it directly follows the new policy: existing mini-game feed pattern, constrained twist, obvious output.

### p002 - Convenience Store Chameleon
- reference_pattern: Prop Hunt / MECCHA CHAMELEON / hidden-object games.
- twist: Korean convenience-store shelf photos become the hiding map; players tint, stretch, and rotate a snack mascot or sticker to disappear among real products.
- playable_hook: "Hide your mascot in 20 seconds; your friend has 10 seconds to tap it."
- target user: friend groups, streamers, students, snack brands.
- first screen: GS25/CU-style shelf playfield, sticker palette, seeker timer, reveal replay.
- spread_trigger: the reveal image and "how did you miss this?" moment are clip-friendly.
- why stronger: same proven hide/fool loop, but local, visual, and immediately understandable.

### p003 - Photocard Blind Trade Room
- reference_pattern: gacha pull tracker + trading card marketplace + fandom wishlist.
- twist: K-pop photocard collectors scan pulls, mark duplicates, and enter a live "fair trade" room that shows who needs what.
- playable_hook: "Open pack -> mark duplicate -> find a trade match before the timer ends."
- target user: K-pop fans, merch sellers, event organizers.
- first screen: binder grid, duplicate stack, wishlist lane, trade fairness meter, QR room invite.
- spread_trigger: collectors share binder status and trade rooms in fan chats.
- why stronger: proven gacha/trading behavior with a Korean-first niche and clear social loop.

### p004 - Hook Fight Club
- reference_pattern: TikTok/Reels creator analytics + party voting + A/B testing.
- twist: Creators bring three hooks/thumbnails; viewers guess which one wins before seeing AI/benchmark feedback.
- playable_hook: "Two hooks enter, one survives the scroll."
- target user: short-form creators, marketers, students learning content.
- first screen: bracket battle with two short hooks, swipe-vote, predicted retention, rewrite button.
- spread_trigger: bracket results and roast-style feedback are easy to post back to creator groups.
- why stronger: creator tools already exist, but this makes feedback social and game-like.

### p005 - Deck Error Speedrun
- reference_pattern: OfficeCLI / document QA / "spot the difference" puzzle.
- twist: Instead of a document automation dashboard, turn PPT/Excel checking into a timed bug hunt: wrong totals, stale dates, off-brand slide, broken appendix.
- playable_hook: "Find 7 meeting-killing mistakes before the boss opens the deck."
- target user: consultants, analysts, startup operators, students.
- first screen: slide thumbnails, error timer, red-flag cards, final "safe to send" stamp.
- spread_trigger: users share the error receipt or challenge coworkers to beat their time.
- why stronger: keeps the GitHub/office-file signal but gives it a tense action loop.

### p006 - Split Bill Court
- reference_pattern: Splitwise + small-claims courtroom + group voting game.
- twist: Instead of quiet expense splitting, roommates/friends submit disputed charges and the room votes with evidence cards.
- playable_hook: "Who pays for the mystery delivery fee? Present evidence, then vote."
- target user: roommates, travel groups, couples, college friends.
- first screen: receipt photo, disputed line items, plaintiff/defendant cards, verdict split.
- spread_trigger: funny verdict screenshots move naturally in group chats.
- why stronger: familiar money pain, but framed as a social game rather than finance software.

### p007 - Room Makeover Guess Duel
- reference_pattern: before/after interior apps + spot-the-difference + viewer voting.
- twist: Users upload a room photo, hide three tiny changes, and friends race to find what changed or vote on the better layout.
- playable_hook: "Can your friend find the three changes before the reveal?"
- target user: interior hobbyists, real-estate agents, couples moving houses, Pinterest users.
- first screen: before/after slider, hidden-change pins, timer, vote board.
- spread_trigger: before/after reveals are inherently shareable and less boring than a planning board.
- why stronger: uses a proven before/after format with direct interaction.

### p008 - Refund Boss Rush
- reference_pattern: boss fight + evidence checklist.
- twist: Keep the useful refund evidence idea, but make it a true card-battle loop with airline/platform delay tactics as enemies.
- playable_hook: "Stack the right proof cards before the deadline bar empties."
- target user: travelers, consumers, consumer-rights creators.
- first screen: boss meter, evidence hand, deadline bar, escalation move, victory receipt.
- spread_trigger: the victory receipt doubles as a real checklist and a funny share artifact.
- why stronger: one practical rescue idea survives because the game loop is clear.
