- **Theme:** 사교육

- **Target user:** Parents of elementary to high-school students who are trying to secure hagwon or tutoring slots in the next 30 days without overpaying or overcommuting.
- **Key UX:** Compare academies side by side, see which waitlists are still realistically winnable, and turn family panic into a single enrollment attack plan before seats disappear.
- **Required UI sections:** registration deadline strip, academy comparison matrix, waitlist probability board, commute and calendar conflict map, monthly spend pressure panel, enrollment action plan drawer
- **Design profile:** operator-dense
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a desktop enrollment decision console called Academy Waitlist Escape Planner for parents trying to secure hagwon or tutoring seats before the next registration window closes. It should help them compare academy options, estimate which waitlists are still realistically winnable, understand commute and schedule friction, and choose the least painful path without blowing up the monthly budget. Include a registration deadline strip, an academy comparison matrix, a waitlist probability board, a commute and calendar conflict map, a monthly spend pressure panel, and an enrollment action plan drawer. Design direction: use an operator-dense aesthetic with disciplined navy and slate structure, compact comparison modules, one urgent amber action accent, and ruthlessly scannable hierarchy so the product feels deadline-aware, parent-practical, and immediately decisive.
