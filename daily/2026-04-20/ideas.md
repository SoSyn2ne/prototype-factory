## Candidate 1 — Practical / Commercial
### Title
Storm Claim Deductible Desk

- **Theme:** 자산 방어
- **One-liner:** A homeowner decision console that compares insurance claim math, contractor timing, and out-of-pocket risk before storm damage turns into a bad financial move.
- **Target user:** Homeowners dealing with roof, siding, or water damage in the first two weeks after a storm who need to decide whether to file a claim, pay cash, or delay work.
- **Key UX:** Compare claim-versus-cash scenarios, see deductible pain against future premium risk, and leave with one defensible next move before contractors or adjusters control the story.
- **Required UI sections:** damage cost snapshot, claim-versus-cash comparison matrix, deductible and premium risk panel, contractor timing board, evidence checklist, recommended move drawer.
- **Design profile:** financial-analyst
- **Stitch prompt:** Design a desktop homeowner decision console called Storm Claim Deductible Desk for people dealing with roof, siding, or water damage right after a storm. It should help them compare insurance claim math, deductible pain, contractor timing, evidence readiness, and likely out-of-pocket exposure before they choose whether to file a claim or pay cash. Include a damage cost snapshot, a claim-versus-cash comparison matrix, a deductible and premium risk panel, a contractor timing board, an evidence checklist, and a recommended move drawer. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with deep navy structure, off-white data surfaces, muted green and restrained amber cues, and premium analytical hierarchy so the product feels fiduciary, calm, and protective under financial stress.
