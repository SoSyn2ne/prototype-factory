# operator-dense

- Use disciplined B2B operator-console styling.
- Prefer deep navy, charcoal, slate, fog, sand, or restrained industrial neutrals.
- Use one sharp accent color for urgency or action.
- Keep hierarchy highly scannable.
- Make tables, queues, timelines, and KPI cards crisp and compact.
- Use a single action accent, 40px controls, tabular numbers, and compact 32-40px rows.
- Avoid decorative hero sections, mixed chart palettes, oversized cards, or modal-heavy flows.
- Product feel: practical, margin-aware, confident, and no-nonsense.
