# calm-consumer

- Use soft, reassuring, guidance-friendly styling.
- Prefer warm neutrals, low-contrast supportive surfaces, and one gentle accent.
- Reduce visual stress while keeping next actions obvious.
- Favor cards, step flows, and friendly information grouping.
- Use one gentle accent, generous section spacing, clear next-step buttons, and helper text that explains recovery from mistakes.
- Avoid alarm red except for true risk, dense data walls, shame-heavy copy, or tiny form controls.
- Product feel: approachable, helpful, clear, and emotionally steady.
