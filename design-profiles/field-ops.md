# field-ops

- Use a firm, trustworthy service-operations visual system.
- Prefer slate blue, steel, graphite, cool gray, or industrial neutrals.
- Use a vivid cyan, orange, or similar action accent sparingly.
- Dense planning surfaces are fine, but must remain legible under pressure.
- Use high-contrast status rows, obvious handoff/checklist states, 44px touch targets where field use is plausible, and urgent color only in small badges or dots.
- Avoid low-contrast maps, tiny controls, ambiguous severity colors, or purely decorative dispatch dashboards.
- Product feel: disciplined, reliable, operational, and action-first.
