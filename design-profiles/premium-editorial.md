# premium-editorial

- Use refined, composed, high-trust styling with editorial restraint.
- Prefer warm charcoal, cream, stone, brass, cocoa, or muted luxury tones.
- Typography should feel elegant but still fast to scan.
- Cards should feel curated, not cluttered.
- Use typography, spacing, and restrained contrast as the polish layer; keep one quiet CTA color and a consistent radius.
- Avoid beige-on-beige monotony, luxury gradients, weak contrast, or decorative cards that do not advance the workflow.
- Product feel: premium, calm, polished, and quietly revenue-smart.
