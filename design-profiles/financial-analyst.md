# financial-analyst

- Use analytical, high-signal, institutional workbench styling.
- Prefer ink, graphite, off-white, navy, muted green, or restrained warning tones.
- Prioritize comparison, trend reading, and confidence cues.
- Layout should feel premium but data-forward.
- Use restrained KPI cards, 2:1 number/unit hierarchy, quiet confidence badges, and charts paired with written interpretation.
- Avoid rainbow legends, fake precision, decorative market gradients, or equal-weight metrics.
- Product feel: rigorous, intelligent, decision-oriented, and calm under ambiguity.
