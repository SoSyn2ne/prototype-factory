# Medicaid Spenddown Paper Trail - PRD

## Core Purpose
To help adult children, spouses, and POAs organize financial facts and documents required for long-term-care Medicaid applications, spenddowns, and renewals.

## Target Users
- Adult children managing a parent's transition to a nursing home.
- Spouses ("Community Spouse") protecting assets.
- Elder-care coordinators and social workers.

## Key Screens to Generate
1. **Intake Dashboard**: Household and state configuration (State, marital status, living arrangement).
2. **Asset & Income Snapshot**: Overview of Social Security, bank accounts, and resources.
3. **Spenddown Action Ledger**: Tracking qualified expenses (pre-need funerals, medical bills, home repairs).
4. **Five-Year Lookback Vault**: Document organization for bank statements, deeds, and gift records.
5. **Renewal & Deadline Tracker**: Countdown for proof of spenddown and annual redeterminations.
6. **Professional Handoff Packet**: A summary view ready for an elder-law attorney or social worker.

## Design Constraints
- **Not legal advice**: Clear disclaimers throughout.
- **Accessibility**: Large text, high contrast.
- **Tone**: Calm, organized, supportive.
- **Sample Data**: Use 'Mary' (Nursing Home resident) and 'John' (Spouse at home) with typical income/asset scenarios.
