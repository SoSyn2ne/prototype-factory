MCG A-0239: Lumbar Spine Surgery

drag_indicator
Crit. 1.1 Imaging Requirement

MRI or CT scan within the last 6 months demonstrating significant spinal stenosis, disc herniation, or other structural anomaly corresponding to clinical symptoms.

drag_indicator
Crit. 2.4 Conservative Care

Failure of at least 6 weeks of non-operative treatment including physical therapy, NSAIDs, or epidural steroid injections, unless progressive neurological deficit is present.

InterQual: Pain Assessment

drag_indicator
Sec. B Subjective Pain Reporting

Patient must report severe pain impacting Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), typically indicated by a score of 7 or higher on a 10-point Visual Analog Scale (VAS) or equivalent metric.
