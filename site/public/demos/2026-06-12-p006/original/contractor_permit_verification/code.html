help Board FAQ

Why is Workers Comp required?

Mandated by Municipal Code 4A to insulate the HOA from liability claims related to on-site injuries during EV charger installation.

What if the City Permit is delayed?

The board may grant 'Conditional Approval' pending final permit issuance. Physical work cannot commence until the permit is uploaded.
