Action & Sharing Toolkit

Use these templates to notify relevant institutions and warn your community securely.

account_balance Payment Dispute Notes

Provide this formal summary to your bank's fraud department.

To Whom It May Concern, I am writing to formally dispute a series of unauthorized/fraudulent transactions totaling [AMOUNT] posted to my account ending in [XXXX] between [START DATE] and [END DATE]. These charges were processed under the merchant name [BROKER/MERCHANT NAME]. I have gathered substantial evidence indicating this entity operates as a fraudulent scheme. I did not receive the promised services/goods, and my attempts to resolve this directly with the merchant have been ignored or met with deceptive practices. I request an immediate freeze on further debits from this merchant and a formal chargeback investigation under my cardholder rights. Supporting documentation (communications, transaction records) is attached. Sincerely, [YOUR NAME] [CONTACT INFO]

forum Community Warning Draft

Anonymized text for Telegram, WhatsApp, or community forums.

🚨 URGENT WARNING regarding [BROKER/PLATFORM NAME] 🚨 Please be advised that [PLATFORM NAME] is currently exhibiting severe red flags indicative of a scam. They are currently blocking withdrawals and requesting "tax payments" or "verification fees" to release funds. This is a classic advance-fee fraud tactic. Do NOT send them any more money. If you have funds with them, contact your bank immediately to dispute the charges. Stay safe.
