NY Paid Family Leave Rate Adjustment

gavel Regulatory Abstract

Ref ID: NY-PFL-24-B

The New York State Department of Financial Services has announced a revision to the employee contribution rate for Paid Family Leave (PFL) effective January 1, 2024. The maximum employee contribution will decrease, while the maximum weekly benefit will increase, necessitating immediate payroll configuration adjustments.

COMPLIANCE DIRECTIVE

TO: Payroll Operations, HR Partners
DATE: November 15, 2023
FROM: Regulatory Compliance Office
SUBJECT: 2024 NY PFL Rate Adjustment

1. Executive Summary

This memorandum serves as official notification regarding mandatory payroll system updates required for compliance with the State of New York's 2024 Paid Family Leave (PFL) regulations.

2. Required System Configurations

  • Update the PFL employee contribution rate from the 2023 rate of 0.455% to the new 2024 rate of 0.373%.
  • Adjust the annualized maximum employee contribution cap to $333.25.
  • Ensure these changes are effective for all payrolls processing with check dates on or after January 1, 2024.

Failure to implement these updates prior to the first payroll cycle of 2024 will result in over-withholding of employee taxes and potential regulatory penalities.
