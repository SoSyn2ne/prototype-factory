# PRD - Solomaxxing Date-Cost Simulator

## Problem
Single Gen Z users turn dating-app fatigue and expensive dates into intentional solo plans they can actually feel proud of.

## Target user
Gen Z singles, recently broken-up adults, and friend groups reframing solo time as a status-positive lifestyle choice.

## Key UX
User compares date cost, emotional recovery, hobby goals, social pressure, and weekend energy; the app builds a solomaxxing itinerary, savings flex, and shareable solo-progress card.

## Required UI sections
- Solo status header with date-cost savings and energy budget
- Dating pressure vs personal-goal tradeoff sliders
- Weekend itinerary builder for hobby, rest, fitness, culture, and friend touchpoints
- Spend comparison for date night vs solo upgrade
- Confidence ritual and no-settling pledge cards
- Social-pressure response script
- Shareable solomaxxing progress poster

## Assigned design profile
playful-experimental

## Theme
외로움 / 역전 / 자산 방어 - making singlehood feel intentional instead of failed.

## Source signal references
- S14 - Wired, solomaxxing among Gen Z singles links dating costs, app fatigue, and intentional singlehood as a lifestyle signal: https://www.wired.com/story/solomaxxing

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Solomaxxing Date-Cost Simulator" for Gen Z singles turning expensive dating fatigue into intentional solo plans. The first screen must be a working simulator with a solo status header, dating pressure vs personal-goal sliders, weekend itinerary builder, date-night vs solo-upgrade spend comparison, confidence ritual cards, no-settling pledge, social-pressure response script, and shareable progress poster. Use realistic sample data: $189 average date cost, three dating-app matches ignored, pottery class, gym block, solo dinner, friend voice note, $72 saved this weekend, and a post-breakup confidence goal. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with a bold shareable first impression, strong but controlled accent color, crisp hierarchy, and a poster-like output that still leaves the main workflow obvious. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: no custom demo implementation.
- Stitch output must preserve the assigned design profile.
- First screen must be the working app, not a marketing landing page.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the core decision or field workflow within 10 seconds.
- Every required UI section appears in the generated Stitch output.
- The cited source signals are visible as problem evidence in the product framing.
