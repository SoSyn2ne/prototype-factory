# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Users say the funnel is obvious and doesn’t change what they do next.
2. Suggested nudges feel cringe/impolite and are not usable without heavy edits.
3. “Reason tags” are seen as judgmental or too speculative.
4. Real-world data is too messy (multiple channels, partial replies), making the model unusable.
5. Users demand features like automatic messaging, contact import, or calendar reminders as table stakes.
6. Segment filters don’t match how users think about guests.
7. The tool increases stress (too many flags) rather than reducing it.
