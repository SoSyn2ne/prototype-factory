# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. A merchant cannot understand the dispute deadline and packet status within 30 seconds.
2. The workflow looks like a generic payment dashboard instead of a timed evidence room.
3. The packet export does not include customer messages, fulfillment proof, policy proof, and refund history.
4. The design profile is not visibly operator-dense.
5. Source signals S1 and S2 are not referenced in the concept notes.
6. The prototype implies guaranteed chargeback wins or gives legal advice.
7. Any placeholder text remains in the spec.
