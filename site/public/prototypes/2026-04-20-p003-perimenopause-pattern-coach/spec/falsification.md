# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users maintain symptom logging long enough to see value?
2. Would a mobile-first habit loop be necessary for real adoption?
3. Does the concept need clinician endorsement to feel credible?
4. Are symptoms too heterogeneous for a shared planning template?
5. Will users prefer education content over tracking surfaces?
6. Does the word perimenopause help discovery or scare off earlier-stage users?
7. Can a calming UI still feel medically serious enough to trust?
