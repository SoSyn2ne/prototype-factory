# PRD — Perimenopause Pattern Coach

## Problem
- A symptom-pattern planning dashboard that helps women connect sleep, mood, cycle shifts, and recovery habits into a calmer weekly health plan before daily life starts unraveling.
- Women navigating perimenopause often get scattered symptoms and vague reassurance, but lack a simple pattern-tracking surface that turns confusion into actionable weekly insight.

## Target user
- Women in their late 30s to early 50s who feel their energy, sleep, mood, or cycle is becoming unpredictable and want a structured way to regain control.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should grasp their strongest symptom patterns in under 6 minutes.
- Budget: Version one should avoid wearable and EHR dependencies, relying on manual entry.
- Platform: Desktop-first guided planning product for weekly reflection and doctor prep.

## Non-goals
- Medical diagnosis or hormone prescription management.
- A full telehealth product or symptom forum.
- Generic period tracking without pattern interpretation.

## Success metrics
- Users can identify recurring triggers and relief habits in one view.
- Users feel more prepared and less dismissed in the next clinician conversation.
- The experience reduces health anxiety by making patterns more legible.
