# PRD - English Kindergarten Anxiety Ledger

## Problem
- Korean parents compare English-kindergarten cost, policy uncertainty, commute fatigue, and status pressure without drowning in mom-chat panic.

## Target user
- Korean parents of preschool children deciding whether to enroll, continue, switch, or resist English-kindergarten spending.

## Key UX
- Parent builds a monthly ledger that weighs tuition, commute, child fatigue, sibling fairness, policy uncertainty, and status pressure, then produces a family conversation card.

## Required UI sections
- Family pressure header with monthly tuition, child age, commute, and spouse stance
- Cost stack for tuition, shuttle, books, uniforms, extra tutoring, and make-up classes
- Policy uncertainty lane for age rules, official statistics, and school-plan risk
- Child fatigue and readiness check-in
- Sibling fairness calculator
- Mom-chat claim parking lot that separates evidence from fear
- Family decision card with enroll, wait, switch, or cap-spend options

## Assigned design profile
- calm-consumer

## Theme
- 사교육 / 체면 - prestige anxiety, child readiness, and household affordability.

## Source signal references
- S6
- S7

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "English Kindergarten Anxiety Ledger" for Korean parents comparing English-kindergarten cost, policy uncertainty, commute fatigue, and status pressure without drowning in mom-chat panic. The first screen must be a working family decision surface with a pressure header, cost stack, policy uncertainty lane, child fatigue check-in, sibling fairness calculator, mom-chat claim parking lot, and family decision card. Use realistic sample data: KRW 1,545,000 monthly tuition, 38-minute commute, shuttle fee, five-year-old child, sibling entering daycare, policy rumor flagged as unverified, and grandparent pressure note. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with reassuring step cards, warm neutral surfaces, gentle blue-green accents, and copy that lowers shame while keeping the decision concrete. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: do not implement a custom demo.
- First screen in Stitch must be the working app, not a marketing landing page.
- Use external sources as problem evidence only, not market-size proof.

## Non-goals
- No external API integration in the prototype.
- No legal, medical, financial, or regulatory advice claims beyond workflow support.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the pain in under 10 seconds.
- User can identify the next action from the first screen.
- The prototype includes every required UI section from this PRD.
