# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Deposit disputes are emotionally urgent because the money is already mentally allocated to the next home or moving costs.
- Renters already gather photos, receipts, and message screenshots, but the evidence is usually scattered across apps.

## ASSUMPTION
- A deadline-driven evidence workflow will feel more actionable than generic legal advice pages.
- Users will value simple negotiation-path comparisons even before they decide whether to escalate.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether landlords or agents respond differently when evidence is presented in a structured summary.
- How much jurisdiction-specific policy detail is required before the product feels trustworthy.
