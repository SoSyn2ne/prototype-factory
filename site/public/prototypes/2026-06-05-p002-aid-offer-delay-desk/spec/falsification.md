# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Schools cannot export useful FAFSA/package status rows.
2. Existing SIS/CRM workflows already cover this pain well.
3. Compliance concerns prevent plain-language outreach templates.
4. Students still call even after proactive messages.
5. Aid offices lack budget for an overlay tool.
6. Bursar and enrollment teams refuse shared workflow ownership.
7. Delay causes resolve too quickly to justify a product.
