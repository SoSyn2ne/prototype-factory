# PRD — Handmade EU Safety Tagger

## Problem
- A GPSR prep tool that helps Etsy and Shopify makers turn messy product details into EU-safe labels, responsible-person fields, and listing blockers.
- Evidence sources: Signal 3, Signal 4.

## Target user
- Handmade sellers, small-batch product makers, Shopify microbrands, Etsy shop owners, and export-curious creators selling physical goods into the EU without a compliance team.

## Key UX
- Pick a product type, paste listing copy, upload ingredient/material notes, packaging photos, supplier details, and customer warnings; the tool maps GPSR fields, flags missing Responsible Person or manufacturer data, drafts safety labels, and recommends whether each SKU can stay live in the EU.

## Required UI sections
- SKU intake and channel selector
- GPSR field completeness checklist
- Responsible Person decision card
- manufacturer and material evidence shelf
- safety warning label composer
- EU listing block / keep matrix
- seller FAQ and export handoff
