- User can identify the target user and triggering pain immediately.
- Main screen exposes all required UI sections.

- Missing or uncertain source data has a clear placeholder state.
- Risk labels do not overclaim legal, financial, medical, or regulatory certainty.
- Mobile layout keeps labels and buttons readable without overlap.
- Community/news signals are cited as inspiration, not proof.
