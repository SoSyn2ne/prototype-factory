# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Users already solve this inside QuickBooks, Stripe, or FreshBooks reminders.
2. Owners avoid escalation tools because they feel too aggressive.
3. Late fees remain uncollectable regardless of better tracking.
4. The product lacks legal nuance for contracts and jurisdictions.
5. Cash impact is not enough to drive daily usage.
6. Integrations are required before value is visible.
7. Collections-ready evidence is overkill for the target invoice size.
