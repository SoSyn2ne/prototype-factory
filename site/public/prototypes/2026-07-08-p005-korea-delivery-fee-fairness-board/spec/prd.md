# PRD - Korea Delivery Fee Fairness Board

## Problem
- A Korean small-restaurant scorecard that compares delivery app commissions, ad pressure, hidden terms, and direct-order escape paths.
- Source evidence: S8 - Reddit r/restaurant, 2026, small restaurant owners handling online ordering and delivery fees: Family-run restaurant operators describe online ordering and delivery as one-sided and profit-killing. S9 - Korea Herald, 2026, Korean restaurants trapped by delivery apps: A Korean government survey found delivery apps take an average 8.2 percent cut, nearly double what restaurant owners say they can sustain.

## Target user
- Korean restaurant owners deciding whether Baemin/Coupang Eats/Yogiyo-style channels are sustainable after commission, ad, and promotion pressure.

## Key UX
- Enter app terms, order volume, ad spend, discounts, and direct-order capacity; the app scores fairness and drafts a customer-facing direct-order nudge.

## Required UI sections
- Platform term comparison
- Commission sustainability meter
- Ad pressure ledger
- Promo loss calculator
- Direct-order readiness checklist
- Customer nudge copy
- Owner fairness score receipt

## Assigned design profile
- financial-analyst
- Design direction: rigorous margin workbench with compact Korean-market fee tables, quiet variance chips, and decision-oriented owner notes.
