# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Clinics manage rebooking well enough in their existing scheduling tools.
2. Package balance is not a strong predictor of rebooking urgency.
3. Operators need provider-specific availability embedded in the same screen.
4. Aesthetic clinics reject data-dense ops dashboards as off-brand.
5. Forecasting retained revenue feels too abstract for front-desk users.
6. The product needs before/after treatment content to be credible.
7. Outreach recommendations vary too much to standardize.
