# PRD — Parent Panic Cutline

## Problem
- A private education triage board that helps parents decide which classes to keep, pause, or replace when monthly hagwon costs are rising faster than confidence scores.
- Parents often buy more classes to reduce guilt, but the result is higher spend, higher child fatigue, and less clarity about what is actually working.

## Target user
- Parents managing multiple hagwon, tutor, worksheet, and test-prep bills who feel guilty cutting anything even when the family budget is tightening.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should understand the top action path in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: The first version should work with manual input, lightweight imports, and no deep enterprise integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning product designed for a single high-focus session.

## Non-goals
- Full back-office system replacement.
- Heavy automation that requires hidden AI decisions.
- Multi-role admin tooling beyond the core operator or end-user workflow.

## Success metrics
- Users can understand risk, upside, or emotional momentum in one screen.
- Users can identify the next action without reading a long manual.
- The interface makes the human driver of the product feel obvious rather than abstract.

## Notes
- Theme: 사교육.
- Design profile: operator-dense.
- Core UI sections: monthly tuition burn meter, subject confidence grid, child fatigue heatmap, keep-pause-replace command board, weekly schedule rebuilder, caregiver alignment memo drawer.
