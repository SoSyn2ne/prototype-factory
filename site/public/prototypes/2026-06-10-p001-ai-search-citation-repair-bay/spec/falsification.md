# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. If owners cannot understand the difference between wrong facts and missing citations, the workflow fails.
2. If the repair queue reads like generic SEO tasks, the idea is too weak.
3. If the UI cannot show before/after evidence, trust will be low.
4. If agencies are the actual buyer, the owner-facing tone may need to shift.
5. If live AI answers vary too much, the product needs confidence bands and snapshots.
6. If all actions require expert help, self-serve conversion may be poor.
7. If Stitch cannot fit comparison lanes legibly, reduce to two surfaces plus an expandable table.
