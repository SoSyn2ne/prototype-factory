# PRD — Storm Claim Deductible Desk

## Problem
- A homeowner decision console that compares insurance claim math, contractor timing, and out-of-pocket risk before storm damage turns into a bad financial move.
- Most homeowners make claim decisions under stress, using scattered contractor estimates and vague insurance fear instead of a clear side-by-side financial frame.

## Target user
- Homeowners dealing with roof, siding, or water damage in the first two weeks after a storm who need to decide whether to file a claim, pay cash, or delay work.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should reach a claim-or-cash recommendation in under 8 minutes.
- Budget: Version one should rely on manual estimate and deductible inputs instead of insurer integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first comparison workflow for focused post-storm decision making.

## Non-goals
- Filing claims directly with insurers.
- Replacing licensed legal or public adjuster advice.
- Running a contractor marketplace or bidding system.

## Success metrics
- Users can understand claim-versus-cash tradeoffs in one sitting.
- Users leave with a documented next move and evidence checklist.
- The product reduces panic-driven overfiling or costly delay.
