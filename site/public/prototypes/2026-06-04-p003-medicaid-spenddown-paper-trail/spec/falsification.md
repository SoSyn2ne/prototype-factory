# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Legal/benefits boundaries make the product too constrained to be useful.
2. State-specific variation overwhelms a simple UI.
3. Caregivers prefer direct attorney or social-worker support.
4. Sensitive document handling creates trust and compliance blockers.
5. Users mistake organized questions for eligibility advice.
6. Renewal/work-verification policy changes move faster than the product can track.
7. The main pain is human service availability, not paperwork organization.
