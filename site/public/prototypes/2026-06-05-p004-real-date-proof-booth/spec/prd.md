# PRD — Real-Date Proof Booth

## Problem
- A playful trust kit that helps singles show they are real without turning dating into another surveillance chore.

## Target user
- Dating-app users, singles-event hosts, friend groups, dating coaches, and people tired of AI photos, fake profiles, and awkward "are you a bot?" conversations.

## Key UX
- The user makes a lightweight proof-of-human card using fresh prompts, friend attestations, photo-context notes, boundaries, and event-mode checks; the app creates a shareable profile insert or party slide that signals authenticity without exposing private ID.

## Required UI sections
- Proof booth hero with "real, recent, respectful" status
- Fresh prompt camera strip for today-only micro moments
- Friend attestation collector with consent controls
- AI-photo suspicion explainer that avoids harsh scoring
- Boundary and privacy toggles
- Date-safe share card generator
- Singles-event wall mode
- Report/ban appeal note stash for unexplained platform issues

## Non-goals

- No legal, medical, financial-aid, or compliance advice beyond workflow framing.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the painful trigger in under 10 seconds.
- User can complete the main sample workflow without placeholder content.
- Stitch prompt can be copied directly into the 08:30 generation flow.
