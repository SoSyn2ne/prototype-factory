# Falsification checklist

1. The idea is too close to a recent PF batch without a new trigger, user, data source, or interaction mechanic.
2. The target user would not feel money, status, relationship, health, or opportunity pressure from the problem.
3. The required UI sections describe a generic dashboard instead of a concrete working app surface.
4. The source signals are irrelevant to the user pain or only decorative trend references.
5. The Stitch prompt omits the assigned design profile or contradicts it visually.
6. The first screen becomes a marketing landing page instead of the actual workflow.
7. The product cannot be explained in one clear before/after workflow.

## Candidate context
Design profile: financial-analyst
Theme: 자산 방어 / 역전 - micro-export margin defense, US buyer retention, and tariff uncertainty.

Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Tariff DDP Margin Splitter" for Etsy and Shopify micro-sellers shipping low-value goods into the US after de minimis changes. The first screen must be a working tariff margin workbench with a tariff exposure header, order-value simulator, margin split table, origin evidence checklist, buyer message preview, SKU triage board, and weekly lost-order tracker. Design direction: use the financial-analyst profile with calm analytical panels, restrained warning tones, precise margin math, and confidence cues that help sellers decide without panic. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.
