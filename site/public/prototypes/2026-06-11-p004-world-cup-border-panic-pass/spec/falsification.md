# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. The UI reads as an official visa checker or gives legal eligibility advice.
2. The first screen does not show go/no-go status and missing blockers.
3. The shareable pass is not visually distinctive enough for the fun/portfolio slot.
4. Serious advisory and emergency states are hidden behind playful styling.
5. The design profile is not visibly playful-experimental.
6. Source signals S7 and S8 are not referenced in the concept notes.
7. Any placeholder text remains in the spec.
