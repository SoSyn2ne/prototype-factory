# PRD — Catalyst-to-Conviction Scorecard

## Problem
Investing decisions often mix facts with narrative. The narrative is useful, but it becomes dangerous when:
- the scoring model is implicit (weights shift unconsciously)
- catalysts are not time-bounded (stale beliefs persist)
- there is no falsification plan (no clear “what would prove me wrong?”)

## Target user
- A retail investor or operator tracking 5–30 tickers in semiconductors/AI and wanting a repeatable process.

## Constraints
- Time: create a catalyst card in < 30 seconds.
- Budget: $0; local-only demo.
- Platform: browser.

## Non-goals
- Not a market data terminal.
- Not a trading system.
- Not financial advice.

## Success metrics
- Users can explain *why* a ticker’s score is high (weights + cards).
- Overdue catalysts are surfaced (forced re-check).
- “7-day sprint” produces an actionable checklist, not generic fluff.

## Notes
The point is not perfect forecasting; it’s a disciplined loop that tightens beliefs quickly.
