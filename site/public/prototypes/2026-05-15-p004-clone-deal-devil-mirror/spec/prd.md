# PRD — Clone Deal Devil Mirror

## Problem
- A playful creator contract scanner that shows what an AI-likeness clause could turn you into before you sign.
- External evidence: Signal 7 (community chatter): Creators worry brands will push too far with AI clones and reuse likenesses without clear limits.; Signal 8 (community chatter): UGC creators flag contracts that grant broad discretion over content or likeness reuse without limits.

## Target user
- UGC creators, micro-influencers, actors, models, voice creators, and creator managers reviewing brand contracts that mention likeness, synthetic media, derivative works, paid ads, whitelisting, or perpetual usage.

## Key UX
- Paste a contract clause and campaign brief; the app turns rights language into a dramatic “future clone” storyboard, flags dangerous duration/channel/AI-training terms, suggests negotiation swaps, and creates a shareable red-flag card.

## Required UI sections
- contract clause paste stage
- AI likeness rights highlighter
- future clone storyboard
- usage-duration and channel danger meter
- negotiation swap cards
- manager/legal handoff notes
- shareable red-flag poster
