# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Are fuel spikes too infrequent for users to adopt a dedicated workflow?
2. Do existing field-service suites already provide enough profit visibility for morning dispatch decisions?
3. Is margin erosion driven more by labor overruns than fuel, making the concept too narrow?
4. Are route changes too disruptive once customers are booked?
5. Would operators reject surcharge recommendations because they fear customer backlash?
6. Is the real demand for automated route optimization rather than a decision dashboard?
7. Would users need highly accurate cost accounting before they trust the recommendations?
