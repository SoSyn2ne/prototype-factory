# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Fuel cost swings matter more to route-heavy service businesses than to office-based teams.
- Dispatch decisions happen under time pressure early in the day.
- Operators will trade some schedule perfection for margin protection when costs spike.

## ASSUMPTION
- A morning margin-rescue screen is more useful than generic weekly reporting.
- Users can tolerate simple what-if scenarios without requiring a full route optimizer.
- Customer sensitivity can be represented with lightweight signals such as VIP, SLA, and cancellation risk.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether operators prefer per-vehicle, per-crew, or per-job margin framing.
- How often surcharges are commercially acceptable in local service categories.
- Which verticals feel the pain most acutely: HVAC, cleaning, plumbing, pest, or appliance repair.
