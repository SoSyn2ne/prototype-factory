# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- A written checklist reduces decision errors in high-variance domains (aviation/medicine pattern transfers). 
- Investment theses can be decomposed into assumptions about demand, competition, execution, and valuation.

## ASSUMPTION
- Investors will actually use a canvas if it feels fast (≤2 minutes) and produces an immediately useful output.
- A lightweight “fragility” heuristic is valuable even without live market data, as long as it is transparent.
- The best immediate value is not prediction; it’s **forcing falsifiable statements**.

## UNKNOWN
- Which input format creates the most compliance: free-form text vs structured prompts.
- Whether users want assumption templates by sector (semis vs SaaS) or prefer generic categories.
- Whether a single score motivates better behavior or encourages gaming.
- What the default falsification tasks should be per category (demand vs execution vs valuation) without becoming generic fluff.
