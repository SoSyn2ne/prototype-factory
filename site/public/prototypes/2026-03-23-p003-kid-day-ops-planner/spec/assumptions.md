# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Core concept: A family ops board that turns school, care, meals, and pickup logistics into one low-stress daily plan.
- Primary user: Busy parents coordinating a child's weekday schedule across home, school, and caregivers.
- Desired interaction style: See the day timeline, owner handoffs, and risk points immediately so the family can avoid missed pickups and last-minute chaos.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer one operational overview before drilling into details.
- The first version can rely on mocked or manually entered data.
- The highest-value output is a clear next action, not a dense settings flow.

## UNKNOWN
- Which field or panel users trust first in real usage.
- Which export/share format matters most after validation.
- How much historical detail is necessary on the first screen.
