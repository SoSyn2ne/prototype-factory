# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Bay utilization and technician overtime heavily affect shop profitability.
- Parts readiness is a frequent hidden blocker in service flow.
- Service advisors need schedule decisions tied to margin, not just calendar occupancy.

## ASSUMPTION
- A board-plus-detail workflow is the clearest mental model for bay rebalancing.
- Recovered-profit framing will resonate more than pure utilization metrics.
- Operators can act on schedule suggestions without seeing every underlying work order field.

## UNKNOWN
- Which job types are most sensitive to daypart placement.
- How managers weigh technician specialization versus pure margin recovery.
- Whether shops prefer hourly or half-day planning granularity.
