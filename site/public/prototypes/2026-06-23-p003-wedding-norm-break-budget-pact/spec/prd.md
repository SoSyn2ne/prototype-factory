# PRD - Wedding Norm Break Budget Pact

## Problem
Couples convert family pressure, guest travel costs, and production creep into a shared low-drama wedding budget pact.

## Target user
Gen Z couples, Korea-based couples, and families trying to plan smaller weddings without triggering social or parent conflict.

## Key UX
Couple ranks what matters, enters family asks, guest travel burden, DIY labor, and venue quotes; the app creates a norm-breaking budget pact and conversation scripts.

## Required UI sections
- Wedding pressure header showing comfort budget, family asks, and guest burden
- Tradition breaker cards for venue, meal, dress, photos, gifts, and afterparty
- Guest travel and cash strain map
- DIY labor reality calculator
- Family conversation script composer
- Budget pact split view for couple, parents, and guests
- Shareable ceremony values card

## Assigned design profile
calm-consumer

## Theme
자산 방어 / 외로움 / 역전 - resisting wedding production pressure while preserving relationships.

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Wedding Norm Break Budget Pact" for couples who want a meaningful wedding without expensive production pressure. The first screen must be a working budget-pact workspace with a wedding pressure header, tradition breaker cards, guest travel and cash strain map, DIY labor reality calculator, family conversation script composer, couple-parent budget pact split view, and shareable ceremony values card. Use realistic sample data: 70 guests, Korea evening reception idea, family request for a standard hall, $18,000 comfort budget, $33,000 comparable venue quote, two guests flying in, and one DIY-heavy plan. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with reassuring spacing, warm but not beige-heavy surfaces, plain-language scripts, and gentle accountability states for family tradeoffs. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: no custom demo implementation.
- Stitch output must preserve the assigned design profile.
- First screen must be the working app, not a marketing landing page.
