# PRD — Paperwork Contraption Floor

## Problem and target user
Automation logic feels abstract; streamers, coworkers, and students need a tactile, funny way to understand it.

## Arrival, business route, and core loop
Open a one-line challenge, drag office machines onto a grid, start the line, inspect a jam, remix, and share the replay. Validate through clip sharing and session return.

## First screen and key screens
Messy desk, blank floor, parts tray, and Start lever. Screens: challenge, floor, parts, throughput, jam camera, replay, rematch.

## Interaction and output
Paper physics visibly follows placed parts; failures stay funny and legible. Output is an eight-second jam or perfect-run clip.

## Design profile, theme, and sources
- Profile: `playful-experimental`
- Theme: 재미
- Sources: S7 and Sheet Job viral scan

## Constraints and non-goals
Mock physics only; no real workflow automation or employee-productivity scoring.

## Success and QA
Rule comprehension under 15 seconds, first action under 3 seconds, and retry under 10 seconds; six named result states render.
