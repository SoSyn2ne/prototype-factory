# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S03 records shoppers leaving unpriced craft stalls to avoid embarrassment.
- Sellers already use paper labels, bundle signs, and improvised price changes.
- Assigned design profile: premium-editorial.
- Theme: 예산 불안과 망신 회피를 매출 기회로 바꾸는 정중한 가격 공개.

## ASSUMPTION
- Anonymous price taps convert more silent walkaways than asking the maker.
- Sellers value bulk updates enough to rent or buy reusable tags.
- A refined presentation preserves handmade-brand value better than retail shelf-label styling.

## UNKNOWN
- Acceptable tag cost and battery/update constraints.
- Whether tap events correlate reliably with purchase.
- How much network loss occurs in real event halls.
