# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users still fail to define a stop/invalidations even with the UI (they skip inputs)?
2. Do users misunderstand “risk %” as “position %” (confusion persists)?
3. When given the same scenario, do 2 users produce wildly different sizing outputs due to subjective stop distance? (tool doesn’t reduce variance)
4. Do warnings get ignored so often that they don’t change behavior?
5. Does the calculator encourage overconfidence (“math says it’s safe”) and lead to riskier behavior?
6. Is the volatility preset seen as patronizing or useless (“I don’t know my vol anyway”)?
7. In a week of use, can a user point to at least one trade where they sized smaller/cleaner because of this?
