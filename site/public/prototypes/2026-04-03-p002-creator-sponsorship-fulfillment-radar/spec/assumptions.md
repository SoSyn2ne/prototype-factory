# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Sponsorship operations fail through missed handoffs more often than missing raw demand.
- Operators need a short actionable queue rather than a generic campaign overview.
- Desktop-first review fits agency and account-management workflows.

## ASSUMPTION
- Deliverable, approval, and payout states can be summarized into a trusted risk score.
- Teams will value payout exposure enough to change prioritization behavior.
- A structured unblock panel is more useful than exposing every message thread.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users think by creator, campaign, or brand first.
- Which delay signal matters most: approvals, assets, or posting slots.
- How much historical campaign context is needed before the interface feels credible.
