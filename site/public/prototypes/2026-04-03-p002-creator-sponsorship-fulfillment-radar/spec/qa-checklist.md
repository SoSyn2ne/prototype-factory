# QA checklist

## Happy path
- User lands on the radar and sees campaigns ranked by fulfillment risk and payout exposure.
- User opens one sponsorship and reviews deliverable status, approval sequence, and the next unblock action in one screen.
- User understands what to do next to protect posting windows and revenue timing.

## Edge cases
- Campaigns with complete assets but stalled brand approvals.
- Partnerships with many deliverables but low payout exposure.
- Urgent campaigns that depend on external posting windows the team cannot move.

## Acceptance verification
- Spec is clearly about sponsorship fulfillment risk rather than generic influencer CRM.
- All required UI sections are explicitly documented.
- Design direction is present and consistent with the Stitch prompt.
