# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do agencies already manage this well enough in project-management tools?
2. Is the real bottleneck waiting on creators or brands rather than operational visibility?
3. Are campaign risks too custom to summarize into a common board?
4. Would users prefer a simple calendar view over a multi-panel risk workspace?
5. Does the product lose value without direct integrations to email, chat, and creator platforms?
6. Is payout exposure interesting but not actionable enough to change behavior?
7. Would account managers rather work from client-specific dashboards than a shared operations console?
