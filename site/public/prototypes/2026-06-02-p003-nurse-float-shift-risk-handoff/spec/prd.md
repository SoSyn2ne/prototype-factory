# PRD — Nurse Float Shift Risk Handoff

## Problem
- Charge nurses feel staffing risk before it becomes an incident, but shift-level risk is often scattered across patient acuity, float familiarity, sitter gaps, violence flags, break coverage, and support-role cuts. They need a fast pre-shift handoff artifact, not another administrative staffing dashboard.

## Target user
- Charge nurses, float pool coordinators, unit managers, and hospital staffing leads handling mixed RN, LPN, CNA, sitter, and agency coverage.

## One-liner
- A field-ready handoff tool that helps charge nurses expose unsafe float coverage before the shift breaks.

## Key UX
- Build a shift risk map from patient acuity, floated staff familiarity, violence flags, med complexity, and missing support roles, then generate a pre-shift escalation note.

## Required UI sections
- Shift command header
- Patient acuity board
- Float staff familiarity matrix
- Missing support role flags
- Violence and sitter risk lane
- Break coverage timeline
- Escalation note composer
- Post-shift incident recap

## Design profile
- field-ops
- Design direction: durable shift-board styling, strong alert hierarchy, mobile-friendly controls, badge-like role markers, and a practical tone for frontline operators.

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Nurse Float Shift Risk Handoff" for charge nurses, float pool coordinators, and unit managers. The app should help a charge nurse map patient acuity, floated staff familiarity, violence or sitter risk, missing support roles, med complexity, and break coverage before the shift starts, then generate a concise escalation note for the supervisor. Required sections: shift command header, patient acuity board, float staff familiarity matrix, missing support role flags, violence and sitter risk lane, break coverage timeline, escalation note composer, and post-shift incident recap. Use realistic hospital shift sample data while avoiding diagnosis or clinical decision support claims. Design direction: use the `field-ops` profile with durable shift-board styling, strong alert hierarchy, mobile-friendly controls, badge-like role markers, and a practical tone for frontline operators under pressure.

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: Spec/scaffold only until Stitch export is provided.
- Platform: Responsive web prototype generated by Stitch later.

## Non-goals
- Do not provide clinical diagnosis, medical advice, or automated staffing decisions.
- Do not replace hospital staffing systems.
- Do not blame individual nurses; focus on shift risk visibility.

## Success metrics
- A charge nurse can see the top shift risks and compose an escalation note from one workflow.
- The design feels like a frontline handoff board, not a generic executive analytics page.
- Metadata and specs include signal references, design profile, theme, and full prompt.
