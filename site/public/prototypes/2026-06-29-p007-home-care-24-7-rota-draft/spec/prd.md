# PRD - Home Care 24-7 Rota Draft

## Problem
- Families hiring round-the-clock elder caregivers test agency coverage, turnover gaps, tasks, and backup shifts before burnout wins.

## Target user
- Adult children coordinating in-home care for parents with dementia or mobility decline, especially when comparing agency and independent caregiver teams.

## Key UX
- Family builds a weekly rota, assigns tasks, exposes uncovered hours, compares agency vs independent coverage, and creates a turnover/back-up plan.

## Required UI sections
- Care coverage header with 24/7 target, open hours, budget, and proximity to family
- Weekly caregiver rota with day/night shifts and handoff notes
- Task matrix for meds, meals, toileting, mobility, companionship, and house safety
- Agency vs independent cost and reliability comparison
- Turnover risk board with backup names and onboarding notes
- Family escalation ladder for missed shifts or health changes
- Print-friendly house guide for the refrigerator or caregiver binder

## Assigned design profile
- field-ops

## Theme
- 건강 / 외로움 - elder-care continuity, family burnout, and home safety.

## Source signal references
- S11

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Home Care 24-7 Rota Draft" for families hiring round-the-clock elder caregivers to test agency coverage, turnover gaps, tasks, and backup shifts before burnout wins. The first screen must be a working care operations console with a 24/7 coverage header, weekly caregiver rota, task matrix, agency-vs-independent comparison, turnover risk board, family escalation ladder, and print-friendly house guide. Use realistic sample data: parent with dementia, daughter 9 minutes away, three agency aides, two independent caregivers, uncovered Sunday 2am-6am shift, medication reminders, fall-risk note, and monthly budget ceiling. Design direction: use the field-ops profile with reliable handoff rows, large status blocks, calm steel neutrals, and one safety-orange accent for uncovered care gaps. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: do not implement a custom demo.
- First screen in Stitch must be the working app, not a marketing landing page.
- Use external sources as problem evidence only, not market-size proof.

## Non-goals
- No external API integration in the prototype.
- No legal, medical, financial, or regulatory advice claims beyond workflow support.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the pain in under 10 seconds.
- User can identify the next action from the first screen.
- The prototype includes every required UI section from this PRD.
