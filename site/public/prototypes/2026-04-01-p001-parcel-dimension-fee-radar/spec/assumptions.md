# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Small operators feel shipping margin pain from parcel fees and DIM logic.
- Desktop-first workflow is appropriate for fulfillment review.
- The core decision is usually packaging swap, carrier swap, split shipment, or accept fee.

## ASSUMPTION
- Teams can access measured dimensions before label purchase.
- Operators prefer a single review queue instead of carrier-by-carrier admin views.
- A risk score plus save estimate is enough to drive action without deep analytics.

## UNKNOWN
- Which fee types create the highest willingness to pay: DIM, oversize, address correction, or audit variance.
- How often packaging changes are operationally feasible same-day.
- Whether users want warehouse-station mode or manager-review mode first.
