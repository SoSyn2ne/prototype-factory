# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do operators already catch most risky cartons inside their WMS before labels are purchased?
2. Is measured-dimension data too inconsistent to make a trusted pre-label score?
3. Are packaging changes operationally too disruptive to use in real fulfillment windows?
4. Do carrier fee rules vary too much for a single normalized comparison view to stay simple?
5. Would finance teams value post-invoice leakage reporting more than pre-label intervention?
6. Is the real bottleneck carton catalog quality rather than decision UX?
7. Would users only buy this if it automates label purchase, not just recommends actions?
