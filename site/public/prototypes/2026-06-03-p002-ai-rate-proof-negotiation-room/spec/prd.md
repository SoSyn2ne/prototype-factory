- Source signals: S4, S5, S6 from daily/2026-06-03/ideas.md.

Create a responsive web app prototype called "AI Rate Proof Negotiation Room" for freelancers and small agencies pricing AI-assisted work. Required sections: project value intake, AI task disclosure ladder, human judgment proof board, scope creep boundary table, rate option builder, client objection simulator, proposal memo preview, and outcome and revision risk ledger.
