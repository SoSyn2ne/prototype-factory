# PRD - Age-Seven English Spend Court

## Problem
Korean parents compare early English hagwon pressure, monthly cost, commute burden, and child readiness before joining the arms race.

## Target user
Korean parents of kindergarten and lower-elementary children deciding whether to start English private education early.

## Key UX
Parent enters child readiness, local hagwon quotes, peer pressure, commute time, trial class notes, and family budget; the app stages a tradeoff court with keep, delay, switch, or cap decisions.

## Required UI sections
- Pressure docket header with age, monthly cost, and peer comparison
- Child readiness and stress signal checklist
- Hagwon quote and fee-disclosure comparison
- Commute and family schedule burden calculator
- Parent anxiety vs child benefit evidence cards
- Decision court with keep/delay/switch/cap arguments
- Grandparent or spouse conversation script

## Assigned design profile
financial-analyst

## Theme
사교육 / 자산 방어 - resisting early private-education escalation with evidence and budget discipline.

## Source signal references
- S12 - Korea Herald, early English education debate highlights heavy private-education spending from kindergarten/age seven and parent anxiety: https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10728315

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Age-Seven English Spend Court" for Korean parents deciding whether early English hagwon spending is justified. The first screen must be a working education-spend court with a pressure docket header, child readiness checklist, hagwon quote comparison, commute burden calculator, parent anxiety vs child benefit cards, keep/delay/switch/cap decision court, and family conversation script. Use realistic sample data: age 7 child, two hagwon quotes at 360,000 KRW and 520,000 KRW per month, 28-minute commute, friend group pressure, trial class note, family education budget cap, and one lower-cost public option. Design direction: use the financial-analyst profile with calm comparison tables, restrained warning tones, confidence labels, and interpretation notes that keep the parent from overreacting to peer pressure. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: no custom demo implementation.
- Stitch output must preserve the assigned design profile.
- First screen must be the working app, not a marketing landing page.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the core decision or field workflow within 10 seconds.
- Every required UI section appears in the generated Stitch output.
- The cited source signals are visible as problem evidence in the product framing.
