1. First-time target users cannot explain the action after seeing: one rejected subway gate, three empty pockets, and the traveler's actual next three stops.
2. The observed problem does not recur outside source references S02.
3. The existing workaround is faster or safer than the proposed mechanic.
4. Users do not save, show, or act on the output artifact: bilingual offline three-pocket payment card tied to three planned stops.
5. The calm-consumer profile obscures the trigger or recovery state.
6. The first validation test fails to improve the chosen behavior or completion time.
7. The concept collapses into a generic dashboard, planner, proof packet, or content page when the mechanic is removed.
