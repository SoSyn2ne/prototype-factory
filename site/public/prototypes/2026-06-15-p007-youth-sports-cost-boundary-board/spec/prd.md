# PRD - Youth Sports Cost Boundary Board

## Slot
- p007

## Label
- Practical / Commercial

## Problem
- A parent decision board that separates a child’s real sports need from travel-team status pressure, hidden fees, and guilt spending.

## Target user
- Parents of kids in travel sports, club teams, competitive dance/cheer, hockey, soccer, volleyball, and baseball.

## Key UX
- Enter tryout fees, monthly dues, tournaments, uniforms, hotels, parent admission, child motivation, and family budget; compare rec/club/travel paths and produce a boundary script.

## Required UI sections
- Season cost shock header
- Full-fee intake for dues, gear, hotels, travel, and parent admission
- Child motivation and burnout check
- Rec vs club vs travel comparison
- Tournament calendar with hotel and PTO burden
- Family budget boundary slider
- Coach/parent group message scripts
- Decision receipt for keep, cap, pause, or switch

## Assigned design profile
- calm-consumer

## Theme
- 사교육

## Source signal references
- S13

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Youth Sports Cost Boundary Board" for parents deciding whether travel sports, club teams, competitive dance, cheer, hockey, soccer, volleyball, or baseball are worth the cost. The app should let a parent enter tryout fees, monthly dues, tournaments, uniforms, hotels, parent admission, child motivation, and family budget, then compare rec, club, and travel paths and produce a boundary script. Required sections: season cost shock header, full-fee intake for dues/gear/hotels/travel/parent admission, child motivation and burnout check, rec vs club vs travel comparison, tournament calendar with hotel and PTO burden, family budget boundary slider, coach/parent group message scripts, and decision receipt for keep/cap/pause/switch. Use realistic sample data with $300/month dues, $4,500 season fees, 10 away tournaments, uniforms, and a 9-year-old who still likes the sport. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with warm neutral surfaces, shame-free family-budget cards, gentle comparison states, and clear next actions that help parents resist status pressure without crushing the child. Do not make a landing page; the first screen must be the working parent decision board.
