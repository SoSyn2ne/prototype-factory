# PRD — Refund Human Trail

## Problem
A consumer proof room for automated airline or retailer refund failures where broken forms, vanished support, and scam numbers create money-loss risk.

## Target user
Travelers and shoppers fighting automated refund systems, plus consumer advocates who help them file complaints.

## Key UX
The user drops in emails, screenshots, failed form captures, call attempts, and charge details; the app builds a timeline, complaint packet, and safe human contact checklist.

## Required UI sections
- Refund case header with deadline, amount, merchant, and risk level.
- Timeline builder for cancellation notice, failed form, emails, chats, calls, and complaint IDs.
- Evidence upload grid grouped by proof type.
- Scam-risk checker for phone numbers, search results, and suspicious payment asks.
- Complaint packet generator for card dispute, regulator, merchant, or travel insurer.
- Human-contact map with verified channels and escalation attempts.
- Final checklist before filing or sharing the packet.

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Refund Human Trail" for consumers documenting automated refund failures. The first screen should be a case workspace, not marketing. Include refund case header, deadline and amount risk, timeline builder, evidence upload grid, scam-risk checker, complaint packet generator, verified human-contact map, and final filing checklist. Use realistic sample data from a canceled flight/refund form failure with emails, failed pages, and suspected fake support numbers. Design direction: use the financial-analyst profile with rigorous case evidence, restrained charts, confidence badges, ink/off-white surfaces, and calm warning tones.
