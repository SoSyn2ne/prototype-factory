# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. The first screen reads like a generic dashboard instead of a decision receipt.
2. The prototype does not visibly produce Camp decision receipt with refund deadline and questions to ask.
3. The required UI sections are missing or reduced below the Phase A spec.
4. The design does not match the calm-consumer profile.
5. The idea cannot cite its source signals: S17, S18, S19.
6. The core trigger is not obvious in 3 seconds: The brochure says AI genius; your calendar says $2,400 and no refund after Friday.
7. The flow asks for vague data instead of concrete inputs from the source-signal workaround.
