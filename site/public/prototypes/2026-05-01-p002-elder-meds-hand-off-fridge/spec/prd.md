# PRD — Elder Meds Hand-Off Fridge

## Problem
- A family caregiver medication hand-off sheet that turns missed-dose anxiety, sibling coordination, and printout needs into a shared daily proof board.

## Target user
- Adult children, spouses, and sibling groups coordinating medication support for aging parents or chronically ill family members who still need a printable fallback.

## Key UX
- Build a medication schedule once; mark taken/skipped/unknown doses; surface missed-dose alerts; generate a refrigerator-print daily sheet; summarize proof for siblings, home aides, or doctors without turning the family chat into chaos.

## Required UI sections
- today dose proof board
- missed-dose alert rail
- medication schedule builder
- sibling/caregiver responsibility row
- printable fridge sheet preview
- doctor visit summary card
- calm family hand-off notes

## Design profile
- calm-consumer

## Stitch prompt
Design a responsive family caregiving product called Elder Meds Hand-Off Fridge for adult children and spouses coordinating medications for an aging parent. It should let a caregiver build a medication schedule, mark each dose as taken, skipped, or unknown, notify the family when a dose is missed, and create a printable refrigerator sheet plus a doctor-visit summary. Include a today dose proof board, missed-dose alert rail, medication schedule builder, sibling/caregiver responsibility row, printable fridge sheet preview, doctor visit summary card, and calm family hand-off notes. Design direction: use a calm-consumer aesthetic with warm neutrals, large legible type, reassuring check states, gentle alerts, and print-friendly cards so the experience feels protective, low-blame, and usable by stressed families.
