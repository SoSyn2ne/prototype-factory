# PRD — Blind Box Sound Kitchen

## Problem
Mix three mystery sounds into a creature friends must identify.

## Target user
Friend groups, classrooms, streamers, and short-form creators.

## Core loop / key UX
Open three capsules, turn pitch/rhythm/texture knobs, hatch a reactive creature, and share a guessing clip.

## First screen
Make the core action and its evidence immediately understandable in three seconds.

## Required UI sections
sound capsules; three-knob mixer; creature hatch; performance; guesses; reveal; rematch.

## Design
- Assigned profile: `playful-experimental`
- Theme: 소리 놀이

## Source signals
S11, S10 from `daily/2026-09-02/ideas.md`.

## Non-goals
- No production integrations or custom demo in Phase A.

## QA / screenshot criteria
Works with prerecorded audio without microphone permission; mute and reduced-motion states are complete.
