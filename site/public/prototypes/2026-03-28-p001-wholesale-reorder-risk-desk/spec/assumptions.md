# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Reorder timing and account prioritization are the core value story, not generic CRM logging.
- Users need buyer-level risk context before deciding whether to call, discount, or wait.
- A single desktop screen should compress account health and recovery actions into one flow.

## ASSUMPTION
- Teams already know their normal reorder cadence by account or product category.
- Most intervention work happens from desktop during the morning sales review.
- Suggested recovery plays can stay lightweight without full ERP integration.

## UNKNOWN
- How much SKU-level detail belongs on the first screen versus a drawer.
- Whether reps trust a risk score without seeing the underlying cadence model.
- Which rescue move actually wins best across discount, sample, and call outreach.
