# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Source signals: S4, S5.
- Assigned design profile: operator-dense.
- Target user: Korean small restaurants, omakase counters, cafes with large orders, bakeries, and salon-like reservation businesses that still rely on phone or DM bookings.

## ASSUMPTION
- The cited signals reflect repeated pain strong enough for a narrow prototype exploration.
- Users will value a proof, decision, or communication packet more than a generic dashboard.
- The strongest first screen is an operational workspace with realistic sample data.

## UNKNOWN
- Actual willingness to pay.
- Whether users trust generated scripts or packets in high-stakes situations.
- Which integrations matter most after prototype validation.
