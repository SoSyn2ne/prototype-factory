# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users already have enough tools and only lack motivation?
2. Is the real blocker weak networks rather than visible planning?
3. Will a dense workflow feel punishing during a vulnerable moment?
4. Does the concept break if users cannot estimate role-fit confidence honestly?
5. Will users abandon after one session because the effort feels too manual?
6. Does income urgency push people toward gig work outside the tool's frame?
