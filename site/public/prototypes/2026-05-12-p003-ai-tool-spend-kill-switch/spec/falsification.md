# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Target users say the trigger is not urgent enough to open a tool.
2. Users already solve the pain with one existing system and no meaningful friction.
3. The workflow requires expert judgment that cannot be safely simplified.
4. The prototype looks like a renamed generic dashboard instead of the specific trigger: AI Tool Spend Kill Switch.
5. Users would share the artifact but not pay, and no B2B buyer emerges.
6. Required inputs are too hard to collect during the moment of pain.
7. The design profile `financial-analyst` makes the experience less clear rather than more credible.
