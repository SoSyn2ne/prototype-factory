## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop creator operating board called Creator Routine Moat for newsletter writers, small YouTubers, expert creators, and brand-community operators who want to stop panicking over algorithm changes and build a weekly owned-audience ritual. It should map owned channels, help choose one recurring audience ritual, schedule cross-platform proof points, and package sponsor/community confidence around routine instead of raw virality. Include an audience ownership map, weekly ritual builder, algorithm-risk weather strip, proof-of-routine calendar, sponsor confidence snapshot, and community moment recap drawer. Design direction: use a premium-editorial aesthetic with refined magazine-like hierarchy, warm ivory and ink tones, elegant calendar cards, tasteful creator-brand accents, and polished narrative modules so the product feels aspirational, credible, and commercially ready.
