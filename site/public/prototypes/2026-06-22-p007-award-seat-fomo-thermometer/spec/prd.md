# PRD — Award Seat FOMO Thermometer

## Problem
- Points travelers already juggle award-seat tools, cash fares, family date pressure, and airline reliability, but the decision often becomes group-chat anxiety rather than a clear book-now/wait verdict.

## Target user
- Points travelers planning fixed-date family trips, friend trips, or event travel while comparing paid award tools and cash fares.

## Constraints
- Time: Must help while an award seat may disappear.
- Budget: Fun validation prototype, not a full award-search engine.
- Platform: Responsive Stitch web prototype with synthetic flight and points data.

## Non-goals
- Do not scrape award inventory or book travel.
- Do not guarantee redemption value.

## Success metrics
- User can turn points/cash/date pressure into a shareable book-now/wait card.
- First screen feels playful but still decision-useful.
