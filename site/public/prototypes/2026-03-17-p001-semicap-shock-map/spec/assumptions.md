# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Semiconductor supply chains are multi-tiered; shocks often propagate (tools ↔ fabs ↔ chip designers).
- Export restrictions can impact both supply (equipment availability) and demand (addressable markets).

## ASSUMPTION
- A small set of transparent factors (exposure to equipment cycle, fab concentration, AI demand sensitivity) is enough to create a *useful* first-pass ranking.
- For a daily workflow, “directionally reasonable” is more valuable than “precisely correct.”
- Users accept that the model does **not** use live data and is scenario-based.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users prefer a single composite score vs. a multi-factor explanation view.
- Whether different user segments (traders vs. long-term) need separate presets.
- Which shocks are most common in the user’s routine (capacity vs. export vs. demand).
