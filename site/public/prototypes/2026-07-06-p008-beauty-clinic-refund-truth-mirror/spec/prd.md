# PRD — Beauty Clinic Refund Truth Mirror

## Problem
- A K-beauty traveler quote checker that exposes refund, deposit, tax, and fine-print surprises before payment.
- Source evidence: S13 - Reddit r/KoreaSeoulBeauty: tax refund promo warnings and deposit pressure: Travelers warn each other that refund, points-back, and store-credit promotions often hide catches in fine print.

## Target user
- International K-beauty and cosmetic-treatment travelers comparing Korean clinic promos, deposits, and refund claims.

## Key UX
- Paste a promo or quote; the mirror highlights refund caveats, deposit risk, final-price gaps, and questions to ask before paying.

## Required UI sections
- Promo paste area
- Quote breakdown
- Refund caveat highlighter
- Deposit risk meter
- Clinic question list
- Before-you-pay receipt

## Design profile
- premium-editorial
- Design direction must be tailored inside the Stitch prompt and avoid generic dashboard styling.

## Theme
- 외모 / 자산 방어

## Source signal references
- S13: Reddit r/KoreaSeoulBeauty: tax refund promo warnings and deposit pressure — https://www.reddit.com/r/KoreaSeoulBeauty/comments/1qq3ymy/psa_be_careful_with_10_back_or_tax_refund_promos/

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web prototype for "Beauty Clinic Refund Truth Mirror". Target user: international K-beauty travelers comparing clinic promos, deposits, tax refund claims, points-back offers, and final quotes before paying. Core UX: the user pastes a promo or quote; the product highlights refund caveats, deposit risk, final-price gaps, and a before-you-pay question list. Required sections: premium quote intake, treatment price breakdown, refund caveat highlighter, deposit risk meter, clinic question checklist, and polished before-you-pay receipt. Use the premium-editorial design profile: refined high-trust styling, elegant but scannable typography, warm charcoal/stone tones, one quiet CTA color, and restrained premium contrast.

## Success metrics
- A Stitch operator can paste the prompt without rewriting it.
- The first screen shows the actual workflow, not a landing page.
- The output artifact is visible before the fold or immediately after the primary input.
