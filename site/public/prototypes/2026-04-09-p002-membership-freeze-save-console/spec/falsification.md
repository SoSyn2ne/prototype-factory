# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Are freezes and cancellations driven mostly by seasonal patterns that operators already understand without software?
2. Is available gym data too incomplete or too delayed to create a trustworthy daily risk queue?
3. Would a simple spreadsheet of absent members be sufficient for most boutique gyms?
4. Do staff lack time to run interventions even if the queue is accurate?
5. Are save actions too dependent on owner intuition to productize credibly?
6. Does billing friction matter less than community and schedule fit, making this dashboard over-optimized for ops signals?
7. Is churn volume too low in the target segment to justify a dedicated tool?
