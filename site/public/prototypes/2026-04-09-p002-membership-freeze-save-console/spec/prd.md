# PRD — Membership Freeze Save Console

## Problem
- Boutique gyms often notice churn too late, after attendance has dropped and members have mentally disengaged.
- Staff have the raw signals, but not a single place that ranks which member needs which save action today.

## Target user
- Boutique gym owners, retention managers, and coach-led member success teams.

## Constraints
- Time: Front-desk and retention staff need a useful intervention list before the morning rush.
- Budget: Phase one should work with lightweight attendance, billing, and notes data rather than complex CRM integration.
- Platform: Desktop-first internal console used by operators, coaches, and managers.

## Non-goals
- Building a full CRM for gyms.
- Auto-sending messages without human approval.
- Long-term workout programming or wearable analytics.

## Success metrics
- Staff can identify the top freeze-risk members and recommended save actions in under 5 minutes.
- The dashboard makes recovered monthly revenue visible after each intervention wave.
- Coach context is visible enough that outreach feels personal instead of generic.

## Notes
- Design profile: operator-dense.
- Core UI sections: retention KPI hero, at-risk member queue, attendance-payment-package signal table, action recommendation panel, coach notes drawer, recovered revenue rail.
