# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S07 describes flat-fee lawyers avoiding time entry and relying on Excel, Trello, or month-end cash.
- Calendar, email, call, and document metadata can provide activity clues without a running timer.
- Assigned design profile: financial-analyst.
- Theme: 월말 후회 전에 보이지 않던 노동을 가격 언어로 바꾸기.

## ASSUMPTION
- Passive activity beads are accurate enough to prompt—not decide—a scope conversation.
- Small firms will review uncertain activity if the source is visible.
- A client-safe card is more useful than another internal profitability dashboard.

## UNKNOWN
- Ethical and privacy limits across jurisdictions.
- Acceptable false-positive rate for activity grouping.
- Which source connectors firms will authorize first.
