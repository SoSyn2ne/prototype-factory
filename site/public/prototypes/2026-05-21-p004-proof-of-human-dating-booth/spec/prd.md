# PRD - Proof-of-Human Dating Booth

## One-liner
A playful profile lab that helps singles prove their photos are real enough without looking staged, fake, or desperate.

## Category
Fun / Experimental

## Target user
Dating-app users anxious about AI-photo backlash, catfish suspicion, and the embarrassment of being called fake.

## Problem
- The user has a timely, stressful workflow exposed by the referenced external/community signals.
- Existing tools are either too generic, too fragmented, or not shaped around the immediate decision moment.

## Key UX
Users assemble a profile photo set and receive a human-proof mix score: real-life evidence, recentness, social context, over-edit risk, and prompts for one casual proof shot.

## Required UI sections
- Photo lineup with authenticity tags
- AI/over-edit suspicion meter
- Proof mix score
- Casual proof-shot prompt generator
- Profile caption tone check
- Shareable verified human vibe card

## Design profile
playful-experimental

## Theme
외모

## Signal references
- Reddit r/digitalminimalism: fatigue with AI generated content and bot comments
- Dating Industry Insights: singles see AI photos as a dating red flag
- Reddit r/DatingOverSixty: suspicion of AI generated dating profiles

## Stitch prompt
Create a playful responsive web app called "Proof-of-Human Dating Booth" for dating-app users who want attractive photos without triggering AI-photo or catfish suspicion. The app displays a photo lineup, authenticity tags, over-edit suspicion meter, proof mix score, casual proof-shot prompt generator, caption tone check, and a shareable "verified human vibe" card. Required screens/sections: bold booth-style header, photo strip, scoring panel, prompt wheel, before/after profile mix, and share card. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with expressive color accents, tactile booth controls, witty microcopy, and polished portfolio energy while keeping text legible and the core scoring UI usable.
