# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- The triggering moment is usually a visible deadline such as an interview, ceremony, date, or photo day.
- Users care about looking more expensive, rested, or put together, not about tracking every beauty metric forever.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users will trust a simple manual-input planner if it produces an obvious prep sequence quickly.
- The biggest value comes from prioritization, not from recommending specific brands.

## UNKNOWN
- How much users would pay for one-off event planning versus an ongoing beauty membership.
- Whether users want solo planning only or also vendor booking integrations.
