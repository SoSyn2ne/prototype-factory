# PRD - Mom-and-Pop Rent Squeeze Court

## Problem
- A small-landlord tradeoff court that compares insurance spikes, rent caps, repairs, and vacancy risk before a renewal decision becomes a fight.
- Source evidence: S14 - Reddit r/realestateinvesting landlord insurance renewal increase thread: Small landlords report steep insurance renewal jumps and compare multi-year premium increases.

## Target user
- Small landlords with one to four units who are squeezed by insurance renewals, rent caps, repairs, and tenant relationship risk.

## Key UX
- Enter premium changes, local rent limits, repair reserve, vacancy estimate, and tenant history; the app stages a tradeoff court with evidence, options, and a respectful renewal explanation.

## Required UI sections
- Cost evidence docket
- Rent-cap rule panel
- Repair reserve calculator
- Vacancy risk witness
- Tenant impact note
- Renewal options bench
- Exportable explanation letter

## Assigned design profile
- field-ops
- Design direction: field-ops clarity, sturdy forms, practical green accents, and printable evidence blocks.
