# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Insurers reject packets regardless of evidence quality.
2. Clinic EHR workflows already capture all required proof.
3. Patients are unwilling to maintain longitudinal evidence.
4. Safety disclaimers make the bridge-plan lane too limited.
5. Rules vary too much by payer to generalize.
6. Clinics prefer outsourced PA services.
7. The product looks too similar to a generic prior-auth builder.
