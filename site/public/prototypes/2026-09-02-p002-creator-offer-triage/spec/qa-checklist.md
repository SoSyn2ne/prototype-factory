# QA checklist — Creator Offer Triage

## Happy path
- Complete: Decompose the offer into sender, brand, link/file, rights, and payment lanes with gated next actions.
- Verify all sections: offer inbox; claim lanes; sender/domain evidence; quarantine; rights/payment terms; action gate; receipt.

## Edge cases
- Show empty, incomplete, error, conflict, and success states with realistic data.
- Preserve the `operator-dense` direction and 안전 theme.

## Acceptance verification
- Desktop and mobile show independent evidence per claim and never collapse safety into one score.
- Cite S3, S8; contain no placeholder copy; create no custom demo in Phase A.
