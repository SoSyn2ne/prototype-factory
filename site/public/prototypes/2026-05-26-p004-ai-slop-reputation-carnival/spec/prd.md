# PRD - AI Slop Reputation Carnival

## Problem
A playful portfolio piece where creators test whether their video/channel looks human-made, lazy, or unfairly suspicious.

## Target user
Creators, editors, students, and portfolio reviewers who want a shareable way to discuss AI-slop perception without turning it into a dry compliance checklist.

## Key UX
A carnival-style booth scores a channel concept across human-effort signals, slop smells, disclosure clarity, and audience trust, then produces a funny share card plus serious fixes.

## Required UI sections
- Ticket-booth input for channel niche, workflow, assets, voice, editing, and disclosure
- Reputation midway with meters for human effort, repetition, originality, and viewer suspicion
- “Jury of viewers” reaction cards with skeptical, supportive, and confused audience personas
- Fix-it prize wall with concrete improvements ranked by impact
- Before/after poster generator for shareable social cards
- Tiny serious footer explaining that the score is a perception exercise, not platform policy advice
