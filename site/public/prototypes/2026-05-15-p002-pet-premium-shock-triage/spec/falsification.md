# Falsification checklist — Pet Premium Shock Triage

1. Do target users already solve this cheaply with one spreadsheet and no anxiety?
2. Does the trigger happen too rarely to justify a standalone product?
3. Are the required inputs too hard to collect for the promised output?
4. Would a professional advisor, platform-native feature, or insurer/legal/compliance portal own the workflow instead?
5. Does the prototype feel like a renamed dashboard rather than a new interaction around Enter current premium, renewal jump, pet age/breed, claim history, deductible, reimbursement rate, quoted alternatives, and known conditions?
6. Would users share or pay for the export artifact?
7. Can the design profile (calm-consumer) make the workflow obvious without visual clutter?
