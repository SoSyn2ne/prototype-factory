# PRD — Health Screening Turnaround Coach

## Problem
- A post-checkup reset planner that turns scary health-screening flags into a calm 8-week routine for food, movement, sleep, and retest readiness.
- Existing workflows split the emotional or financial pressure signal away from the next best action, so users stall, overspend, or default to chaos.

## Target user
- Adults who just received concerning annual health screening results and want a structured lifestyle turnaround plan before medication or worse numbers become unavoidable.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should understand the core plan in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: The first version should work with manual input and lightweight guidance instead of deep integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning product designed for one focused session.

## Non-goals
- Full marketplace, booking, or back-office replacement.
- Hidden AI automation that makes critical decisions for the user.
- Broad multi-role admin tooling beyond the primary workflow.

## Success metrics
- Users can understand the risk, upside, or social state in one screen.
- Users can identify the next action without reading a long manual.
- The interface makes the human driver of the product feel obvious rather than abstract.

## Notes
- Theme: 건강.
- Design profile: calm-consumer.
- Core UI sections: risk and retest summary, 8-week habit runway, meal and movement plan cards, sleep and stress tracker, lab marker trend panel, doctor question prep drawer.
