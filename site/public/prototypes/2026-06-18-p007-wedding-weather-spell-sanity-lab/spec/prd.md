# PRD - Wedding Weather Spell Sanity Lab

## Problem
A playful wedding panic board converts weather-spell purchases, forecast odds, and vendor backups into calm control.

## Target user
Engaged couples, bridesmaids, photographers, and wedding planners dealing with outdoor ceremony weather anxiety and ritual/spell purchases.

## Key UX
User drops in wedding date, venue, forecast, backup tent quote, vendor clauses, and silly spell receipt; the app turns magical thinking into a rational backup ladder and shareable panic meter.

## Required UI sections
- Forecast panic header with ceremony date, rain odds, and guest comfort risk
- Spell receipt drawer for Etsy ritual, lucky objects, and emotional-control notes
- Vendor backup ladder for tent, indoor flip, umbrellas, towels, signage, and photo plan
- Decision clock showing when each backup option becomes too late
- Budget sanity slider comparing $12 spell, $900 umbrellas, and $8,000 tent
- Guest experience preview for sunny, drizzle, storm, and heat scenarios
- Shareable calm card for planner, partner, and family chat

## Design profile
playful-experimental

외모 / 자산 방어 / 외로움 - wedding status pressure, fear of ruined photos, and desire for control.

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Wedding Weather Spell Sanity Lab" for outdoor wedding couples who are anxious enough to buy weather spells but still need real backup decisions. The first screen must be a working playful planning lab with a forecast panic header, spell receipt drawer, vendor backup ladder, decision clock, budget sanity slider, guest experience preview, and shareable calm card. Use realistic sample data: Saturday garden ceremony, 42% rain odds, $18 Etsy weather ritual PDF, tent quote, photographer sunset window, mother-in-law pressure, umbrella order deadline, and indoor flip option. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with a memorable concept-forward composition, bold readable accents, charming motion cues, and one obvious workflow that turns superstition into a backup plan. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Non-goals
- Do not build a marketing landing page for Phase A.
- Do not implement a custom demo during Phase A.
- Do not claim external sources prove market size; they are problem evidence only.
