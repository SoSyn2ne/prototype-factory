# Assumptions — 사장님 시범 한 번

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: Korean small-shop owners, clinic administrators, academy managers, and service businesses without IT staff.
- The observed workaround is: SOP document, screen recording, n8n/Make consultant, chat prompt, or continuing manual work.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: operator-dense.
- Source references are: S09.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: record one 90-second routine, point to the painful step, select exactly one automation candidate, and compare three shadow runs.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: verified one-step macro card with trigger, inputs, human checkpoint, break signs, and manual fallback.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: small-business associations, accountant/IT consultant referral, search phrase “반복 업무 자동화 어려움”.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: the owner can hand a one-page “what changed / how to do it manually” card to staff or a consultant.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
