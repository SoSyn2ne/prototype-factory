# Falsification checklist — 사장님 시범 한 번

1. First-time target users cannot explain the action after seeing: one 90-second recording with eight extracted step frames and a hard “choose one” gate.
2. The observed problem does not recur outside source references S09.
3. The existing workaround is faster or safer than the proposed mechanic.
4. Users do not save, show, or act on the output artifact: verified one-step macro card with trigger, inputs, human checkpoint, break signs, and manual fallback.
5. The operator-dense profile obscures the trigger or recovery state.
6. The first validation test fails to improve the chosen behavior or completion time.
7. The concept collapses into a generic dashboard, planner, proof packet, or content page when the mechanic is removed.
