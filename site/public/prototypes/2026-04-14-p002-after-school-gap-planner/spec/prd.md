# PRD — After-School Gap Planner

## Problem
- Parents spend heavily on academies, tutoring, and activities without a clear view of whether a child's weekly plan actually matches their academic or developmental goals.
- Weekly schedules often create hidden overload, dead travel time, or duplicated spend that only becomes obvious after burnout or disappointing results.

## Target user
- Dual-income parents managing multiple academy, tutor, and activity decisions for one or two school-age children.

## Constraints
- Time: A parent should understand one child's schedule-health picture in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: The first version should work with manual entry and simple progress scoring instead of school-system integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning experience used on weekends or evening schedule reviews.

## Non-goals
- Direct booking or payment for private tutors and academies.
- Replacing full school LMS or homework tools.
- Diagnosing learning disorders or giving formal counseling advice.

## Success metrics
- Parents can see where money, time, and energy are misaligned in one weekly view.
- The planner suggests a clearer timetable that reduces overload and duplicated spend.
- Users leave with a concrete next decision: cut, move, replace, or keep an activity.

## Notes
- Theme: 사교육.
- Design profile: calm-consumer.
- Core UI sections: child progress summary cards, weekly schedule heatmap, spending versus outcome panel, skill-gap tracker, recommended timetable builder, parent decision notes drawer.
