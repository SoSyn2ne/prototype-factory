# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Same-day cancellations are painful because lost chair time cannot be recovered later.
- Front-desk operators need speed and clarity more than feature breadth.
- Waitlist and patient-preference data are usually partial rather than perfect.

## ASSUMPTION
- A ranked candidate list can outperform manual recall and memory.
- Staff will adopt a dedicated refill screen if it reduces context switching.
- Basic patient-fit signals are enough without exposing full chart detail.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether SMS, call, or app notification is the most valuable first action to show.
- How much provider-specific nuance affects candidate matching.
- Whether single-location or multi-location operators are the stronger initial buyer.
