# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do clinics already refill most valuable same-day cancellations fast enough with existing PMS tools?
2. Is the main bottleneck patient responsiveness rather than operator prioritization?
3. Are waitlist and availability signals too incomplete for a trusted match score?
4. Would staff prefer a simple calling list over a richer scheduling console?
5. Is same-day refill more dependent on procedure duration accuracy than UX?
6. Does this only become compelling when tightly integrated with existing vendor ecosystems?
7. Would owners rather buy reporting on utilization loss than an intervention workflow?
