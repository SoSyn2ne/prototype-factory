# PRD — Dental Cancellation Fill Console

## One-liner
A clinic-ops console that refills same-day cancellation slots before chair time turns into lost revenue.

## Problem
- Same-day cancellations create wasted chair capacity that small clinics rarely recover fast enough.
- Front-desk staff bounce between schedule view, patient list, and call notes under time pressure.
- Teams need a focused refill console, not a broad practice-management suite.

## Target user
- Primary: dental practice managers, front-desk coordinators, and multi-location clinic operators.
- Secondary: owners reviewing schedule utilization and fill performance.

## Why now
- Labor and chair costs keep rising while clinics are pushed to improve utilization.
- Waitlist demand exists, but matching and outreach are still manual.
- Same-day slot recovery has immediate revenue impact.

## Key UX
- Spot open chair-time immediately and understand urgency by provider, procedure, and value.
- Review best-fit waitlist patients with confidence signals in one panel.
- Trigger the next outreach move without losing schedule context.

## Required UI sections
- Open-capacity hero
- Same-day slot board
- Candidate match list
- Patient-fit detail panel
- Outreach action composer
- Fill-rate summary rail

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: should feel lightweight enough for independent clinics.
- Platform: desktop web scheduling console.

## Non-goals
- Full EHR integration.
- Insurance eligibility workflows.
- Automated two-way patient messaging logic.

## Success metrics
- Increase same-day slot refill rate.
- Reduce time between cancellation and first outreach.
- Improve utilization of high-value procedure blocks.

## Stitch-ready design direction
Use a clean clinical interface with soft teal, white, and charcoal accents; emphasize calm hierarchy, fast readability, and the overall product feel dependable, polished, and operationally reassuring rather than hospital-heavy.
