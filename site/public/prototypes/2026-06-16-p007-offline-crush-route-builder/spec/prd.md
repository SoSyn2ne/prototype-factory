# PRD - Offline Crush Route Builder

## Problem
- A playful city-social route planner for dating-app-burned singles who need recurring real-world contexts, not another swipe deck.
- Evidence: S13 (Reddit r/GenZ, where to meet people without apps, recurring offline contexts).

## Target user
- Gen Z and millennial singles, people new to a city, friend-group builders, singles-event hosts, and dating coaches.

## Key UX
- A social route builder that turns interests, weekly recurring events, comfort level, friend invites, budget, and romantic intent into a low-pressure 4-week offline circuit with post-event reflection and shareable plans.

## Required UI sections
- Loneliness-to-momentum header with week streak and social energy budget
- Interest and comfort intake for sports, trivia, DnD, music, volunteering, classes, and coffee walks
- Recurring-context map showing weekly events, mixed-audience fit, cost, travel time, and conversation hooks
- Friend-to-date ladder with invite scripts, non-creepy follow-up timing, and group safety cues
- Four-week route calendar with one anchor event, one light invite, and one recovery night per week
- Reflection cards for who felt easy to talk to, where to return, and what to avoid
- Shareable wingmate plan for friends to join without turning it into a date pitch
