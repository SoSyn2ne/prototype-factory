# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- External signals show owners compare vet quotes, seek second opinions, research payment plans, and delay care under cost pressure.
- Clinics also experience repeated payment-plan requests and receivables risk.
- Design profile is calm-consumer; theme is 건강.

## ASSUMPTION
- Owners want a decision aid that respects medical urgency rather than a pure price-shopping tool.
- A call sheet is a concrete enough artifact to validate demand.
- Quote line items can be mocked without implying medical advice.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether clinics would object to users comparing line items.
- Which pet-care cases are best for first version: dental, surgery, emergency, or chronic care.
- Whether users will pay directly or expect this from insurers/rescues.
