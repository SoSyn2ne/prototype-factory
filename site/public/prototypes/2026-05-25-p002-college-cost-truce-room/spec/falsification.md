# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Does the prototype avoid being a renamed version of recent PF dashboards/planners?
2. Can a new viewer understand the trigger and target user without explanation?
3. Is the assigned design profile (calm-consumer) visible in density, palette, hierarchy, and interaction tone?
4. Does the primary workflow center on: Parent and student enter budget limits, dream schools, in-state options, scholarship targets, transfer paths, and debt boundaries; the app turns them into a negotiated school list and conversation script.
5. Are signal references (S3, S4) represented as problem evidence rather than market-size claims?
6. Are the required UI sections present without burying the main action?
7. Would the user have a reason to save, share, export, or pay after completing the workflow?
