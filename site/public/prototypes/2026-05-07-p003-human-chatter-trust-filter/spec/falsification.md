# Falsification checklist — Human Chatter Trust Filter

1. Can the target user recognize the pain from the hero screen without explanation?
2. Does the prototype use the cited raw signals as problem evidence instead of pretending to prove market size?
3. Does every major screen reflect the premium-editorial design profile?
4. Are all required UI sections visible or clearly represented in the flow?
5. Is the workflow meaningfully different from the last seven PF batches by user, trigger, data source, or interaction mechanic?
6. Does the output artifact feel worth saving, forwarding, or paying for?
7. Is the product non-advisory where legal/safety/financial/dating-risk boundaries matter?
