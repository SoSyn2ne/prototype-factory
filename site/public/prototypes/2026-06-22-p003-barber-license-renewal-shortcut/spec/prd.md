# PRD — Barber License Renewal Shortcut

## Problem
- Policy changes can simplify license renewals, but barbers, cosmetologists, notaries, and similar solo workers still need to know which steps changed before a license expires.

## Target user
- Licensed solo service workers and small shops affected by simplified 2026 renewal processes.

## Constraints
- Time: Must fit a short pre-expiry renewal session.
- Budget: Simple consumer workflow; no paid filing service in Phase A.
- Platform: Responsive Stitch web prototype with synthetic state/profession examples.

## Non-goals
- Do not file renewals or provide legal advice.
- Do not cover every state or profession with real-time rules.

## Success metrics
- User can identify changed steps, missing documents, fees, and portal handoff in one flow.
- First screen shows expiry pressure and a safe next step.

## Notes
- Design profile: calm-consumer.
- Theme: 자산 방어.
- Source: New York red-tape and license-renewal simplification signal.
