# PRD - Camouflage Shelf Sprint

## One-liner
A browser party toy where friends hide product stickers in a chaotic shelf photo and vote on the funniest invisible placement.

## Problem
Friend groups, streamers, design students, and ecommerce teams who want a quick visual party mechanic. are reacting to fresh market/community signals but still solve the work with scattered screenshots, invoices, threads, or ad hoc spreadsheets. The prototype should turn that repeated pain into one focused first-screen workflow.

## Target user
Friend groups, streamers, design students, and ecommerce teams who want a quick visual party mechanic.

## Key UX
Players place and tint stickers on a shelf scene, then a seeker gets 20 seconds to find them before the room votes on stealth and comedy.

## Required UI sections
- Lobby and 15-second rule card
- Shelf image playfield with sticker palette
- Tint/scale/rotate controls
- Seeker timer and reveal animation
- Vote results: stealth, funniest, most cursed

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- Viral hide/fool interaction

## Reference pattern
- Prop Hunt / Meccha Chameleon hide-and-seek + browser drawing toy

## Twist
- Turns camouflage into a product-shelf sticker game that can double as portfolio UX.

## Small group fit
- Friend groups instantly understand “hide this label so nobody sees it” and can play in one screen.

## Spread trigger
- The final reveal image is inherently shareable in group chats and short clips.

## Copy/paste Stitch prompt
Create a playful browser party prototype called “Camouflage Shelf Sprint.” Show a lobby rule card, a chaotic grocery or cosmetics shelf playfield, sticker palette, tint/scale/rotate controls, a 20-second seeker timer, reveal animation, and vote results for stealth/funniest/most cursed. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with a bold but readable composition, one vivid accent, tactile controls, and a shareable first impression.
