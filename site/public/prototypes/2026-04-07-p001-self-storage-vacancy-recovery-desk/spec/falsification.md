# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Operators already manage this effectively in existing storage software.
2. Recoverable MRR is too abstract to change daily behavior.
3. Vacancy recovery depends more on local demand than operational follow-up.
4. Pricing drift is not a major enough factor to deserve dedicated UI.
5. Teams need map views or facility photos to act confidently.
6. A next-action drawer feels too prescriptive for property managers.
7. Unit-type comparisons add clutter without improving decisions.
