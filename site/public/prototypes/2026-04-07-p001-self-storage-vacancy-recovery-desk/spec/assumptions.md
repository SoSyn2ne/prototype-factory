# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Vacant units create visible recurring revenue loss.
- Pricing drift and stale leads contribute to occupancy gaps.
- Operators need a recoverability view, not just a vacancy count.

## ASSUMPTION
- A queue ranked by recoverable MRR will feel more actionable than a facility-by-facility report.
- Unit-type comparison helps teams decide whether pricing or follow-up is the real issue.
- Leasing staff can act from a single-screen workflow without deep CRM detail.

## UNKNOWN
- Which lead signals best predict near-term lease recovery.
- How much pricing elasticity operators will want to see inline.
- Whether teams think in unit clusters, floors, or facility-level summaries first.
