# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- News volume around semiconductors/AI hardware is high and often repetitive; manually filtering is time-expensive.
- Retail investors frequently miss earnings dates and major scheduled events without a calendar.
- A short, consistent daily loop (same format, same time) increases trust vs. sporadic long reports.

## ASSUMPTION
- A “tripwire + next action” format (incident-response style) reduces impulsive trades vs. narrative commentary.
- Users will accept *rule-based* suggestions if uncertainty is explicitly labeled.
- We can get “good enough” signal using only free/low-cost sources (company IR pages, major news, SEC filings summaries, macro headlines) without paid terminals.
- The highest leverage early is not better prediction, but better *behavioral guardrails* (pre-commitment actions).

## UNKNOWN
- Which tripwires are most valuable for semis (export controls, guidance changes, supply chain disruptions, competitor launches, hyperscaler capex signals, etc.)?
- How to rank severity reliably without overfitting or hidden bias.
- Whether users prefer ticker-centric briefs or theme-centric briefs (e.g., “HBM”, “foundry”, “EDA”).
- The optimal delivery surface (Telegram vs. web dashboard vs. email) for daily consumption.
