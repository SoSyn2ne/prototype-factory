# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Private education spending pressure is emotionally charged and tied to fear of falling behind.
- Parents already juggle spreadsheets, chat screenshots, and academy schedules manually.

## ASSUMPTION
- A weekly keep-cut-replace workflow will feel safer than forcing a full annual plan.
- Parents value a calm decision record they can show to a spouse or to themselves later.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether tutors and hagwons would supply enough structured performance signals for semi-automated comparisons.
- How different the workflow needs to be for elementary versus middle-school students.
