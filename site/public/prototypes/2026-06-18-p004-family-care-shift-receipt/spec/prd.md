# PRD - Family Care Shift Receipt

## Problem
Adult siblings turn elder-care tasks, missed work, and Medicaid paperwork into a fair weekly responsibility receipt.

## Target user
Adult children coordinating care for aging parents, siblings negotiating responsibility, and family caregivers applying for support programs.

## Key UX
Care lead logs appointment rides, medication refills, phone calls, forms, missed work, and out-of-pocket spend; the app turns invisible labor into a shareable weekly receipt and next-shift handoff.

## Required UI sections
- This-week care load header with hours, money, missed work, and emotional-risk notes
- Task timeline for appointments, meds, errands, calls, meals, and paperwork
- Sibling contribution matrix with promised vs completed work
- Benefits paperwork tracker for caregiver program, Medicaid, respite, and doctor forms
- Care fairness receipt with hours, cost, urgency, and next owner
- Family message composer that is direct without sounding accusatory
- Emergency handoff card with medications, contacts, and next appointment

## Design profile
calm-consumer

## Theme
건강 / 외로움 / 자산 방어 - family duty, caregiver burnout, unpaid labor, and resentment prevention.

## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Family Care Shift Receipt" for adult siblings coordinating care for an aging parent. The first screen must be a working family-care workspace with a weekly care-load header, task timeline, sibling contribution matrix, benefits paperwork tracker, care fairness receipt, family message composer, and emergency handoff card. Use realistic sample data: one daughter handling most rides, one sibling sending money, medication refills, doctor appointment, Medicaid caregiver paperwork, missed work hours, and a Sunday family check-in. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with soft reassuring surfaces, warm neutrals, gentle accountability cues, and obvious next-step buttons that reduce family defensiveness. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Non-goals

- Do not implement a custom demo during Phase A.
- Do not claim external sources prove market size; they are problem evidence only.
