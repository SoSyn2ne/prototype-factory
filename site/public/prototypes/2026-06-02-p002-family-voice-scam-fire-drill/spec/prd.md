# PRD — Family Voice Scam Fire Drill

## Problem
- Families are anxious about AI voice-clone scams, but many older relatives resist abstract safety advice. The highest-pressure moment happens during a fake emergency call, so the product must rehearse behavior before panic, without generating non-consensual clones.

## Target user
- Adult children, family caregivers, and households with older relatives who are worried about AI voice-clone emergency scams.

## One-liner
- A consent-first household drill board that turns AI voice-clone panic into a practiced verification routine.

## Key UX
- Create a family verification protocol, rehearse safe-word and call-back steps, log who has practiced, and print a fridge-ready crisis card without generating or storing a real voice clone.

## Required UI sections
- Household safety roster
- Consent and boundaries panel
- Emergency call script
- Safe-word and call-back checklist
- Practice drill timeline
- Relative confidence meter
- Fridge card preview
- Scam report and recovery steps

## Design profile
- calm-consumer
- Design direction: reassuring family-safety colors, low-pressure checklists, large readable controls for older users, and soft but unmistakable fraud warnings.

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Family Voice Scam Fire Drill" for adult children and family caregivers protecting older relatives from AI voice-clone scams. The app should guide a household through a consent-first verification protocol: build a safety roster, choose a safe word, rehearse call-back behavior, track who has practiced, and print a simple crisis card. Do not include any real voice cloning or impersonation feature; make the product about prevention, consent, and rehearsal. Required sections: household safety roster, consent and boundaries panel, emergency call script, safe-word and call-back checklist, practice drill timeline, relative confidence meter, fridge card preview, and scam report and recovery steps. Design direction: use the `calm-consumer` profile with reassuring family-safety colors, low-pressure checklists, large readable controls for older users, and soft but unmistakable fraud warnings.

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: Spec/scaffold only until Stitch export is provided.
- Platform: Responsive web prototype generated by Stitch later.

## Non-goals
- Do not generate, store, or simulate a real person's cloned voice.
- Do not encourage deception or scare tactics.
- Do not provide guaranteed fraud prevention.

## Success metrics
- A household can understand the call-back and safe-word protocol in under one minute.
- The experience feels consent-first, readable for older users, and emotionally calm.
- Metadata and specs include signal references, design profile, theme, and full prompt.

## Notes
- Candidate type: practical / commercial.
- Novelty check: accepted because it is a rehearsed family protocol, not a fraud analytics dashboard.
