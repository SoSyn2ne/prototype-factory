# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. The prototype includes or implies real voice cloning.
2. The safety protocol is buried behind dashboards instead of visible as a simple drill.
3. Older-user readability is poor.
4. Consent and boundaries are missing.
5. The design profile `calm-consumer` is not evident in layout, color, copy, and controls.
6. Scam recovery and reporting steps are absent.
7. Metadata or PRD lacks signal references, theme, design profile, or full Stitch prompt.
