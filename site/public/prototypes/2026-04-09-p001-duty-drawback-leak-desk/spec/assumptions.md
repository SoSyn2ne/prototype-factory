# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Refund recovery work is usually constrained by paperwork quality and filing windows, not just by the cash opportunity.
- Finance teams need a ranked queue, not another raw transaction export.
- Desktop is the right starting point because users compare many line items and documents at once.

## ASSUMPTION
- Small importers have enough shipment and return data to estimate recoverable cash even before perfect systems integration.
- A visual reimbursement timeline will help teams decide whether to process internally or outsource to a broker.
- Users will trust a recoverable-cash score if the document gaps and risk flags are visible next to it.

## UNKNOWN
- How often filing rules differ enough by product category to require separate workflows.
- Whether users want broker collaboration inside the tool or only a handoff export.
- What minimum evidence set is needed for a convincing phase-one demo.
