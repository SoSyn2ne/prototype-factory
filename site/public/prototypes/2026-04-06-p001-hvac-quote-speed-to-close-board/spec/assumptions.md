# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Open quotes have value, age, and stage.
- Financing friction and homeowner urgency are major close blockers.
- Operators need an actionable priority list, not just a raw pipeline dump.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple scoring model can rank close likelihood and urgency effectively enough for prototype evaluation.
- Inside sales teams want a single-screen workflow for follow-up prioritization.
- Managers care about weekly close-rate momentum alongside deal-level detail.

## UNKNOWN
- Which objections matter most by ticket size.
- Whether teams prefer board or table views for quote triage.
- How much detail financing partners expose in real workflows.
