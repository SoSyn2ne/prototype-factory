# PRD — Roofing Quote Stall Rescue Board

## Problem
- Residential roofing deals often stall after inspection because financing, insurance paperwork, and homeowner follow-up happen in disconnected tools.
- Owners lose high-ticket jobs not from lack of leads, but from slow rescue on quotes already in flight.

## Target user
- Roofing company owners, sales coordinators, and office managers handling residential estimate pipelines.

## Constraints
- Time: Office staff should identify the top rescue opportunities for today in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: Must provide value even if the company is still coordinating through spreadsheets, CRM notes, and texts.
- Platform: Desktop-first internal board for daily sales standups and owner review.

## Non-goals
- Full CRM replacement.
- Insurance claim estimation or legal policy advice.
- Automated homeowner messaging in phase one.

## Success metrics
- Users can sort stalled jobs by revenue at risk and unblock reason in one view.
- Coordinators can decide the next rescue action without digging through separate systems.
- Owners can see where pipeline leakage comes from across inspection, financing, and insurance steps.

## Notes
- Design profile: operator-dense.
- Core UI sections: pipeline rescue KPI header, stalled quote priority board, inspection-to-close funnel chart, financing and insurance blocker panel, homeowner timeline, next-best-action composer.
