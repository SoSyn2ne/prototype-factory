# Falsification checklist — Agent Forensic Timeline Viewer

1. What observation would immediately disprove that target users are already pulling on this?
2. What workaround is good enough and kills switching (e.g., Notion template, r/smallbusiness thread, 네이버 지식i answer)?
3. What arrival path is too weak to reach users (e.g., keyword SEO vs. community embed)?
4. What hidden cost kills the ROI (e.g., manual data curation, legal review, partner integration)?
5. What operational bottleneck blocks delivery (e.g., single dev, mock data limit, mobile QA infra)?
6. What regulatory or trust constraint blocks deployment (e.g., legal advice claim, medical claim, financial recommendation claim)?
7. What would force a pivot to a different format (web app → calculator / field tool / printable)?
