# Falsification checklist (5–7)

Use these to kill the idea fast (≤1–2 days) before building.

1) **Coverage failure:** For a realistic watchlist (e.g., 20 SaaS/AI tools), can we reliably fetch at least 3 signals per entity weekly *without* authentication/ToS risk?

2) **Non-informativeness:** When back-testing 10 handpicked case studies (5 known “decliners”, 5 “winners”), do the signals fail to show clear pre‑trend deltas in ≥3 cases on the losing side?

3) **False positives too costly:** Do obvious benign events (major launch, pricing experiment, viral post) create “Red” alerts that a human would consistently label noise (>30%)?

4) **Explainability gap:** When we show a mock alert to 3–5 target users, do they say “I don’t trust this / I can’t act on it” because the drivers aren’t specific enough?

5) **Entity-type fragmentation:** Do we need totally different signal sets for each category (B2B vs consumer vs devtools) such that a generic product is not viable?

6) **Adversarial/gaming:** Can a company trivially manipulate the score (e.g., buying reviews, posting jobs) such that the radar becomes unreliable for investing decisions?

7) **Time-to-value miss:** Even if accurate, does the weekly cadence make it too slow (alerts arrive after the market already repriced)?
