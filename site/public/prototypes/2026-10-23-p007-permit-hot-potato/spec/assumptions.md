# Assumptions — Permit Hot Potato

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: friends, classrooms, streamers, planners, and urban-policy communities.
- The observed workaround is: dense city builders, debate games, or improvised role-play with no visible consequence.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: playful-experimental.
- Source references are: S13.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: one proposal, four secret interests, one concession per player, simultaneous vote, visible project deformation.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: deformed before/after proposal poster, vote breakdown, and seven-second failure clip.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: browser-game portals, classroom civics links, streamer clips, party-game communities.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: every failed vote produces a 7-second “concession pile” clip showing who killed the project and why.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
