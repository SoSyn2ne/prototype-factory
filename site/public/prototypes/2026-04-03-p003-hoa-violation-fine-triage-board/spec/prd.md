# PRD — HOA Violation Fine Triage Board

## One-liner
A property ops dashboard that prioritizes resident violation cases by fine exposure, response deadline, and escalation risk before avoidable fees stack up.

## Problem
- Community managers juggle violation notices, resident responses, and deadlines across inboxes and spreadsheets.
- Small misses turn into stacked fines, escalations, and poor resident experience.
- Teams need one triage workspace that shows which cases need action now and what resolution step is most likely to work.

## Target user
- Primary: property managers, HOA community coordinators, and portfolio operations leads.
- Secondary: regional leaders monitoring avoidable fines and escalation volume.

## Why now
- Rising operating costs make preventable fines more painful.
- More distributed property portfolios increase deadline management complexity.
- Teams often have case data but lack an urgency-first interface.

## Key UX
- Sort resident cases by deadline pressure, fine exposure, and escalation risk.
- Open one property and review violation history, outreach attempts, and the next resolution move in one place.
- Understand how today’s actions reduce future fines across the portfolio.

## Required UI sections
- Exposure summary hero
- Violation case queue
- Property timeline
- Resident communication panel
- Resolution action drawer
- Avoided-fines forecast rail

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: should fit property teams without enterprise customization.
- Platform: desktop web operations dashboard.

## Non-goals
- Legal document generation.
- Resident portal replacement.
- Full maintenance or leasing operations.

## Success metrics
- Reduce missed response deadlines on violation cases.
- Lower preventable fine totals and escalation rates.
- Improve operator response consistency across properties.

## Stitch-ready design direction
Use a calm municipal-ops visual system with slate blue, soft stone, and muted terracotta accents; keep hierarchy clear, queues and case details easy to scan, and the product feel credible, orderly, and de-escalatory while still action-oriented.
