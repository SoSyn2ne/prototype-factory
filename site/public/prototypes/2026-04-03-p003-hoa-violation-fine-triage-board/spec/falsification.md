# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do property managers already handle this adequately in generic ticketing tools?
2. Is the real issue resident compliance rather than staff triage quality?
3. Are cases too policy-specific to normalize into one board?
4. Would teams prefer a simple calendar and reminders flow over a richer dashboard?
5. Does value depend on direct integrations with property management systems and email?
6. Are avoidable fines too infrequent to justify a dedicated product surface?
7. Would leadership care more about audit reporting than an active operator queue?
