# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Users in the cited communities say this is already solved by one obvious template.
2. The trigger moment is too rare or not urgent enough to revisit.
3. The output artifact (PDF-style appeal packet with timeline, record checklist, and insurer letter.) is not trusted by the counterparty.
4. Users refuse to enter the required personal or financial context.
5. A simple static article performs as well as the interactive prototype.
6. The design profile (calm-consumer) makes the experience feel unserious for the stakes.
7. The idea is judged a near-duplicate of a recent PF batch despite the changed mechanic.
