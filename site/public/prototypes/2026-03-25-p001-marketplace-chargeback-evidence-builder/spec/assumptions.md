# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Core concept: A dispute-prep workspace that helps small sellers assemble the strongest chargeback response before payout gets clawed back.
- Primary user: Small marketplace sellers and ecommerce ops owners handling payment disputes.
- Desired interaction style: See which disputes are winnable, what evidence is missing, and what to submit next without opening five tabs.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer one operational overview before drilling into details.
- The first version can rely on mocked or manually entered data.
- The highest-value output is a clear next action, not a settings-heavy workflow.

## UNKNOWN
- Which signal users trust most when ranking urgency.
- Which export/share step matters most after the first review.
- How much historical context is necessary on the first screen.
