# PRD - First Ping Roulette

## One-liner
A playful roulette that picks one dormant friendship, shows how long it's been silent, and hands you a low-cringe first line plus a printable weekly mission card.

## Problem
Solo dwellers and 20-40s who keep reading "먼저 연락하라" advice but never do it; friend groups doing a reconnection challenge together. are reacting to fresh market/community signals but still solve the work with scattered calls, notes, screenshots, or community threads. The prototype should turn that repeated pain into one focused first-screen workflow.

## Target user
Solo dwellers and 20-40s who keep reading "먼저 연락하라" advice but never do it; friend groups doing a reconnection challenge together.

## Key UX
Add a handful of names, spin — the wheel lands on one contact with "마지막 대화: 217일 전", a choice of three first lines, and a send-or-skip decision; weekly missions print as a card.

## Required UI sections
- Contact wheel with silence-days counter
- Spin reveal moment
- Three first-line drafts with tone choice
- Send/skip log and streak
- Printable weekly mission card

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- 외로움 / contact-initiation ritual

## Source signal references
S20: Friend-app guides and 20s friendship-effort posts keep multiplying (Bubblic blog, 2026) https://www.bubblic.app/ko/blog/how-to-make-friends-in-your-20s.html
S21: 7-year solo-living essay: loneliness is managed with routines and initiating contact (Brunch essay) https://brunch.co.kr/@ryus/170

## Reference pattern
- roulette ritual + printable mission card

## Twist
- The wheel takes the blame for reaching out first — advice becomes a 10-second ritual with a concrete artifact.

## Small group fit
- One friend group can run a 7-day reconnection challenge from a single shared link.

## Spread trigger
- "이 룰렛이 너 골랐어" — the sent message names the wheel, and the mission card is photographed for group chats.

## Copy/paste Stitch prompt
Create a playful reconnection toy called "First Ping Roulette" for people who keep reading "reach out first" advice but never do it. The app is based on observed user pull: friend-making app guides and solo-living essays repeat that friendship after your 20s requires initiating contact repeatedly ("먼저 연락하고 거듭 얼굴을 비춰야"), yet people only read and save the advice. The app should use a roulette-ritual mechanic around the trigger moment "an empty weekend evening after seeing a group photo" and make the first screen communicate "이 친구와 마지막 대화: 217일 전". Use the user's own language: "오랜만에 연락하면 어색하지 않을까?". Build the core workflow around a contact wheel with silence-days counters and three tone-choice first lines, and produce the sent first message plus a printable weekly mission card with a streak. Required sections: contact wheel with silence counter, spin reveal moment, three first-line drafts, send/skip log with streak, printable weekly mission card. Emotional pressure to make visible: 외로움 — friendships quietly expiring while you wait to be contacted. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with a bold but warm composition, one vivid accent, tactile spin interaction, and a shareable reveal moment. Avoid generic dashboard framing; make the main interaction feel like letting the wheel take the blame for texting first.

## Non-goals
- Do not implement a custom local demo in Phase A.
- Do not present source signals as proof of market size.
- Do not turn the product into a generic dashboard without the specific trigger and workflow.

## Success metrics
- A viewer understands the target user and core action within 5 seconds.
- The primary workflow can be inferred from the first screen without explanatory marketing copy.
- The Stitch output preserves the assigned design profile and includes all required sections.
