## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Med Fee Pushback File" for adult children, POAs, spouses, assisted-living residents, eldercare advocates, and family coordinators facing surprise medication-management fees or facility policy changes. The app should record the old process, new facility demand, monthly fee impact, medication safety details, family roles, and written policy, then produce questions, negotiation notes, and a care-continuity plan. Required sections: fee shock header, before/after medication process timeline, facility policy and notice intake, monthly cost impact card, medication safety and responsibility map, questions for administrator and physician, family decision lane, and pushback memo plus care-continuity packet. Use realistic sample data with a $1,200/month medication-management fee, a parent in assisted living, existing family refill workflow, and a pending administrator meeting. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with warm neutral surfaces, readable caregiver cards, gentle but unmistakable cost warnings, and reassuring next-step flows for overwhelmed families. Do not make a landing page; the first screen must be the working caregiver evidence room.
