# PRD — Overtime W-2 Proof Desk

## One-liner
A payroll evidence desk that turns messy overtime hours into clean 2026 W-2 reporting, employee notices, and audit-ready pay records.

## Candidate type
Practical / Commercial

## Problem
- A payroll evidence desk that turns messy overtime hours into clean 2026 W-2 reporting, employee notices, and audit-ready pay records.
- Inspired by external problem signals Signal 1 and Signal 2 from today's search sweep.

## Target user
- Small business owners, payroll admins, bookkeepers, and franchise operators who must track qualified overtime pay, wage-hour changes, employee classification decisions, and year-end W-2 support without a dedicated HR compliance team.

## Key UX
Import timesheets, salaries, overtime premiums, pay periods, job roles, state wage rules, and payroll-provider exports; the tool separates regular pay from qualified overtime premium, flags classification changes, prepares employee explanations, and creates a year-end W-2 proof packet.

## Required UI sections

- qualified overtime premium calculator
- role/classification review board
- state wage-hour rule timeline
- employee notice composer
- W-2 support evidence packet

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop payroll-compliance product called Overtime W-2 Proof Desk for small business owners, payroll admins, bookkeepers, and franchise operators preparing for 2026 wage-hour and overtime reporting complexity. Users import timesheets, salaries, overtime premiums, pay periods, job roles, state wage rules, and payroll-provider exports; the app separates regular pay from qualified overtime premium, flags classification changes, prepares employee explanations, and creates a year-end W-2 proof packet. Include a payroll export import, qualified overtime premium calculator, role/classification review board, state wage-hour rule timeline, employee notice composer, W-2 support evidence packet, and bookkeeper/CPA handoff memo. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with crisp spreadsheet-like tables, calm navy and white surfaces, precise money deltas, audit-note sidebars, and restrained amber risk markers so payroll stress feels measurable and defensible.
