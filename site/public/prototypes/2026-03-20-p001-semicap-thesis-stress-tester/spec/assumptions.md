# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Semiconductor equipment demand is cyclical and sensitive to customer capex timing.
- Export controls and licensing can change TAM and shipment mix quickly.
- KRW/USD moves impact KR exporters’ reported margins and cost structure (timing varies by hedging).
- Higher funding rates typically compress equity multiples and reduce risk appetite.

## ASSUMPTION
- A small set of macro shock inputs (export severity, demand slump, FX, rates) is enough to *stress* a semicap thesis at a first pass.
- Mapping “shock → assumption status” via deterministic rules is useful even with false positives.
- Users prefer an explicit list of broken assumptions over a vague narrative summary.

## UNKNOWN
