# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Host community chatter shows same-day turnover pressure, late checkout conflicts, written SOPs, and photo/text proof practices.
- The prototype is assigned `field-ops` and theme `자산 방어`.

## ASSUMPTION
- Hosts and cleaners need a lightweight workflow between a generic checklist and full property-management software.
- Photo proof and timed handoff reduce review, refund, and damage-claim risk.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether cleaners will tolerate required photo capture on every turnover.
- Whether hosts would pay per property, per cleaner, or per turnover.
