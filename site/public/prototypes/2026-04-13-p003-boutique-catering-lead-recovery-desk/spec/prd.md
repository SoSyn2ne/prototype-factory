# PRD — Boutique Catering Lead Recovery Desk

## Problem
- Boutique caterers lose high-intent event inquiries when quote turnaround slips, menu decisions stall, or venue coordination details stay unresolved across email threads.
- Leads go cold not because demand disappears, but because the follow-up experience feels slow and fragmented.

## Target user
- Boutique catering owners, event sales managers, and hospitality coordinators handling inbound event leads.

## Constraints
- Time: A sales manager should identify and rescue the day's most valuable at-risk leads in under 10 minutes.
- Budget: The concept should work before CRM replacement, using imported inquiries and manual stage updates.
- Platform: Desktop-first hospitality workflow used by small teams juggling multiple live event conversations.

## Non-goals
- Full catering CRM replacement or invoicing.
- Menu costing engine or kitchen production planning.
- Venue contract management in phase one.

## Success metrics
- Users can instantly spot which event inquiries are at risk of going cold and why.
- Teams can trigger polished follow-up packages faster from one workspace.
- The product makes premium service quality visible inside a sales operations tool.

## Notes
- Design profile: premium-editorial.
- Core UI sections: lead rescue headline metrics, at-risk event inquiry list, quote turnaround tracker, menu and venue blocker panel, client conversation timeline, follow-up package composer.
