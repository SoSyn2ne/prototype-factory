# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Reject if the UI tells users what to sign rather than what proof to gather.
2. Verify contract timeline, messages, wage, housing, and visa risk are all visible.
3. Test whether a user can export an evidence receipt in under 5 minutes.
4. Confirm known/unknown states are clear.
5. Reject if translation notes look like official legal translation.
6. Check mobile use under stress.
7. Validate that the first screen slows down the signature decision.
