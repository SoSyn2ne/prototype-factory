# PRD — Hagwon Resignation Receipt Room

## Problem
- Hagwon teachers facing resignation or contract documents need to preserve proof before signing under employer pressure, but current help is scattered across screenshots, Reddit advice, and labor-office calls.

## Target user
- Foreign teachers in Korean hagwons facing resignation papers, contract amendments, wage issues, or MOEL escalation decisions.

## Constraints
- Time: Must help in the same day an employer asks for a signature.
- Budget: Evidence-room prototype, not legal representation.
- Platform: Responsive Stitch web prototype with synthetic contract/message examples.

## Non-goals
- Do not provide legal advice or tell users definitively whether to sign.
- Do not store real immigration or employment documents.

## Success metrics
- User can produce a pre-signature evidence receipt and escalation memo.
- Sign/wait/escalate options depend visibly on proof completeness.
