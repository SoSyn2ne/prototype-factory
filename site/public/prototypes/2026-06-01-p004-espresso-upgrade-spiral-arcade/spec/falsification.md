# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. The joke is too niche outside espresso communities.
2. Users want real product recommendations, not a playful simulator.
3. The upgrade ladder becomes a generic quiz rather than a memorable interaction.
4. Hobbyists reject budget-friction tools as anti-fun.
5. Partners are amused but not willing to share.
6. Product data ages too quickly.
7. The portfolio value is weaker than a more visual experimental concept.
