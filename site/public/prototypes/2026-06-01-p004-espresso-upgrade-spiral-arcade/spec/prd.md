# PRD - Espresso Upgrade Spiral Arcade

## One-liner
A playful hobby-spend simulator that shows coffee gear buyers the exact moment a tiny fix becomes a $5K identity quest.

## Problem
- Enthusiast communities often turn small equipment problems or mild curiosity into expensive upgrade ladders.
- Signals S9 and S10 show users asking whether a premium espresso upgrade is overkill and how a cheap repair can spiral into endgame gear.

## Target user
- Home espresso hobbyists, gear-curious beginners, and partners trying to keep hobby upgrades grounded.

## Key UX
- Pick a starting itch, explore branching upgrade paths, compare actual use and household constraints, then share a final receipt poster with joy-per-dollar and regret-risk scores.

## Required UI sections
- Starting itch selector.
- Upgrade ladder.
- Reality cards.
- Joy-per-dollar gauge.
- Regret-risk meter.
- Community quote carousel.
- Final receipt poster.
- Cheapest-fix reset.

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme

- S9: Reddit r/espresso, "Upgrading from Bambino+ [$1000-$5000 budget]", https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1tt2nbz/upgrading_from_bambino_10005000_budget/
- S10: Reddit r/espresso, "Upgrading my setup! [No budget]", https://www.reddit.com/r/espresso/comments/1singkm/upgrading_my_setup_no_budget/

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive playful web experience called "Espresso Upgrade Spiral Arcade" for home coffee hobbyists. Build an interactive upgrade simulator where a small coffee problem branches into repair, grinder-first, sensible machine, and premium endgame paths, with joy-per-dollar, regret risk, household constraints, community advice cards, and a shareable final receipt poster. Required sections: starting itch selector, upgrade ladder, reality cards, joy-per-dollar gauge, regret-risk meter, community quote carousel, final receipt poster, and cheapest-fix reset. Design direction: use the `playful-experimental` profile with bold cafe-inspired contrast, lively but readable interaction states, arcade-like progression, and a memorable portfolio-worthy first screen.
