# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. In a 10-user trial, do fewer than 7 users produce a numeric kill criterion and timeframe after one pass?
2. Do users still write non-falsifiable goals (for example, "get traction") even after guided prompts?
3. Compared with free-form planning on the same idea, is final decision clarity (`Go`/`Kill`/`Inconclusive`) unchanged or worse?
4. Does median completion time exceed the 3-minute target for first-time users?
5. Do users choose confirming tests over disconfirming tests despite the falsification-first sequence?
6. Are generated plans frequently non-actionable because proposed tests exceed the stated $0-$100 validation budget?
7. After one week, do fewer than 30% of users run any experiment from the plan they created?
