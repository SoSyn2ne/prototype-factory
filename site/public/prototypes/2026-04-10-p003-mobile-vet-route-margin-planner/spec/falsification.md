# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Are most mobile vet practices too small to need dedicated route-planning software?
2. Do clinicians override dispatch plans so often that a planner becomes irrelevant?
3. Is appointment urgency too unpredictable for pre-dispatch optimization to matter?
4. Would generic field-service routing software already solve enough of the problem?
5. Are margin estimates too rough to drive trustworthy decisions?
6. Does client preference for clinician continuity outweigh route efficiency gains?
