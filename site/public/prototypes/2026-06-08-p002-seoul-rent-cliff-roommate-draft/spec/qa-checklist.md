- User compares three apartment/roommate combinations, checks subsidy readiness, and chooses a draft pick with monthly-burn summary.

## Edge cases
- Subsidy window closes before lease start.
- Best financial match has high lifestyle conflict.
- Deposit risk is high despite low monthly burn.
