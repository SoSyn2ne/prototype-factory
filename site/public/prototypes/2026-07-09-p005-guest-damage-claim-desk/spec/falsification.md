# Falsification checklist

1. The first screen reads like a generic dashboard instead of insurance claim room + hospitality operations board.
2. A target user cannot identify the next action within 5 seconds.
3. The required UI sections are missing: Property and booking header; Photo evidence board by room; Cleaner/task invoice lane; Platform claim status and excluded-cost warnings; Next booking recovery checklist.
4. The design direction does not match premium-editorial.
5. The prototype depends on source claims as market-size proof instead of treating them as problem evidence.
6. The workflow duplicates one of the last 7 PF batches without a new user, trigger, data source, or interaction mechanic.
7. The final screen lacks a shareable output, packet, receipt, or result tied to: The before/after claim timeline is easy to forward to owners, cleaners, or platform support.
