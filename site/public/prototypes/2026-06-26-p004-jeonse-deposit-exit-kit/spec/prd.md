# PRD — Jeonse Deposit Exit Kit

## Problem
- A renter proof kit that helps Korean tenants assemble fixed-date, HUG, landlord, registry, and move-out evidence before their deposit is at risk.

## Target user
- Korean tenants and foreign residents approaching lease end, especially first-time jeonse or ban-jeonse renters worried about deposit return.

## Key UX
- Tenant follows a dated evidence checklist, uploads registry and landlord messages, then receives a move-out proof packet and escalation calendar.

## Required UI sections
- Deposit risk countdown
- Fixed date / address registration checklist
- Registry and lien snapshot tray
- Landlord message timeline
- HUG guarantee and claim stepper
- Move-out inspection proof drawer
- Escalation letter and consultation packet

## Design profile
- calm-consumer

## Theme

- S12: Korean jeonse fraud and deposit-return risk remain a structural tenant problem; official and tenant guides emphasize fixed date, registration, and HUG guarantee steps. Source: https://www.kdi.re.kr/eng/research/focusView?pub_no=18395
