# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Semis are cyclical; inventory + pricing + utilization often move together across the cycle.
- Macro risk can dominate even when industry KPIs look healthy.
- A small, consistent checklist reduces decision fatigue.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple weighted scorecard can be *useful* even without live data, as long as inputs are explicit.
- Users prefer “stance + receipts” over a raw numeric score.
- Presets (Bull/Base/Bear) help calibrate how the sliders map to reality.

## UNKNOWN
- Which KPI set most reliably matches the user’s mental model (inventory vs lead times vs bookings)?
- Whether users want separate stances per sub-sector (memory, foundry, GPU/AI) instead of one.
- How often users would update inputs (daily vs weekly).
