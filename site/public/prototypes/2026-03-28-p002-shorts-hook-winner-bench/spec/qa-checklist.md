# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Review the top hooks and identify today’s winner within seconds.
- Compare one winning and one losing concept using the retention drop chart.
- Make a scale or remix decision with clear projected spend allocation.

## Edge cases
- Low-spend tests should not outrank stronger hooks just because of noisy early data.
- Audience mismatch notes must stay concise and not overwhelm the layout.
- Multiple hooks with similar names must remain easy to distinguish.

## Acceptance verification
- Core sections from the idea brief are present and clearly labeled.
- The demo stub reflects title/one-liner correctly and the spec explains the product without placeholders.
- The Stitch prompt can be pasted directly into Stitch with no extra editing.
