# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. The board overemphasizes CTR while understating hold-rate quality.
2. Users cannot tell whether to scale, remix, or kill a hook in one pass.
3. The retention chart is too analytical for quick creative decisions.
4. Audience-fit notes feel subjective and weaken confidence.
5. The scale decision footer duplicates information instead of resolving it.
6. The product loses value if inputs arrive too slowly for daily use.
7. The Stitch export becomes visually noisy when all comparison signals are present.
