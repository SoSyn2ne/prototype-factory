# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- RSVP follow-ups have social cost; tone and relationship matter.
- Headcount certainty becomes more valuable as the date approaches.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple feature set (relationship, RSVP state, friction) is sufficient to rank follow-up urgency.
- Users prefer “polite template + editable” over free-form AI chat in a wedding context.
- Keeping everything offline increases trust.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users want separate scripts per channel (KakaoTalk/SMS/DM).
- Whether “friction” should be decomposed (distance, family dynamics, language barrier).
- Whether planners need export/print workflows (CSV, checklists).
