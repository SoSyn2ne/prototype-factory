# PRD — AI Coding Bill Explainer

## Problem
- AI documentation and coding workflows may increase medical-bill complexity, while patients still lack calm language for questioning EOBs and billing codes.

## Target user
- Privately insured patients, caregivers, and HR benefits helpers trying to understand higher medical bills.

## Constraints
- Time: Must help before bill due date or collections pressure.
- Budget: Consumer-facing explainer, not a paid patient advocate service.
- Platform: Responsive Stitch web prototype with synthetic bill/EOB data.

## Non-goals
- Do not provide medical, legal, or claims advice.
- Do not diagnose overcoding or accuse providers.

## Success metrics
- User can turn a confusing bill into three precise billing questions.
- First screen shows bill shock, code uncertainty, and calm next action.
