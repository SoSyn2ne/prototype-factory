# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Appeal filing windows vary by payer and directly affect the recoverable value of each case.
- Billing teams need a deadline-oriented queue rather than a flat claim list.
- Missing documentation is a common reason profitable appeals stall.

## ASSUMPTION
- Clinic teams will accept manual evidence-status updates if the prioritization payoff is strong.
- Recoverable dollars and filing deadline pressure are the two strongest ranking signals for daily use.
- A desktop control surface is the right first form factor for billing leads.

## UNKNOWN
- How often payer rules change enough to require active rule maintenance.
- Whether teams want appeal templates embedded in the same workflow.
- Which integrations become mandatory first: clearinghouse, PM system, or payer portal export.
