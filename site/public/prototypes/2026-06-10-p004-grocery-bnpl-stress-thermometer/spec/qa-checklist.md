# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Add a grocery BNPL receipt to the inbox.
- Drag it onto the payday calendar.
- See repayment blocks and stress temperature update.
- Pick one relief card.

## Edge cases
- Multiple receipts land on the same payday.
- An unexpected bill pushes the stress meter into danger.
- Essential purchase cannot be cancelled.

## Acceptance verification
- The Stitch output should show receipt inbox, payday calendar, repayment blocks, stress thermometer, tiny debt pile, relief cards, sliders, and future-me receipt.
- The visual direction should read as playful-experimental while staying readable.
