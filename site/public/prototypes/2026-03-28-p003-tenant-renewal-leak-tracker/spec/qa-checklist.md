# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Scan the triage list and identify which tenants need outreach first.
- Open one tenant record and understand the main risk signals immediately.
- Compare one save offer option and see its effect on vacancy exposure.

## Edge cases
- Month-to-month tenants should not break the ranking logic visually.
- Recent maintenance issues must be visible without overpowering renewal data.
- Projected lost rent needs to remain legible even when many leases expire together.

## Acceptance verification
- Core sections from the idea brief are present and clearly labeled.
- The demo stub reflects title/one-liner correctly and the spec explains the product without placeholders.
- The Stitch prompt can be pasted directly into Stitch with no extra editing.
