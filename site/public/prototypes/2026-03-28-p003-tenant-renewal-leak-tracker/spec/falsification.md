# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Users cannot tell whether a low-confidence renewal actually needs intervention now.
2. The tenant signal panel feels invasive or noisy instead of actionable.
3. Offer simulations distract from the more urgent communication timeline.
4. Projected vacancy exposure is too abstract without market context.
5. The workflow feels more like reporting than save-action planning.
6. The value drops if most properties handle renewals manually through email anyway.
7. The Stitch export becomes too crowded when risk, offers, and timeline coexist.
