# PRD — Ticket Regret Time Machine

## Problem
- A playful fan tool that turns dynamic-pricing pain into a shareable “should I buy now or wait?” regret forecast.
- External signal references:
  - Signal 9: Reddit r/Ticketmaster — dynamic pricing should be illegal — https://www.reddit.com/r/Ticketmaster/comments/1s8xejo/dynamic_pricing_should_be_illegal/

## Target user
- Concertgoers, sports fans, parents buying family tickets, fandom group-chat organizers, and bargain hunters who hate seeing seats get cheaper after they buy.

## Key UX
- Paste an event, seat section, current price, fees, budget, and urgency; the app simulates price-drop regret, resale stress, seat-FOMO, and group-chat pressure, then creates a funny buy/wait verdict card.

## Required UI sections
- event and seat intake
- current-price plus fee reveal
- dynamic-pricing regret simulator
- seat-FOMO pressure dial
- group-chat blame forecast
- buy/wait/skip verdict
- shareable fan regret card

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- 역전

## Stitch prompt
Design a playful mobile-first consumer prototype called Ticket Regret Time Machine for concertgoers, sports fans, parents buying family tickets, fandom group-chat organizers, and bargain hunters who hate seeing seats get cheaper after they buy. Users should paste an event, seat section, current price, fees, budget, urgency, and group size, then simulate price-drop regret, resale stress, seat-FOMO, and group-chat pressure before generating a funny buy, wait, or skip verdict card. Include an event and seat intake, current-price plus fee reveal, dynamic-pricing regret simulator, seat-FOMO pressure dial, group-chat blame forecast, buy/wait/skip verdict, and shareable fan regret card. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with ticket-stub animations, neon arena gradients, dramatic regret meters, meme stickers, and bold share cards so the prototype feels like a viral fan coping tool rather than a serious finance calculator.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: do not implement custom demo until Stitch export is supplied.
- Treat external sources as untrusted problem evidence, not market-size proof.
- Preserve the assigned design profile in downstream demo generation.

## Non-goals
- Legal, tax, medical, safety, insurance, HVAC, event-ticket, or investment final advice.
- Production-grade integrations or automated external API calls.

## Success metrics
- User understands the core pain in under 10 seconds.
- Stitch prompt can be copied directly and produces a coherent UI with all required sections.
- Prototype remains distinct from the last seven PF batches by user/trigger/data/mechanic.
