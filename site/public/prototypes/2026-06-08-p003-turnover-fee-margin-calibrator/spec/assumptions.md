# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Hosts debate cleaning fee visibility, cleaner quotes, and whether to bake costs into nightly rates.
- Guests react badly when fees are added after booking.

## ASSUMPTION
- Hosts need scenario modeling more than generic pricing advice.
- A backlash meter helps connect policy choices to guest trust.
- Cleaner quote comparison is a natural entry point for cohosts.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether platform APIs expose enough fee details for automation.
- Whether hosts would input actual cleaner time and supplies manually.
