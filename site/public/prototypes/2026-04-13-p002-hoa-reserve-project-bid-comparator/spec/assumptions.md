# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- HOA capital decisions usually balance reserve sufficiency, project urgency, and resident disruption.
- Board members often receive bids and reserve documents in fragmented formats.
- A clear recommendation narrative matters almost as much as the raw numbers.

## ASSUMPTION
- A side-by-side bid and reserve impact surface will improve board confidence and speed.
- Treasurers will value scenario views that explain the cost of delay.
- Community managers can maintain enough structured project data for a comparison product to stay useful.

## UNKNOWN
- How standardized reserve-study terminology and component scoring are across associations.
- Whether boards will trust summarized contractor comparisons without full attachment review.
- How often smaller HOAs have enough active bids to justify dedicated software.
