# PRD — HOA Reserve Project Bid Comparator

## Problem
- HOA boards struggle to compare contractor bids against reserve-study priorities, reserve cash timing, and resident disruption in one view before major repair votes.
- Decisions often swing on anecdote or cheapest price because the financial and operational tradeoffs are scattered across PDFs, meeting notes, and spreadsheets.

## Target user
- HOA board treasurers, community association managers, and reserve planning consultants.

## Constraints
- Time: A treasurer should prepare a board-ready recommendation in under 20 minutes.
- Budget: The concept must work from uploaded reserve studies, bid summaries, and manually entered project assumptions.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning tool used before board meetings and capital project approvals.

## Non-goals
- Contractor procurement workflow or contract execution.
- Full reserve-study authoring.
- Public homeowner communication portal in phase one.

## Success metrics
- Users can compare project options by reserve impact, urgency, and disruption in one screen.
- The dashboard makes deferred maintenance risk visible enough to support better board decisions.
- Boards can explain why a selected bid is financially safer, not just cheaper.

## Notes
- Design profile: financial-analyst.
- Core UI sections: reserve health summary, bid comparison matrix, project cashflow timeline, component urgency panel, owner disruption heatmap, board recommendation summary.
