# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do identity providers and current SaaS admin tools already solve this well enough?
2. Is AI seat sprawl still too small at SMBs to justify a dedicated product?
3. Are buyers more worried about generic SaaS offboarding than AI-specific workflows?
4. Is file/workspace transfer complexity too vendor-specific for a unified desk to stay simple?
5. Would teams only trust a tool that performs automatic revocation, not checklist coordination?
6. Is the real purchase trigger compliance reporting rather than operational risk reduction?
7. Are API limitations across AI vendors too severe for the roadmap users would expect?
