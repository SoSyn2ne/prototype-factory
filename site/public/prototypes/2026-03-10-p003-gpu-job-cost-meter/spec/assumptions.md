# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Electricity is a real cost and can be estimated from (W × hours).
- Utilization matters: low utilization for long periods is often accidental waste.
- Operators benefit from a “what to check” note more than perfect decimals.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users can reasonably guess average power draw for a job.
- KRW/kWh is easier for Korea-based ops; tool could later support currency switch.
- A simple opportunity cost per hour is sufficient for decision-making.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether most users want cost per output (per image / per 1M tokens).
- Whether operators will maintain a library of presets by model/job.
- Whether the biggest pain is electricity or time-to-result.
