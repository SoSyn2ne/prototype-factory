# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Buyers cannot access enough HOA/insurance documents to use the workflow.
2. Users confuse the product with legal or insurance advice and lose trust.
3. The exposure score feels arbitrary without citations to document excerpts.
4. Agents avoid sharing it because it may slow closings.
5. Brokers already cover the coverage-gap checklist well enough manually.
6. The product cannot distinguish special assessments from loss assessments clearly.
7. Users will not pay unless it connects to a quote, broker, or closing workflow.
