# PRD - HOA Assessment Shock Scanner

## One-liner
Condo buyers and owners paste HOA docs to spot surprise assessment exposure before the bill lands.

## Problem
- Condo and townhouse buyers often do not understand HOA reserve, master-policy deductible, loss-assessment, and special-assessment exposure until a multi-thousand-dollar bill appears.
- Community signals S1 and S2 show owners/buyers asking whether they can fight or predict assessments; formal signal S3 shows HOA fees rising as insurance costs pressure ownership.

## Target user
- Condo buyers, townhouse owners, buyer agents, and insurance brokers.

## Key UX
- Paste HOA budget, reserve notes, meeting minutes, master policy, and HO-6 coverage.
- Generate a shock exposure score, red-flag excerpts, deductible split estimate, missing-rider checklist, and buyer/owner action queue.

## Required UI sections
- Document intake strip.
- Assessment exposure score.
- Red-flag excerpts.
- Coverage gap checklist.
- Scenario table.
- Buyer/owner action queue.
- Exportable broker/realtor question sheet.

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app called "HOA Assessment Shock Scanner" for condo buyers and owners. Build a data-forward due-diligence workbench where users paste HOA budget/reserve/meeting-minute/insurance excerpts and get a shock exposure score, missing coverage checklist, deductible split estimate, red-flag excerpts, and a buyer action queue. Required sections: document intake strip, exposure score, red-flag excerpts, coverage gap checklist, scenario table, action queue, and exportable question sheet. Design direction: use the `financial-analyst` profile with institutional off-white, graphite, muted navy, restrained warning tones, compact comparison tables, and calm confidence cues; make it feel like a premium risk memo rather than a consumer quiz.
