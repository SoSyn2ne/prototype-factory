# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Most ad platforms constrain headline / primary text length and penalize unclear offers.
- Teams often need multiple variants to avoid creative fatigue.
- Copy/paste + spreadsheet is still a common workflow for launching creatives.

## ASSUMPTION
- A deterministic “template forge” can be more useful than a stochastic AI generator for early iteration.
- A simple heuristic score helps users choose without overthinking.
- Users are willing to provide structured inputs (audience, value prop, forbidden words) if the payoff is fast.

## UNKNOWN
- What default tones/templates produce the highest “keep rate” across niches.
- Whether users prefer exporting CSV vs. direct ad-manager formatting (e.g., one column per field).
- Whether scoring needs to be configurable (different brands optimize for different signals).
