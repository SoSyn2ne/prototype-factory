# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Creators care more about audience analytics than payout quality in first-pass prioritization.
2. The board feels too corporate for solo creators.
3. Deliverable readiness is not a real blocker before verbal agreement.
4. Cash forecasting is too uncertain to be trusted.
5. Brand-fit insights require more data than a simple desktop mock can credibly imply.
6. Users cannot quickly compare high-effort vs high-payout deals.
7. The Stitch output becomes visually noisy because too many commercial signals compete.
