# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Creators often manage deal flow in fragmented tools and need a clearer revenue pipeline.
- Not all sponsorships are equal; payout quality and effort cost matter alongside close probability.
- A single board can merge sales progress with deliverable readiness.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users want business-oriented visuals rather than social-media analytics visuals.
- Audience-fit signals can be summarized without requiring full audience reports.
- Weekly cash timing is more useful than monthly forecasting for small creators.

## UNKNOWN
- How much contract/legal detail belongs in the main board versus drill-down views.
- Whether creators prefer kanban dominance or metric-panel dominance on first load.
- How prominently reputational-fit warnings should appear without adding anxiety.
