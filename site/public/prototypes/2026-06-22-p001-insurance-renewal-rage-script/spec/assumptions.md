# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

- Source signals show small employers comparing unaffordable 2026 health plans and brokers/account managers manually shopping carriers under client pressure.

- Assigned design profile is operator-dense.

- Brokers would pay or share a tool that makes angry renewal calls easier, even if it does not connect to carrier systems.
- A demo can use synthetic plan data while still showing the real workflow.
