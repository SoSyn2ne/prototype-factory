# Falsification checklist

1. Five target users cannot explain the first screen in three seconds.
2. A note, spreadsheet, or existing product completes the job faster.
3. Users do not encounter the cited trigger often enough to return.
4. The proposed output artifact is not saved, shared, or acted on.
5. The core input is too difficult or unreliable to capture.
6. The assigned playful-experimental profile hides the main action on mobile.
7. Users mistake sample or self-reported data for verified external truth.
