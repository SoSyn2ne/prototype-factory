# PRD — College Essay Human Voice Alibi Studio

## Problem
- A playful admissions-anxiety studio that helps applicants prove their essay sounds human without turning their story into AI-detector paranoia.

## Target user
- Anxious college applicants, parents, counselors, and high-school writers worried that AI scoring or AI detectors will misread a real essay.

## Key UX
- Paste drafts, add personal memory fragments, process notes, counselor comments, and version history; visualize over-polish risk, collect human-proof artifacts, rehearse authenticity answers, and create a shareable voice receipt.

## Required UI sections
- draft paste lane
- human memory ingredient wall
- over-polish heatmap
- version-history scrapbook
- authenticity interview rehearsal cards
- parent panic meter
- shareable human-voice receipt

## Stitch prompt
Design a playful experimental education product called College Essay Human Voice Alibi Studio for anxious college applicants, parents, counselors, and high-school writers worried that AI scoring or AI detectors will misread a real essay. Users should paste essay drafts, add personal memory fragments, writing-process notes, counselor comments, and version history, then visualize whether the essay sounds over-polished, collect human-proof artifacts, rehearse authenticity answers, and create a funny-but-useful voice receipt. Include a draft paste lane, human memory ingredient wall, over-polish heatmap, version-history scrapbook, authenticity interview rehearsal cards, parent panic meter, and shareable human-voice receipt. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with scrapbook textures, bold school-notebook accents, animated detector ghosts, high-contrast before/after voice cards, and cheeky but supportive microcopy so the product feels portfolio-worthy and emotionally sharp rather than a sterile plagiarism checker.
