# PRD — Commercial Cleaning Renewal Risk Console

## Problem
- Commercial cleaning businesses lose profitable contracts when renewal risk hides behind service-quality drift, staffing instability, and margin compression until it is too late to intervene.

## Target user
- Commercial cleaning owners, account managers, and regional operations leads.

## Key UX
- Sort accounts by renewal risk, open one contract, and review quality drift, staffing issues, and the highest-leverage save action in a single workflow.

## Required UI sections
- renewal KPI hero
- at-risk account queue
- service-quality timeline
- labor and margin panel
- save-action drawer
- retained-ARR forecast rail

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; spec-first scaffold with stub demo.
- Budget: No external APIs or external LLM usage.
- Platform: Desktop-first service operations dashboard for account retention.

## Non-goals
- Not a field workforce management suite.
- Not a QA inspection mobile app.
- Not a billing or payroll product.

## Success metrics
- Operators can identify the highest-risk renewals in under 2 minutes.
- Selected contracts reveal service, staffing, and margin context without workflow hopping.
- The prototype clearly ties save actions to retained ARR.

## Stitch prompt
- Design a desktop service operations dashboard called **Commercial Cleaning Renewal Risk Console** for commercial cleaning businesses. It should help operators prevent contract churn by surfacing accounts with rising renewal risk caused by quality drift, staffing instability, margin pressure, and unresolved issues before renewal dates arrive. Include a renewal KPI hero, an at-risk account queue, a service-quality timeline, a labor and margin panel, a save-action drawer, and a retained-ARR forecast rail. **Design direction: use a modern field-ops visual system with slate-blue surfaces, cool gray neutrals, and vivid cyan accents; keep hierarchy firm and legible, dense panels easy to parse, and the product feel trustworthy, disciplined, and retention-focused.**
