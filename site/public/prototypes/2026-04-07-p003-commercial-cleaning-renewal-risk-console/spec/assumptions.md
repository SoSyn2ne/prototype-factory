# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Contract churn is expensive and often visible before renewal through service issues.
- Staffing instability and margin pressure influence account health.
- Account managers need a retention queue with concrete next actions.

## ASSUMPTION
- A unified renewal-risk score will be more useful than separate service and finance reports.
- Operators will value retained-ARR framing when prioritizing interventions.
- Quality drift over time is best understood in a timeline format.

## UNKNOWN
- Which service signals most strongly predict renewal failure.
- Whether regional leads want account-level or route-level rollups first.
- How much labor detail is useful before the screen becomes too operational.
