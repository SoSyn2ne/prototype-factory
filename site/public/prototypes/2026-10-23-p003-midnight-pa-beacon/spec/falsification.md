# Falsification checklist — 심야 방송 등대

1. First-time target users cannot explain the action after seeing: tabletop halo, detected timestamp, exact Korean line, translation, and a large “긴급 여부 불명확” state.
2. The observed problem does not recur outside source references S03.
3. The existing workaround is faster or safer than the proposed mechanic.
4. Users do not save, show, or act on the output artifact: timestamped local transcript-and-action card with source and uncertainty.
5. The field-ops profile obscures the trigger or recovery state.
6. The first validation test fails to improve the chosen behavior or completion time.
7. The concept collapses into a generic dashboard, planner, proof packet, or content page when the mechanic is removed.
