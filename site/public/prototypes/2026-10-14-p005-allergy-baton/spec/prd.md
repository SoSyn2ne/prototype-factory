# PRD — Allergy Baton

## Product intent
Carry exact allergy wording visibly from customer to counter, kitchen, and pickup without shrinking into a note.

## Arrival / validation
QR beside a pop-up menu or staff tablet. Test completion, conflict stops, and customer read-back confidence across three stations.

## Core loop
Select allergen → preserve exact phrase → counter tap → kitchen tap → conflict check → pickup read-back → receipt.

## First screen / key screens
One oversized color-and-symbol baton with three unchecked stations. Customer card, counter, kitchen, pickup, incident record.

## Interaction rules
Do not promise safety. Unknown ingredients or shared-fryer conflicts stop the order. Never rely on color alone.

## QA / risks
Shared fryer, unknown ingredient, substitution, rushed pickup, offline handoff. Main risk is false reassurance.

## Development start prompt
```text
Build a tablet/mobile station-handoff prototype with a giant accessible baton, exact wording, three acknowledgments, conflict stop, final read-back, and receipt.
```
