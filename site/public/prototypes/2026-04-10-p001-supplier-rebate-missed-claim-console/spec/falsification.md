# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Are most rebate programs too custom or contract-specific for a reusable dashboard to model credibly?
2. Do brands already outsource rebate claims, making internal triage software unnecessary?
3. Is data quality too poor to estimate threshold progress and payout risk with confidence?
4. Would a monthly accountant review catch nearly all value, reducing urgency for a dedicated tool?
5. Are missed rebates too infrequent in the target segment to justify workflow change?
6. Does proof gathering depend more on vendor relationships than on better software visibility?
