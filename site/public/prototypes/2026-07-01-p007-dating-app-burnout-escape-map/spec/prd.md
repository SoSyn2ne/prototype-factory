# PRD - Dating App Burnout Escape Map

## Problem
- If the apps feel like bots and dead ends, your next match might be at the $180 gym you actually enjoy.
- Source signals: S18 (Reddit r/dating, 2026); S19 (Reddit r/askSingapore, 2026); S20 (Reddit r/AskMenAdvice, 2026)

## One-liner
- A playful offline-dating route planner that turns app fatigue into a weekly map of gyms, classes, events, and low-pressure conversation openings.

## Target user
- Singles burned out by dating apps who are willing to spend time or money on real-world communities.

## Key UX
- Users answer what drained them on apps, choose comfort/budget constraints, receive an offline venue route and tiny conversation missions for the week.

## Required UI sections
- Burnout intake quiz
- Offline venue match map
- Conversation opener cards
- Budget/time commitment slider
- 7-day escape plan poster

## Design profile
- playful-experimental
- use the `playful-experimental` profile with energetic cards, clear map/list toggles, one coral accent, and humorous but not cringe copy; make the share poster the visual payoff.

## Theme
- 외로움

## Output artifact
- Shareable 7-day offline dating escape map.

## First validation test
- Post the map mechanic in dating communities and track whether users share their escape plan screenshots.
