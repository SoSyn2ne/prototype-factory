# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- 2026 external signals show U.S. households are worried about rising power bills and some customers are petitioning for utility audits after bill spikes.
- The prototype is assigned `calm-consumer` and theme `자산 방어`.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users will enter or upload enough bill details to separate usage, rate, fee, and weather causes.
- A plain-language explanation plus dispute/support script is more useful than another generic energy-savings tip list.

## UNKNOWN
- Which utility bill formats should be supported first.
- Whether users would pay directly or convert through an energy-audit, rebate, or consumer-support partner.
