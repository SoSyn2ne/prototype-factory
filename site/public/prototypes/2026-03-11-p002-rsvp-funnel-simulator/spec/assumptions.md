# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- RSVP responses typically follow a curve with spikes after reminders.
- Deadlines (venue/caterer) force decisions even with incomplete info.

## ASSUMPTION
- A small set of parameters (baseline conversion + reminder lift) is enough for a useful planning tool.
- Users can estimate reasonable ranges for open/respond rates even without analytics.
- A visual timeline (responses by date) reduces planning stress.

## UNKNOWN
- How to estimate reminder lift without historical data.
- Whether users prefer cohort segmentation (family/coworkers) vs one blended funnel.
- The best UI for expressing social "pushiness" and its tradeoff.
