# PRD — Kiosk Queue Body-Language Lab

## Problem
- Cafes and franchise shops often know customers freeze at kiosks only through staff rescue moments and complaints, not structured field evidence that can justify retrofit or workflow changes.

## Target user
- Cafe owners, franchise managers, kiosk vendors, and accessibility auditors observing real customers during slow-hour field tests.

## Constraints
- Time: Must work during a short in-store observation session.
- Budget: Tablet field-test prototype, not a full UX research platform.
- Platform: Responsive Stitch web prototype optimized for tablet use with synthetic sessions.

## Non-goals
- Do not record identifiable customer video or biometric data.
- Do not claim formal accessibility certification.

## Success metrics
- Staff can log hesitation, rescue, abandonment, and completion events quickly.
- Output report prioritizes kiosk steps that need retrofit or staff-support changes.
