# PRD — Three-Second Drift Desk

## Problem and target user
Office friends and stream viewers need a browser game that starts instantly and turns a visible human gesture into funny failure.

## Arrival, business route, and core loop
Choose mug, pen, or palm, calibrate in three seconds, drive a ten-second rally, watch the wipeout, race a ghost, and retry. Validate through completion, rematch, and clip share rates.

## First screen and key screens
Three giant gesture choices over a privacy-first camera crop. Screens: picker, calibration, gate, course, ghost, replay, leaderboard, rematch.

## Interaction and output
One gesture directly maps to steering; keyboard fallback remains available. Output is a six-second side-by-side gesture and car clip.

## Design profile, theme, and sources
- Profile: `playful-experimental`
- Theme: 재미
- Sources: S5 and Shakedown Rally viral scan

## Constraints and non-goals
No recording upload, biometric identity, or long onboarding; use mocked local gesture recognition.

## Success and QA
First drive begins under 10 seconds and rematch under 10 seconds; low light, missing object, fallback, false gesture, clean run, and wipeout states render.
