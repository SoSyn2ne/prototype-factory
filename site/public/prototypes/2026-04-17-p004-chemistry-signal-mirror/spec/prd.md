# PRD — Chemistry Signal Mirror

## Problem
- A playful attraction sandbox where two people trade tiny flirt signals, reveal reciprocity safely, and unlock bolder prompts only when mutual chemistry is obvious.
- Most flirting products jump too quickly from vague chatting to overt pressure, leaving users unsure whether desire is mutual or just socially risky.

## Target user
- Curious singles or couples in social chat contexts who want a safer, lower-awkwardness way to test attraction before making a bolder move.

## Constraints
- Time: The concept should communicate the main loop in under 3 minutes.
- Budget: Version one should simulate chemistry states without real matching infrastructure.
- Platform: Desktop-first concept demo focused on visual storytelling and interaction logic.

## Non-goals
- A full dating app, identity verification stack, or social network.
- Explicit adult content or unsafe consent-blurring mechanics.
- Long-profile browsing or algorithmic endless feeds.

## Success metrics
- Users instantly understand the mirrored-consent mechanic.
- The concept feels flirtatious without feeling creepy or unsafe.
- The first screen is visually memorable enough to serve as portfolio bait.

## Notes
- Theme: 성욕.
- Design profile: playful-experimental.
- Core UI sections: signal intensity stage, mirrored reciprocity meter, prompt unlock carousel, boundary and comfort toggles, shared chemistry timeline, playful next-move drawer.
- Stitch prompt: Design a bold desktop social concept called Chemistry Signal Mirror for people who want to test attraction without jumping straight into overt flirting. It should let two users trade tiny chemistry signals, measure mirrored interest, keep boundaries explicit, and unlock bolder prompts only when both sides are clearly reciprocating. Include a signal intensity stage, a mirrored reciprocity meter, a prompt unlock carousel, boundary and comfort toggles, a shared chemistry timeline, and a playful next-move drawer. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with glossy dark gradients, saturated cherry and ultraviolet accents, soft glass panels, and one crystal-clear escalating interaction path so the product feels sexy, mischievous, and portfolio-worthy without becoming crude or confusing.
