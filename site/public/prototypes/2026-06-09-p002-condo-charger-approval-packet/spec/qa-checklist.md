# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Open the Stitch-generated prototype and confirm the first screen is the working app for Condo Charger Approval Packet.
- Verify the primary workflow matches: A site-plan packet workspace where a resident or manager maps parking space, meter route, panel distance, trenching/restoration, insurance, billing, load-share assumptions, waitlist status, and board decision language into one approval package.
- Confirm the required UI sections are visible or clearly represented.

## Edge cases
- Long labels and dates fit on mobile and desktop.
- Risk or deadline states have visible severity without overwhelming the layout.
- Sample data references the cited external signals without copying source text.

## Acceptance verification
- p002 is scaffolded with actual metadata, source signals, theme, design profile, and Stitch prompt.
- No placeholder bullets remain in spec files.
- Phase A does not include a custom implemented demo.
