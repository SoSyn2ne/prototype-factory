# PRD — Service Fee Receipt Roast Arcade

## Problem
- Surprise restaurant service fees turn receipts into social content. Diners want to understand the real bill and tip math, while public backlash can become unfair or defamatory. This concept makes the feeling shareable but keeps the artifact focused on math and clarity.

## Target user
- Diners irritated by surprise service fees, friend groups splitting bills, and food-internet people who screenshot receipts for debate.

## One-liner
- A playful receipt scanner that turns surprise restaurant service fees into shareable fairness scores and tip math.

## Key UX
- Upload or mock a receipt, highlight service fees, calculate what the diner actually paid, choose a roast tone, and generate a shareable fairness card with tip guidance.

## Required UI sections
- Receipt upload/mock panel
- Fee highlighter
- Tip math calculator
- Fairness score dial
- Roast tone selector
- Share card preview
- Local rule explainer
- Restaurant response note

## Design profile
- playful-experimental
- Design direction: arcade-like scoring, bold receipt-paper textures, animated-feeling controls, punchy color contrasts, and screenshot-ready share cards.

## Theme
- 역전

## Signal references
- S10 SFGate Oakland restaurant viral service-fee backlash: https://www.sfgate.com/food/article/bay-area-burdell-reviews-21336111.php

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Service Fee Receipt Roast Arcade" for diners who want to understand and share confusing restaurant service fees. The app should let a user upload or mock a receipt, highlight mandatory fees, calculate true total and tip math, choose a roast tone, and generate a shareable fairness card. Required sections: receipt upload/mock panel, fee highlighter, tip math calculator, fairness score dial, roast tone selector, share card preview, local rule explainer, and restaurant response note. Keep it playful and non-defamatory: the output should explain the math and feelings without encouraging harassment. Design direction: use the `playful-experimental` profile with arcade-like scoring, bold receipt-paper textures, animated-feeling controls, punchy color contrasts, and share-card composition built for screenshots.

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: Spec/scaffold only until Stitch export is provided.
- Platform: Responsive web prototype generated by Stitch later.

## Non-goals
- Do not encourage harassment, review bombing, or defamatory claims.
- Do not provide legal advice about restaurant fee rules.
- Do not build OCR in Phase A; Stitch can show a mocked receipt flow.

## Success metrics
- User can understand total fee impact and tip options from a single receipt card.
- The artifact is fun and screenshot-ready without becoming harmful.
- Metadata and specs include signal references, design profile, theme, and full prompt.

## Notes
- Candidate type: fun / experimental.
- Novelty check: accepted because it is a shareable receipt arcade, not another compliance or finance tracker.
