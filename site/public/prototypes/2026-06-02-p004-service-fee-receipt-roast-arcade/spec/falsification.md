# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. The prototype encourages harassment, review bombing, or naming and shaming.
2. Fee math is unclear or hidden behind jokes.
3. Share card is not visually distinct or screenshot-ready.
4. Tip calculator does not handle mandatory service fees.
5. The design profile `playful-experimental` is not evident.
6. Local rule explainer reads like legal advice.
7. Metadata or PRD lacks signal references, theme, design profile, or full Stitch prompt.
