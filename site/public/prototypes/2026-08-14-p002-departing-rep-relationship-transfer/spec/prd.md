# PRD — Departing Rep Relationship Transfer Room

## Problem
- Small teams preserve promises, politics, and next moves before a sales or CS owner walks out the door.

## Target user
- Founder-led B2B teams and small agencies reassigning accounts after a sales or customer-success departure.

## Arrival path
- Search, community referral, or operator handoff at the trigger: revenue continuity / relationship memory.

## Business / validation route
- Test core-flow completion and artifact sharing; validate a paid lightweight subscription, template pack, or qualified partner lead where appropriate.

## Core loop
- Import an account list, record spoken promises and stakeholder politics, pair every account with a successor, rehearse the first message, and export a signed transfer receipt.

## First screen
- Communicate the one-liner in three seconds and expose the first action beside a realistic result preview.

## Key screens
- account import; promise and risk capture; stakeholder map; successor pairing; first-message rehearsal; transfer receipt.

## Interaction rules
- One decisive primary action per state; preserve entered data; show loading, empty, success, blocked, and error states.

## Output artifact
- Outgoing rep, successor, and founder sign the same transfer receipt.

## Share / return loop
- Outgoing rep, successor, and founder sign the same transfer receipt.

## Constraints
- Time: one-session prototype.
- Budget: realistic local mock data; no paid external API.
- Platform: responsive web, desktop and mobile.

## Non-goals
- Production integrations, legal guarantees, payments, or a custom Phase A demo.

## Success metrics
- First action understood in 3 seconds; core flow completed without instruction; output shared or saved.

## QA / screenshot criteria
- Profile operator-dense is unmistakable; primary interaction and result fit above fold; mobile has no overflow; all required sections are visible.
