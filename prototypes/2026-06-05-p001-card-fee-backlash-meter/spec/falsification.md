# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. Owners do not see surcharge backlash as a paid problem.
2. Processor consultants already provide better calculators for free.
3. Compliance concerns make owners unwilling to test surcharge copy in software.
4. The app cannot gather enough review/POS data to feel specific.
5. Staff scripts are ignored at checkout.
6. Cash-discount alternatives create equal or worse friction.
7. Owners prefer a one-time consultant memo over an interactive tool.
