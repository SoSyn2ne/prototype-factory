# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- People overweight recent narratives and underweight explicit re-checks.
- A time-bounded catalyst is easier to evaluate than a vague long-term belief.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple transparent scoring model beats a complex black box for personal decision-making.
- The act of writing falsification steps improves decision quality even if the score is imperfect.

## UNKNOWN
- Which catalyst types are most predictive for the user’s universe.
- Whether linear freshness decay is the right mental model.
- How many cards users can maintain before the process becomes too heavy.
