# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users ignore weights and still act on intuition (making the score unused)?
2. Does the process become too slow vs. a simple journal entry?
3. Are catalyst categories too coarse to be meaningful (everything becomes “earnings”)?
4. Does “freshness decay” unfairly down-weight long-horizon theses?
5. Do users treat the score as a prediction, rather than a decision aid?
6. Is the sprint checklist too generic to drive real verification actions?
7. Do negative catalysts get under-recorded due to confirmation bias?
