# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Core concept: A receivables screen that helps small teams prioritize late invoices by payoff likelihood instead of chasing every customer equally.
- Primary user: Small B2B finance leads and founders managing overdue invoices without a full collections team.
- Desired interaction style: Know who to contact first, which invoices are slipping into danger, and what follow-up move has the best expected recovery.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer one operational overview before drilling into details.
- The first version can rely on mocked or manually entered data.
- The highest-value output is a clear next action, not a settings-heavy workflow.

## UNKNOWN
- Which signal users trust most when ranking urgency.
- Which export/share step matters most after the first review.
- How much historical context is necessary on the first screen.
