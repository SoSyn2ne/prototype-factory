# PRD - Drone Roof Nonrenewal Defense Kit

## Summary
A homeowner evidence workspace for fighting insurance roof flags from drone, satellite, or virtual inspections.

## Candidate type
- Practical / Commercial

## Problem
- S6: Homeowners fear virtual or drone inspections will misread roof condition and trigger repair demands or cancellation.
- S7: Insurers increasingly use aerial images for underwriting, renewals, and claims, often before homeowners know there is a problem.

## Target user
- Homeowners, recent homebuyers, insurance agents, roofers, and family members helping someone respond to repair-demand or nonrenewal letters.

## Key UX
- The homeowner enters the insurer notice, roof age, inspection deadline, photos, roofer letters, and recent repairs; the app builds a response timeline, evidence checklist, contractor packet, and appeal letter.

## Required UI sections
- Notice intake with cancellation, nonrenewal, repair-demand, and premium-hike modes
- Deadline and coverage-risk header
- Roof evidence locker for photos, receipts, permits, inspection reports, and roofer letters
- Aerial-image challenge checklist for outdated imagery, moss/staining, false positives, and repaired issues
- Contractor visit planner and quote comparison
- Insurer response letter composer
- Agent and regulator escalation tracker
- Shareable family/agent packet export
