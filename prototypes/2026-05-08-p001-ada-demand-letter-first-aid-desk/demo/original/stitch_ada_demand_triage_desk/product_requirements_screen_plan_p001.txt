# Project: ADA Demand Letter First-Aid Desk (p001)

## Product Vision
An emergency operations command center for small business owners and agencies to manage ADA demand letters. It transforms legal threats into a structured technical remediation workflow.

## Target Audience
- Small ecommerce owners
- One-person businesses
- Web agencies
- Non-technical founders

## Core User Flow
1. **Intake**: Paste letter allegations and site details.
2. **Analysis**: Map allegations to WCAG issues and rank by risk.
3. **Execution**: 48-hour fix queue with screenshot evidence.
4. **Export**: Generate a counsel-ready response packet.

## Proposed Screen List
1. **Dashboard (The Command Desk)**: Overview of active letters, upcoming deadlines, and current triage status (Red to Green rail).
2. **Demand Letter Intake**: A multi-field form for allegations, URLs, platform details, and current accessibility status.
3. **Allegation Mapper**: A split-pane view to map specific letter claims to WCAG criteria and risk levels.
4. **48-Hour Fix Queue**: A dense remediation checklist and task manager for developers.
5. **Evidence Board**: A gallery of before/after screenshots with technical notes for legal proof.
6. **Counsel Export**: A final review and packet generation screen for lawyer handoff.
