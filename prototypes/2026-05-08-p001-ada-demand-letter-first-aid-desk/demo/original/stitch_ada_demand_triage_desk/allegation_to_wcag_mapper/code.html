[LEGAL_EXTRACT_START]

Plaintiff alleges that the website's checkout process presents a significant barrier to users relying on keyboard navigation. Specifically, upon reaching the "Shipping Method" selection modal, keyboard focus becomes trapped within the modal interface.

[CRITICAL_OBSERVATION] Users are unable to exit the modal using the 'Escape' key or navigate past the 'Confirm' button to access the main page content, forcing a keyboard trap scenario.

Furthermore, several custom radio buttons within this modal lack sufficient contrast against their background, specifically the 'Express Shipping' option which utilizes a light gray (#B0B0B0) text on a white background, failing to meet the required contrast ratio for non-text content.

[LEGAL_EXTRACT_END]
