# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. A user cannot identify the likely bill-jump reason within 60 seconds.
2. The app needs unavailable utility data before it can say anything useful.
3. Dispute guidance feels legally risky or too generic.
4. Assistance/rebate actions are too local to prototype without integrations.
5. Users only want a free calculator and show no willingness to pay or share.
6. Bill upload/manual entry is too tedious for stressed households.
7. The visual hierarchy increases anxiety instead of reducing it.
