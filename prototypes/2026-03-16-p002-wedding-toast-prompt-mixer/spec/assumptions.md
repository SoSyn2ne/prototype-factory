# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- People underestimate toast length; a timer helps.
- Structure reduces anxiety more than clever wording.
- The best toasts are specific (one vivid moment) and warm.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer selecting prompts over receiving a fully-written toast.
- A “tone guardrail” can prevent awkward jokes (by steering topic/phrasing).
- Estimated time from word count is “good enough” for rehearsal.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether people want a printable cue card vs phone teleprompter.
- Which relationships need distinct templates (parents vs siblings vs friends).
- How much customization is needed before templates feel restrictive.
