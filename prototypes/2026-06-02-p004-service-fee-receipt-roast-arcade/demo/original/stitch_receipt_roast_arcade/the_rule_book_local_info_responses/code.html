STAGE 1: THE LAWS

RULE 01 gavel

The Hidden Fee Trap

Service fees MUST be clearly disclosed before ordering. A tiny font at the bottom of the menu after you've eaten does not count as "clear disclosure" in most jurisdictions.

RULE 02 receipt_long

The Tip vs. Fee Divide

A "Service Charge" is legally the property of the restaurant, NOT a tip for the staff. If a restaurant claims it goes to staff, they must prove it or it's deceptive practice.

RULE 03 warning

Mandatory Gratuity Limits

Large parties (usually 6+) often trigger mandatory gratuity. However, applying it to small tables without prior notice is often contestable. Always check the fine print before the check arrives.
