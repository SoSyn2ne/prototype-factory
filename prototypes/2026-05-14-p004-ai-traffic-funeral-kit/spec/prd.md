# PRD — AI Traffic Funeral Kit

## Candidate type
- Fun / Experimental

## Theme
- 역전

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Problem
- A darkly funny creator tool that turns disappearing Google traffic into a shareable “what died, what pivots” recovery ritual.

## Target user
- Niche bloggers, indie publishers, affiliate-site owners, SEO consultants, newsletter writers, and creators watching search clicks vanish into AI answers.

## Key UX
- Paste Search Console snapshots, top losing pages, AI Overview screenshots, audience emails, newsletter CTAs, and monetized links; the app stages a mock funeral for dead pages, identifies survivor assets, suggests repackaging moves, and exports a memeable recovery card.

## Required UI sections
- traffic obituary intake
- dead-page memorial wall
- AI answer theft theater
- survivor asset triage
- newsletter rescue CTA builder
- repackage move spinner
- shareable recovery card

## External/community signal references
- Signal 3 — 9to5Google / Axios data — Google Search traffic to publishers has plummeted while AI referrals are under 1%: https://9to5google.com/2026/03/18/google-search-traffic-publishers-report/
- Signal 4 — Reddit r/google — can we please get rid of AI overview: https://www.reddit.com/r/google/comments/1las2mk/can_we_please_get_rid_of_ai_overview/

## Stitch prompt
Design a bold creator recovery concept called AI Traffic Funeral Kit for niche bloggers, indie publishers, affiliate-site owners, SEO consultants, newsletter writers, and creators watching search clicks vanish into AI answers. Users should paste Search Console snapshots, top losing pages, AI Overview screenshots, audience emails, newsletter CTAs, and monetized links, then stage a mock funeral for dead pages, identify survivor assets, suggest repackaging moves, and export a memeable recovery card. Include a traffic obituary intake, dead-page memorial wall, AI answer theft theater, survivor asset triage, newsletter rescue CTA builder, repackage move spinner, and shareable recovery card. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with gothic editorial humor, black-and-cream contrast, neon resurrection accents, theatrical cards, and one clear pivot-from-grief-to-action interaction so the product feels funny, shareable, and useful instead of just another SEO dashboard.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: spec/scaffold, no custom demo implementation.
- Preserve the assigned design profile in Stitch output.
- Use external signals as problem evidence, not market-size proof.

## Non-goals
- No external LLM API calls.
- No production backend or payer/platform/carrier integration in Phase A.
- No legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice claims.

## Success metrics
- User understands the target pain and next action within 10 seconds.
- The UI exposes all required sections with concrete sample content.
