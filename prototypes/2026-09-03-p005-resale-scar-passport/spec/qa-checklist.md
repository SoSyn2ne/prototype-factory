# QA checklist

## Happy path
- First-screen promise and primary action are clear in three seconds.
- Realistic sample data completes the full core loop.
- Output artifact matches the idea board.

## Edge cases
- Empty, loading, invalid, unavailable, and completed states are specified.
- Mobile or target-device controls meet the candidate's use context.

## Acceptance verification
- Exactly one design profile: `premium-editorial`.
- Source references: S5, S12.
- Buyer view reveals flaws first and supports missing-shot, repaired, unverified, disputed, and complete states.
