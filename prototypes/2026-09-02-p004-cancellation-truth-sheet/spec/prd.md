# PRD — Cancellation Truth Sheet

## Problem
Replay signup and cancellation side by side as sourced friction evidence.

## Target user
Korean subscription operators, compliance teams, advocates, and journalists.

## Core loop / key UX
Record timed paths, align equivalent decisions, annotate wording, and export a dated truth sheet.

## First screen
Make the core action and its evidence immediately understandable in three seconds.

## Required UI sections
flow recorder; signup timeline; cancellation timeline; step diff; wording evidence; consequence clock; export.

## Design
- Assigned profile: `premium-editorial`
- Theme: 소비자 보호

## Source signals
S4, S5 from `daily/2026-09-02/ideas.md`.

## Non-goals
- No production integrations or custom demo in Phase A.

## QA / screenshot criteria
Evidence and interpretation are visibly separated; changed and missing confirmation states are shown.
