# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Select a completed chicken order that was reversed after a complaint.
- Attach POS ticket, prep photo, and rider handoff note.
- Watch evidence completeness rise.
- Export a platform-ready dispute packet and owner-safe response script.

## Edge cases
- Missing bag seal photo.
- Repeat claimant with mixed valid and invalid complaints.
- Platform policy differs by country.

## Acceptance verification
- The Stitch output should show disputed order queue, evidence timeline, policy checklist, completeness meter, packet preview, and margin ledger.
- The visual direction should read as field-ops, not a generic finance dashboard.
