# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Export controls can hit different semiconductor nodes unevenly and then propagate downstream.
- Investors usually need directional triage faster than they need precision.
- Concentrated portfolios should show larger scenario sensitivity than diversified books.

## ASSUMPTION
- A single selected node is enough for a first-pass shock entry point.
- Longer horizons reveal more knock-on pressure for manufacturing-heavy nodes such as tools, wafers, and packaging.
- Representative listed tickers are sufficient anchors for diligence prompts, even if they are not the only exposed names.
- A simple 0-100 score is easier to compare and challenge than a more complex financial model in this prototype.

## UNKNOWN
- Actual policy scope by geography, threshold, exemptions, and enforcement timing.
- Company-specific mitigation: inventory buffers, redesigns, licensing carve-outs, local capacity, or customer substitution.
- How much market pricing will move on sentiment versus realized operational damage.
- The user’s true portfolio overlap with the representative names shown in the demo.
