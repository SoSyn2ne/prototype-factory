# Falsification checklist

1. The idea is too close to a recent PF batch without a new trigger, user, data source, or interaction mechanic.
2. The target user would not feel money, status, relationship, health, or opportunity pressure from the problem.
3. The required UI sections describe a generic dashboard instead of a concrete working app surface.
4. The source signals are irrelevant to the user pain or only decorative trend references.
5. The Stitch prompt omits the assigned design profile or contradicts it visually.
6. The first screen becomes a marketing landing page instead of the actual workflow.
7. The product cannot be explained in one clear before/after workflow.

## Candidate context
Design profile: playful-experimental
Theme: 외모 / 자산 방어 / 외로움 - wedding status pressure, fear of ruined photos, and desire for control.
Source signals:
- S13: Brides buy weather spells for perceived control over expensive weddings (https://nypost.com/2026/06/11/lifestyle/brides-paying-witches-to-conjure-perfect-100k-wedding-amid-soaring-costs/)

Stitch prompt:
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Wedding Weather Spell Sanity Lab" for outdoor wedding couples who are anxious enough to buy weather spells but still need real backup decisions. The first screen must be a working playful planning lab with a forecast panic header, spell receipt drawer, vendor backup ladder, decision clock, budget sanity slider, guest experience preview, and shareable calm card. Use realistic sample data: Saturday garden ceremony, 42% rain odds, $18 Etsy weather ritual PDF, tent quote, photographer sunset window, mother-in-law pressure, umbrella order deadline, and indoor flip option. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with a memorable concept-forward composition, bold readable accents, charming motion cues, and one obvious workflow that turns superstition into a backup plan. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.
