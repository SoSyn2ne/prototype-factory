# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Earnings events create concentrated attention and higher volatility in many liquid names.
- A checklist reduces errors in repeatable workflows (aviation/ops analogy).
- LocalStorage is sufficient for a no-login demo.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users can estimate a coarse "expected volatility" bucket (low/medium/high) from their own intuition.
- A single subjective slider (whisper vs consensus) captures enough narrative context to be useful.
- A process/readiness score will encourage preparation rather than false confidence.

## UNKNOWN
- What checklist items are actually predictive of better decisions for this user segment.
- Whether users prefer a per-ticker checklist or a global "earnings week" checklist.
- How much manual entry friction is acceptable before needing auto-import.
- Whether "whisper" framing confuses users who aren’t familiar with the term.
