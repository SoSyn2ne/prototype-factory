# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users interpret the readiness score as a *price prediction* even with warnings?
2. Does manual entry (date + vol + slider) feel like too much work compared to a simple notes app?
3. Are the checklist items too generic to change behavior ("I already do this")?
4. Does the date-range filter solve a real pain, or is sorting by date enough?
5. After 1 week, do users actually review outcomes, or do they abandon post-earnings notes?
6. Does "whisper vs consensus" add clarity, or does it create noise / anchoring bias?
7. Would users rather track *macro events* (CPI/FOMC) than individual earnings, making this niche?
