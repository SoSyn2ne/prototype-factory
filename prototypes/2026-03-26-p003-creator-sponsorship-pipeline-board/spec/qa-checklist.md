# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Scan the pipeline and identify the best deal to advance today.
- Compare payout quality and effort cost before committing to a negotiation push.
- Review likely close timing and this week's expected cash inflow quickly.

## Edge cases
- Deals with missing briefs should still appear actionable without looking complete.
- Low-fee but high-brand-prestige opportunities need nuanced presentation.
- Overlapping deliverable deadlines must be visible without cluttering the stage board.

## Acceptance verification
- The concept reads clearly as a monetization tool for creators within 30 seconds.
- Specs are filled with concrete product content and no placeholder bullets remain.
- The generated demo remains a stub and does not attempt full custom implementation in Phase A.
