# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Show the first screen to a benefits broker; if they call it a generic dashboard, the concept fails.
2. Ask a broker to build a call packet from three synthetic quotes in under 5 minutes.
3. Verify that the contribution slider visibly changes employer and employee pain.
4. Check whether objection cards use language from real renewal conversations.
5. Confirm the export memo is useful without external carrier integrations.
6. Reject if the product looks like legal advice or plan recommendation.
7. Reject if the emotional pressure of client anger is not visible in 3 seconds.
