# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S1: Among 76 wedding-fair contract disputes, 96.1% involved businesses refusing deposit refunds or demanding penalties, making the first days after signing a timer-based evidence problem.
- S2: Reported wedding disputes include a 3.1M won deposit refusal 153 days before the ceremony and surprise charges around guaranteed headcount, showing contract-stage damage and proof confusion.

## ASSUMPTION
- A focused prototype can be built around the trigger moment: A couple signs at a wedding fair and later realizes the cancellation/refund window matters.
- Users already have enough raw material to enter into the product: calling vendors, reading contract fine print, consumer agency complaints.
- The assigned design profile (financial-analyst) matches the decision pressure and intended buyer.

## UNKNOWN
- Exact willingness to pay for this narrow wedge.
- Whether Stitch will render all sections cleanly without manual correction.
