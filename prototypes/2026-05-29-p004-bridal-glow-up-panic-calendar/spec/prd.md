# PRD - Bridal Glow-Up Panic Calendar

## One-liner
A wedding-countdown beauty planner that turns last-minute glow-up anxiety into safe, budget-aware, photo-ready micro-decisions.

## Candidate type
Fun / Experimental

## Problem
The user enters wedding date, budget, skin concerns, current routine, procedure curiosity, and photo priorities; the app builds a countdown calendar that separates safe experiments, lock-in dates, no-new-product zones, emergency calm steps, and confidence rituals.

## Target user
Budget-conscious brides, grooms, wedding parties, beauty creators, estheticians, and photographers who field last-minute appearance panic.

## Key UX
The user enters wedding date, budget, skin concerns, current routine, procedure curiosity, and photo priorities; the app builds a countdown calendar that separates safe experiments, lock-in dates, no-new-product zones, emergency calm steps, and confidence rituals.

## Required UI sections
- Wedding countdown and panic-level header
- Budget and photo-priority intake
- Skin/hair/body routine inventory
- Safe-to-try calendar versus lock-in/no-new-product zones
- Vendor appointment and patch-test timeline
- Last-30-days micro-decision deck
- Photo-confidence pose, sleep, hydration, and stress ritual lane
- Shareable "do not panic buy this" card
