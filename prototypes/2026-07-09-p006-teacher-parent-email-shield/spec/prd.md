# PRD - Teacher Parent Email Shield

## One-liner
Teachers convert angry parent emails about cheating, phones, or discipline into documented, policy-aligned replies they can defend.

## Problem
K-12 teachers and department heads who need to respond to parent complaints without escalating conflict. are reacting to fresh market/community signals but still solve the work with scattered screenshots, invoices, threads, or ad hoc spreadsheets. The prototype should turn that repeated pain into one focused first-screen workflow.

## Target user
K-12 teachers and department heads who need to respond to parent complaints without escalating conflict.

## Key UX
A calm reply builder classifies the complaint, anchors it to policy, drafts a neutral response, and logs evidence for admin escalation.

## Required UI sections
- Complaint inbox with tone/severity tags
- Policy anchor selector
- Evidence and prior-contact log
- Reply draft with warmth/firmness slider
- Admin escalation summary

## Design profile
- calm-consumer

## Theme
- Education conflict / defensive documentation

## Reference pattern
- email triage + policy-backed response helper

## Twist
- Targets the emotional labor of parent conflict, not lesson planning or grading.

## Small group fit
- Teachers already copy-paste from old replies; a calming shield saves time and reduces tone risk.

## Spread trigger
- Departments can share reply templates and policy anchors across grade teams.

## Copy/paste Stitch prompt
Create a calm communication tool called “Teacher Parent Email Shield” for teachers responding to angry parent emails. Include an inbox, tone/severity tags, policy anchor selector, evidence log, warmth/firmness slider, reply draft, and admin escalation summary. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with reassuring warm neutrals, gentle accent color, clear next-step buttons, and supportive copy that reduces stress.

## Non-goals
- Do not implement a custom local demo in Phase A.
- Do not present source signals as proof of market size.
- Do not turn the product into a generic dashboard without the specific trigger and workflow.

## Success metrics
- A viewer understands the target user and core action within 5 seconds.
- The primary workflow can be inferred from the first screen without explanatory marketing copy.
