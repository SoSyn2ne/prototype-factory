# PRD — FAFSA Verification Panic Binder

## One-liner
A family-safe aid appeal workspace that shows exactly what documents to gather after a FAFSA mismatch.

## Type
Practical / Commercial

## Problem
Users are dealing with a time-sensitive, high-stakes workflow where evidence, documents, or trust signals are scattered across systems and the penalty for delay is financial loss, missed opportunity, or public embarrassment.

## Target user
First-generation students, parents, transfer students, and school counselors dealing with FAFSA verification, family-status mismatches, tax-data errors, or aid holds.

## Key UX
Answer a short intake about the flagged mismatch, then receive a document path, office-contact map, deadline clock, and plain-language explanation of what to ask the school financial-aid office.

## Required UI sections
- Panic intake wizard
- Mismatch type cards
- Required document checklist
- Family timeline builder
- School office contact script
- Deadline and hold-risk tracker
- Appeal packet export

## Design profile
- calm-consumer

## Stitch prompt
Build a web app prototype called "FAFSA Verification Panic Binder" for first-generation students and families facing aid verification after a FAFSA mismatch. The interface should include a calm intake wizard, mismatch type cards, required document checklist, family timeline builder, school financial-aid office contact script, deadline and hold-risk tracker, and appeal packet export. Use realistic sample data: parent identity theft tax issue, separated parents marked incorrectly, transfer school verification link, and risk of account hold. Design direction: apply the `calm-consumer` profile with reassuring spacing, warm neutral surfaces, plain-language microcopy, and highly visible next-step checklists for anxious families.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: spec and scaffold, no custom implementation.
- Demo must later preserve the original Stitch export environment.

## Success metrics
- User can understand the core workflow from first screen.
- Prototype presents a concrete, copyable artifact or decision output.
- Prompt includes design direction aligned with the assigned profile.
