# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S9: Refund and return abuse is large enough for card networks to launch AI risk scoring that uses transaction, return, refund, and dispute patterns, but small merchants still need operational decisioning.
- S10: Operators warn that solving fraud only with blunt rules can block legitimate repeat buyers and create angry tickets, so teams need pattern-based step-up friction and human-review lanes.

## ASSUMPTION
- A focused prototype can be built around the trigger moment: A suspicious return, refund, or chargeback appears while the customer still expects service.
- Users already have enough raw material to enter into the product: manual inspection, signature requirements, fraud apps, platform disputes.
- The assigned design profile (operator-dense) matches the decision pressure and intended buyer.

## UNKNOWN
- Exact willingness to pay for this narrow wedge.
- Whether Stitch will render all sections cleanly without manual correction.
