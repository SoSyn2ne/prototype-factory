# PRD — Delivery App Margin Truth Board

## Candidate type
- Practical / Commercial

## Theme
- 자산 방어

## Design profile
- field-ops

## Problem
- A restaurant channel-profit workbench that exposes when third-party delivery orders are busy but unprofitable.

## Target user
- Independent restaurant owners, food-truck operators, ghost kitchens, franchise managers, and hospitality accountants balancing marketplace discovery against thin margins.

## Key UX
- Enter menu items, in-store price, app price, commission tier, promo spend, processing fees, packaging cost, kitchen load, and repeat-customer assumptions; the app calculates true contribution margin by channel, recommends direct-order nudges, and drafts a transparent customer explanation.

## Required UI sections
- menu and channel intake
- commission and fee stack breakdown
- true contribution margin table
- promo burn simulator
- direct-order incentive planner
- customer price-transparency script
- weekly channel decision board

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop restaurant operations product called Delivery App Margin Truth Board for independent restaurant owners, food-truck operators, ghost kitchens, franchise managers, and hospitality accountants balancing marketplace discovery against thin margins. Users should enter menu items, in-store price, app price, commission tier, promo spend, processing fees, packaging cost, kitchen load, and repeat-customer assumptions, then calculate true contribution margin by channel, recommend direct-order nudges, and draft a transparent customer explanation. Include a menu and channel intake, commission and fee stack breakdown, true contribution margin table, promo burn simulator, direct-order incentive planner, customer price-transparency script, and weekly channel decision board. Design direction: use a field-ops aesthetic with sturdy kitchen-service cards, steel blue panels, high-legibility margin badges, tactile checklist controls, and decisive action rails so the product feels like a back-office command board for a live restaurant, not a pretty analytics vanity screen.
