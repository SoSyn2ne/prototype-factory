# PRD - EU AI Label Stamp Room

## Problem
- Marketing teams package AI-generated images, text, and public posts with the right disclosure label before the August 2026 transparency deadline.

## Target user
- Small marketing agencies, content studios, and SaaS teams publishing AI-assisted public content into EU-facing channels.

## Key UX
- Drop content cards into a labeling room, classify AI use, choose disclosure wording, and export stamped asset instructions for each channel.

## Required UI sections
  - Asset intake wall
  - AI-use classifier
  - Public-interest warning lane
  - Disclosure wording picker
  - Channel-specific label preview
  - Approval history
  - Stamped asset checklist export

## Design profile
- premium-editorial

## Theme
- AI content labeling / EU readiness

## Source signal references
