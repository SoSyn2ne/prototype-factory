# Assumptions — Artifact Closet Relay

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: museum visitors, families, classrooms, cosplay groups, and event hosts.
- The observed workaround is: annual costume contest, generic cosplay prompt, or social post with no shared timer/reveal.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: playful-experimental.
- Source references are: S14.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: draw silhouette, collect three allowed household categories, build for 90 seconds, freeze-pose, audience guess, reveal source.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: source-credited side-by-side reveal card and six-second transformation clip.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: museum QR, classroom activity, party link, maker-space event, short-form challenge.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: the side-by-side pose and original artifact create a funny, legible vertical reveal clip.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
