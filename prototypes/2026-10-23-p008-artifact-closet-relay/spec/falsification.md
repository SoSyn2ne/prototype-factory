# Falsification checklist — Artifact Closet Relay

1. First-time target users cannot explain the action after seeing: one high-contrast artifact silhouette, three material icons, one 90-second button, and no gallery browsing.
2. The observed problem does not recur outside source references S14.
3. The existing workaround is faster or safer than the proposed mechanic.
4. Users do not save, show, or act on the output artifact: source-credited side-by-side reveal card and six-second transformation clip.
5. The playful-experimental profile obscures the trigger or recovery state.
6. The first validation test fails to improve the chosen behavior or completion time.
7. The concept collapses into a generic dashboard, planner, proof packet, or content page when the mechanic is removed.
