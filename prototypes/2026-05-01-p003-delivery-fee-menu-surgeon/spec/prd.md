# PRD — Delivery Fee Menu Surgeon

## Problem
- A restaurant margin workbench that compares delivery-app commissions, item-level profit, and direct-order nudges before owners raise prices blindly.

## Target user
- Independent restaurant owners and managers who depend on delivery marketplaces but are unsure which menu items are losing money after commissions, packaging, promos, and refunds.

## Key UX
- Paste a menu with prices, food cost, packaging cost, delivery commission, promo discounts, and refund rates; the app marks items to keep, reprice, bundle, delist, or push to direct order; it generates a negotiation brief and customer-safe direct-order nudge copy.

## Required UI sections
- delivery channel margin table
- item surgery board: keep/reprice/bundle/delist
- commission and promo sensitivity sliders
- direct-order nudge copy lab
- vendor negotiation evidence brief
- weekly margin recovery target

## Design profile
- field-ops

## Stitch prompt
Design a tablet-first restaurant operations product called Delivery Fee Menu Surgeon for independent restaurant owners squeezed by third-party delivery commissions. Users should paste or upload menu items with price, food cost, packaging cost, marketplace commission, promo discount, and refund rate, then see which items to keep, reprice, bundle, delist, or push to direct ordering. Include a delivery channel margin table, item surgery board with keep/reprice/bundle/delist lanes, commission and promo sensitivity sliders, direct-order nudge copy lab, vendor negotiation evidence brief, and weekly margin recovery target. Design direction: use a field-ops aesthetic with rugged tablet-friendly controls, bold item status chips, kitchen-service urgency, high-contrast margin warnings, and action-first cards that a busy owner can use between lunch and dinner rush.
