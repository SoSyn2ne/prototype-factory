# PRD - No-Show Deposit Guard

## Slot
- p001

## Label
- Practical / Commercial

## Problem
- A reservation desk that helps Korean restaurants and appointment businesses enforce deposits without sounding hostile.

## Target user
- Korean restaurants, omakase counters, salons, clinics, repair shops, and studio owners losing booked capacity to no-shows.

## Key UX
- Build a reservation proof packet from deposit state, confirmation replies, prep cost, cancellation timing, and customer history; then choose a fair fee, reopen-slot action, or legal-counseling handoff.

## Required UI sections
- No-show exposure header
- Booking and deposit intake
- Reply-to-confirm timeline
- Prep-cost and staff-cost calculator
- Fair fee policy preview
- Reopen-slot waitlist action lane
- Customer message scripts in Korean and English
- Evidence packet export for counseling or platform dispute

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "No-Show Deposit Guard" for Korean restaurants, omakase counters, salons, clinics, repair shops, and studio owners losing capacity to no-shows. The app should turn reservation details, deposit state, confirmation replies, prep cost, cancellation timing, and customer history into a fair enforcement workflow. Required sections: no-show exposure header, booking and deposit intake, reply-to-confirm timeline, prep-cost and staff-cost calculator, fair fee policy preview, reopen-slot waitlist action lane, Korean/English customer message scripts, and evidence packet export for legal counseling or platform dispute. Use realistic sample data for a Seoul dinner reservation, a hair color appointment, and a repair slot. Design direction: use the operator-dense profile with compact charcoal/fog tables, crisp deadline chips, margin-aware cost cards, and one sharp action accent for slots that must be reopened. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working reservation desk.
