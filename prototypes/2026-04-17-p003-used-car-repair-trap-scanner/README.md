# Used Car Repair Trap Scanner

A buyer-defense workspace that helps used-car shoppers estimate hidden repair risk, compare listings, and script inspection-day negotiation before they inherit an expensive mistake.

## Status
- prototype

## Concept snapshot
- Theme: 자산 방어
- Design profile: field-ops
- Target user: Budget-sensitive used-car buyers who are visiting listings within the next two weeks and fear buying a vehicle that will immediately trigger costly repairs.
- Key UX: Bring scattered listing notes, inspection clues, and repair-cost fears into one practical board that tells the buyer which car to inspect, what to challenge, and when to walk away.

## Required UI sections
- listing risk queue
- inspection checklist board
- repair cost exposure estimator
- ownership cost horizon
- negotiation script panel
- walk-away verdict drawer

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop buyer-defense workspace called Used Car Repair Trap Scanner for shoppers who need a reliable used vehicle without inheriting a repair nightmare. It should help users compare listings, score hidden repair risk from inspection clues, estimate likely post-purchase costs, and bring a tougher negotiation script to the dealership or private seller. Include a listing risk queue, an inspection checklist board, a repair cost exposure estimator, an ownership cost horizon, a negotiation script panel, and a walk-away verdict drawer. Design direction: use a field-ops aesthetic with steel, graphite, and cool gray surfaces, one sharp orange warning accent, rugged checklist modules, and service-bay clarity so the product feels practical, trustworthy, and mechanically alert under pressure.
