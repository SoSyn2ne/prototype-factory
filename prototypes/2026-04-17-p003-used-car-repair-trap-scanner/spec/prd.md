# PRD — Used Car Repair Trap Scanner

## Problem
- A buyer-defense workspace that helps used-car shoppers estimate hidden repair risk, compare listings, and script inspection-day negotiation before they inherit an expensive mistake.
- Used-car marketplaces optimize browsing and excitement, not disciplined buyer defense against hidden maintenance risk and weak inspection-day decision making.

## Target user
- Budget-sensitive used-car buyers who are visiting listings within the next two weeks and fear buying a vehicle that will immediately trigger costly repairs.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should be able to prep for an inspection in under 8 minutes.
- Budget: Version one should use user-entered listing details and heuristic repair ranges.
- Platform: Desktop-first comparison and prep tool used before or after dealership visits.

## Non-goals
- Live VIN integrations or full dealership software replacement.
- Mechanical certainty or formal appraisal guarantees.
- Financing marketplace or insurance shopping workflow.

## Success metrics
- Users can rank listings by likely pain, not just price and mileage.
- Users can walk into an inspection with a clear challenge list and walk-away threshold.
- The product makes hidden future cost feel more visible than sales pressure.
