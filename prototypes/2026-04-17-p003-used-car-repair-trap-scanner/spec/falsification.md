# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users still default to brand reputation and ignore structured risk scoring?
2. Is repair-cost uncertainty too wide to drive real decisions?
3. Will sellers or dealers block the kind of inspection prep the tool assumes?
4. Does the concept become redundant if users always bring a mechanic anyway?
5. Are financing terms a stronger purchase driver than repair exposure?
6. Will users trust orange warning cues or see them as fear marketing?
