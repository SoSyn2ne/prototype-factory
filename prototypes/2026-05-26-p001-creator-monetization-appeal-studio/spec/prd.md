# PRD - Creator Monetization Appeal Studio

## Problem
A clean appeal-room that helps solo creators assemble proof they are original before a platform demonetization appeal.

## Target user
Solo YouTubers, animators, educational channels, and faceless creators who need to defend originality without hiring a consultant.

## Key UX
Upload or paste channel evidence, tag what proves human effort, then assemble an appeal timeline with screenshots, project files, edit notes, and a calm final appeal letter.

## Required UI sections
- Case status header with appeal deadline and revenue-at-risk estimate
- Evidence intake rail for project files, drafts, screen recordings, timestamps, analytics, and public comments
- Originality proof timeline showing human decisions across scripting, editing, recording, and publishing
- Policy-risk checklist for AI voice, reused assets, repetitive formats, and disclosure gaps
- Appeal packet preview with platform-ready language and attachments
- Public support card generator for creators who want viewers to comment or share
