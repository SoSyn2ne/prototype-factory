# Auto Bay Profit Scheduler

A service-bay scheduler that reorders jobs by margin and cycle risk so shop owners can protect throughput and profit.

## Status
- prototype

## How to use
- Review the product framing in `meta.json`.
- Stitch export has been ingested into `demo/original/code.html`.
- Open the PF-safe demo wrapper to review how bay allocation, tech load, and profit-per-hour are surfaced.
