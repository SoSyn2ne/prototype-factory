# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S5: Community backlash: customers notice separate 3-5 percent wellness/health fees and tell restaurants to raise menu prices instead.
- S6: Operator pressure: higher prices, worse perceived value, and refund demands turn pricing into a service-risk decision.
- Assigned design profile: financial-analyst.

## ASSUMPTION
- The cited signals represent a real trigger moment for the target user, not a full market-size proof.
- A focused prototype around the key UX is enough to test initial pull.
- The target user will trust a mock-data demo if the evidence and workflow language feel specific.

## UNKNOWN
- Exact willingness to pay.
- Which data import path will matter most after first user testing.
- Whether this should become a standalone tool, content lead magnet, or workflow module.
