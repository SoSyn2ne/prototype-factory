# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Most avoidable blow-ups come from sizing/discipline mistakes, not math errors.
- A simple “risk per trade” budget is common in trading education and easy to communicate.
- Users can estimate an entry and invalidation (stop) even if they can’t forecast.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users will accept a small number of inputs (4–6) if the outputs feel immediately useful.
- Volatility presets (low/medium/high) help novices pick a reasonable stop distance.
- Showing warnings (instead of blocking) improves completion rate.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether the default risk % should be 0.5%, 1.0%, or personalized.
- Whether users prefer sizing in shares vs notional dollars.
- Whether users want a “sell on close below” stop type vs a fixed stop price.
