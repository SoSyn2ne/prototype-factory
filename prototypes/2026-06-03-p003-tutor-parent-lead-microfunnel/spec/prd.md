# PRD - Tutor Parent Lead Microfunnel

## Problem
- A local tutoring lead cockpit for turning parent-group chatter into consults, trial lessons, and proof packets.
- Source signals: S7, S8, S9 from daily/2026-06-03/ideas.md.

## Target user
- Independent tutors, small tutoring studios, and former teachers trying to find students without relying only on referrals or expensive ads.

## Key UX
- Map local parent channels, score lead quality, draft non-spammy replies, schedule trial lessons, and assemble proof packets that help parents decide quickly.

## Required UI sections
- Local channel map
- Parent pain keyword inbox
- Lead quality scorecard
- Reply and DM composer
- Trial lesson slot board
- Proof packet builder
- Consult follow-up tracker
- Cost per booked consult strip

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Tutor Parent Lead Microfunnel" for independent tutors and small tutoring studios. The app should help tutors map local parent groups, neighborhood channels, school-specific communities, Google Business signals, and referral sources, then turn parent pain language into consults and trial lessons. Required sections: local channel map, parent pain keyword inbox, lead quality scorecard, reply and DM composer, trial lesson slot board, proof packet builder, consult follow-up tracker, and cost per booked consult strip. Use realistic tutoring sample data such as algebra panic, SAT prep, writing support, and parent Facebook group leads. Design direction: use the `financial-analyst` profile with clean performance metrics, disciplined comparison tables, restrained trust colors, and calm lead economics that make tutoring growth feel measurable rather than desperate.
