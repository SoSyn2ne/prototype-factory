# Refund Rescue Coach

A household refund recovery desk that helps renters spot deposit leak risks, stage evidence, and choose the fastest negotiation path before moving day turns into cash damage.

## Context
- Theme: 자산 방어
- Design profile: financial-analyst
- Target user: Korean renters preparing to move out who fear unfair cleaning fees, deposit deductions, or landlord stalling but do not know how to organize proof.

## Key UX
- Turn move-out anxiety into a visible recovery plan with evidence status, risk ranking, and the next best money-protecting move.

## Required UI sections
- deposit recovery snapshot
- move-out deadline timeline
- deduction risk checklist
- evidence locker board
- negotiation path compare panel
- refund action log drawer

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop household refund recovery workspace called Refund Rescue Coach for Korean renters preparing to move out and protect their housing deposit from unfair deductions or delays. It should help users organize evidence, spot the biggest deposit leak risks, and choose the fastest negotiation path before moving day turns into cash damage. Include a deposit recovery snapshot, a move-out deadline timeline, a deduction risk checklist, an evidence locker board, a negotiation path compare panel, and a refund action log drawer. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with precise comparison panels, muted navy and graphite tones, disciplined tables, and careful warning accents so the product feels rigorous, protective, and built for cash-defense decisions.
