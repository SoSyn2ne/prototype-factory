# PRD — Refund Rescue Coach

## Problem
- A household refund recovery desk that helps renters spot deposit leak risks, stage evidence, and choose the fastest negotiation path before moving day turns into cash damage.
- Most renters collect photos, chat logs, and fee quotes too late, so they lose leverage exactly when cash stress peaks.

## Target user
- Korean renters preparing to move out who fear unfair cleaning fees, deposit deductions, or landlord stalling but do not know how to organize proof.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should understand the top action path in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: The first version should work with manual input, lightweight imports, and no deep enterprise integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning product designed for a single high-focus session.

## Non-goals
- Full back-office system replacement.
- Heavy automation that requires hidden AI decisions.
- Multi-role admin tooling beyond the core operator or end-user workflow.

## Success metrics
- Users can understand risk, upside, or emotional momentum in one screen.
- Users can identify the next action without reading a long manual.
- The interface makes the human driver of the product feel obvious rather than abstract.

## Notes
- Theme: 자산 방어.
- Design profile: financial-analyst.
- Core UI sections: deposit recovery snapshot, move-out deadline timeline, deduction risk checklist, evidence locker board, negotiation path compare panel, refund action log drawer.
