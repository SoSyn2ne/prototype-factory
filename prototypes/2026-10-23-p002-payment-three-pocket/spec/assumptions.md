# Assumptions — 결제 3칸 포켓

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: first-time Korea visitors, exchange students, older travelers, and hotels onboarding guests.
- The observed workaround is: screenshots, blog lists, several cards, cash envelopes, and asking again at each counter.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: calm-consumer.
- Source references are: S02.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: drag payment tools into Primary, Backup, and Emergency pockets, then survive three realistic failure cards.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: bilingual offline three-pocket payment card tied to three planned stops.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: pre-trip checklist, airline/hotel welcome email, Korea travel communities, search phrase “한국 여행 결제 뭐 준비”.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: a bilingual three-pocket card can be saved offline and forwarded to travel companions.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
