# PRD - Companion App Privacy Confession Booth

## Problem
- A playful but pointed privacy mirror that helps AI companion users reveal what intimate data, token fees, and emotional hooks they are trading away.
- Source evidence: S16 - Apple App Store listing for VIRL AI video/photo prank edit app: Viral AI prank/photo apps market fake relationship edits and shock transformations as shareable attention products. S17 - Reddit r/artificial post on AI girlfriend app privacy risks: Users interested in companion bots worry they are feeding intimate psychological profiles to advertisers.

## Target user
- Curious or lonely AI companion users who want connection but feel uneasy about privacy, pricing, and emotional dependency.

## Key UX
- Answer confession-card prompts about what you would tell a bot, what you would pay for, and what would hurt to lose; the app returns a privacy risk mirror and safer-use pact.

## Required UI sections
- Confession card stack
- Intimacy data map
- Token and paywall creep meter
- Ad-targeting fear checklist
- Boundary pact builder
- Delete/export reminder
- Shareable redacted mirror

## Assigned design profile
- playful-experimental
- Design direction: playful-experimental mood with neon confession cards, privacy-warning contrast, and humorous but humane microcopy.

## Theme
- 외로움 / 성욕
