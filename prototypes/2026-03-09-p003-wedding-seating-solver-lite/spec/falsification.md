# Falsification checklist (7)

1. Do users reject drag-and-drop and prefer spreadsheet-style editing for speed?
2. Are "cannot sit together" constraints too sensitive to encode (users avoid typing them)?
3. Does the tool create a false sense of correctness (warnings missed, but user trusts it)?
4. Is capacity/conflict checking not the real pain (the pain is negotiating with relatives)?
5. Do users need a much richer model immediately (kid chairs, high chairs, accessibility, plus-ones)?
6. Is export to JSON/text insufficient because printing place cards is the primary deliverable?
7. Do people already use an incumbent product that makes this redundant (planner suites)?
