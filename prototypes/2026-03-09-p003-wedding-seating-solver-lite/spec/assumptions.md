# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Table capacity overflow is a common mistake in manual seating.
- Social constraints (exes, feuds, politics) are real and change often.

## ASSUMPTION
- Drag-and-drop is the fastest mental model for most users.
- Most users start with a rough draft, then refine; they need warnings more than automation.
- A small set of constraints ("cannot sit together") captures a large portion of real pain.

## UNKNOWN
- How many tables/guests typical users need in the first session.
- Whether people want "must sit together" groups as strongly as "cannot sit together" pairs.
- Whether planners require printable layout output (cards) vs. just a plan.
