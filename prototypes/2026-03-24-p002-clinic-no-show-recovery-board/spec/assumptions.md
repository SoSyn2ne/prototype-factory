# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Core concept: A clinic recovery screen that helps staff rescue unused appointment slots before revenue disappears.
- Primary user: Small clinic managers and front-desk staff dealing with same-day cancellations and no-shows.
- Desired interaction style: Spot empty slots, waiting-list matches, and recovery value immediately so staff can refill the day with minimal calls.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer one operational overview before drilling into details.
- The first version can rely on mocked or manually entered data.
- The highest-value output is a clear next action, not a dense settings flow.

## UNKNOWN
- Which panel users trust first in real usage.
- Which export/share format matters most after validation.
- How much historical detail is necessary on the first screen.
