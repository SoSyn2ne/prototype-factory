# PRD - Writing Proof Classroom

## One-liner
A low-friction writing room that records draft process evidence without turning every essay into an AI accusation fight.

## Candidate type
Practical / Commercial

## Problem
The teacher sends a no-login assignment link; students draft inside a focused editor that records writing sessions, paste events, pauses, outlines, and revision checkpoints. Teachers review a process timeline and coaching notes instead of relying on AI-detector scores.

## Target user
High-school writing teachers, private tutors, after-school academies, homeschool co-ops, and academic-support operators.

## Key UX
The teacher sends a no-login assignment link; students draft inside a focused editor that records writing sessions, paste events, pauses, outlines, and revision checkpoints. Teachers review a process timeline and coaching notes instead of relying on AI-detector scores.

## Required UI sections
- Assignment launch header with class, due date, and allowed-support rules
- Student focused writing room with outline, draft, reflection, and submit steps
- Paste/import event log and version-history strip
- Teacher process-timeline review
- Student explanation and conference note panel
- Parent/admin share packet
- Tutor coaching rubric
- Policy-safe export without AI probability scoring
