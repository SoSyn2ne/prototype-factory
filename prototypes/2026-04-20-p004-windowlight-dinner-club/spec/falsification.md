# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Would users actually open this at dinner instead of defaulting to video or feeds?
2. Does the concept feel meaningful enough without direct conversation?
3. Is the emotional value too dependent on a critical mass of concurrent users?
4. Would people interpret the product as sad rather than comforting?
5. Does anonymity make the room feel empty instead of gentle?
6. Can the UI stay magical without becoming visually cluttered?
7. Is this better as a mobile ambient object than a desktop prototype?
