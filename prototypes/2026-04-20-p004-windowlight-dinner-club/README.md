# Windowlight Dinner Club

A dreamy social ritual prototype where solo eaters share anonymous dinner moods, ambient tablescapes, and tiny acts of company instead of eating alone with a dead scroll.

## Status
- prototype

## Theme
- 외로움

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Key UX
- Pick tonight’s dinner mood, enter a shared ambient room, and trade tiny signals of presence while the table scene slowly changes with everyone’s emotional tempo.

## Required UI sections
- ambient dinner stage
- mood table selector
- anonymous presence feed
- tiny gesture tray
- room warmth meter
- collectible scene capture drawer

## Stitch prompt
```
Design a whimsical desktop social ritual prototype called Windowlight Dinner Club for solo dwellers who want a gentler sense of company while eating alone. It should let users choose a dinner mood, enter a shared ambient room, exchange tiny anonymous gestures, and watch the table atmosphere shift as more people quietly join the ritual. Include an ambient dinner stage, a mood table selector, an anonymous presence feed, a tiny gesture tray, a room warmth meter, and a collectible scene capture drawer. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with cinematic window-glow lighting, saturated dusk colors, soft glass overlays, and a memorable storybook composition so the product feels intimate, shareable, and emotionally warm without turning into a chat app.
```
