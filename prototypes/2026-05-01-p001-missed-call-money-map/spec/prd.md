# PRD — Missed Call Money Map

## Problem
- A small-business lead triage board that turns missed calls, voicemail snippets, and AI-receptionist pricing anxiety into a callback priority map and buy-vs-hire decision.

## Target user
- Solo and small local-service owners who miss calls while serving customers: clinics, repair shops, salons, contractors, studios, and appointment-led storefronts.

## Key UX
- Import a call log or paste missed-call notes; tag call intent and possible job value; replay the lost revenue timeline; compare callback, AI receptionist, and human answering options; produce a 7-day callback sprint and vendor-fit brief.

## Required UI sections
- missed-call revenue heatmap
- voicemail/intent inbox
- callback priority lane
- AI receptionist vs human answering cost card
- caller trust-risk checklist
- 7-day save-the-leads sprint
- owner-ready ROI memo

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop small-business lead triage product called Missed Call Money Map for local service owners who miss calls while working. The product should let users paste a call log, voicemail snippets, missed-call notes, rough average job value, and current answering costs, then turn that input into a callback priority map and a buy-vs-hire AI receptionist decision. Include a missed-call revenue heatmap, voicemail/intent inbox, callback priority lane, AI receptionist vs human answering cost card, caller trust-risk checklist, 7-day save-the-leads sprint, and owner-ready ROI memo. Design direction: use an operator-dense aesthetic with compact tables, high-signal badges, strong red/amber/green triage states, visible money-at-risk totals, and fast keyboard-like workflows so the owner feels the product is a revenue control room, not a generic CRM.
