# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- RSVP follow-ups are common and emotionally sensitive.
- Different relationships require different tone (family vs coworker).
- Short, clear asks reduce confusion.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users prefer 2–3 good templates over a blank-page writing UI.
- A simple "pushy risk" meter helps users choose safer copy.
- Korean/English toggle is useful for mixed guest groups.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users want much more personalization (venue, dress code, schedule) in the same message.
- Whether channel-specific templates need deeper differences (email subject lines, etc.).
- Which heuristics best match perceived pushiness across cultures.
