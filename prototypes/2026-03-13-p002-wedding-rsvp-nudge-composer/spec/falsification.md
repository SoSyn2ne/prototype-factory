# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users prefer writing their own messages and ignore templates entirely?
2. Does the pushiness meter feel "made up" and reduce trust?
3. Are relationship categories too coarse to be helpful (need closer mapping)?
4. Does bilingual output create more confusion than value for most weddings?
5. Would a spreadsheet + mail merge be the real solution for the target user?
6. Do guests react negatively to any follow-up, making copy quality irrelevant?
7. Is the highest pain actually tracking who needs a nudge (not writing the nudge)?
