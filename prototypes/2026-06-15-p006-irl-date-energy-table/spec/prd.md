Create a responsive playful web app prototype called "IRL Date Energy Table" for dating-app-fatigued singles, friend groups, singles-event hosts, matchmakers, bars, cafes, and community organizers. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with bold but readable social-game energy, expressive status stickers, lively contrast accents, and a polished portfolio-worthy first screen that feels fun rather than desperate. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working event tool.
