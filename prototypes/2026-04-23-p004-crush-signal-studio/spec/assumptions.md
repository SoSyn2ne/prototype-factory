# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- The appeal is emotional momentum and shareable confidence, not clinical relationship advice.
- Users want a playful tone that validates attraction without feeling sleazy or explicit.

## ASSUMPTION
- Scenario remixing will feel more engaging than static dating tips.
- A visibly game-like confidence meter will make the product portfolio-worthy and memorable.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users would treat this as a solo toy, a group chat game, or a real pre-date tool.
- How far the concept can lean into theatricality before it stops feeling useful.
