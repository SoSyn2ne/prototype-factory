# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Small hospitality teams often manage lead conversations through inboxes, spreadsheets, and quote documents.
- Quote speed and perceived polish both influence event booking conversion.
- Catering inquiries frequently stall on menu selection, budget fit, or venue logistics.

## ASSUMPTION
- A recovery-focused workspace will outperform generic CRM views for small premium caterers.
- Teams will value elegant follow-up packaging as much as raw pipeline metrics.
- Surfacing blocker type will help owners prioritize rescue actions more effectively than lead age alone.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether caterers have enough consistent lead volume to justify a specialized tool.
- How much automation users expect for quote drafting versus manual service touch.
- Whether venue coordination blockers are structured enough to standardize.
