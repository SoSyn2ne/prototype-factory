# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do most boutique caterers handle too few simultaneous leads to need a dedicated recovery desk?
2. Is generic CRM plus email already sufficient for this segment?
3. Are quote delays mostly caused by owner capacity, not information visibility?
4. Would teams resist entering blocker data because service businesses run too informally?
5. Does premium hospitality value bespoke conversation so much that templates feel harmful?
6. Are venue and menu blockers too varied to summarize cleanly in one interface?
