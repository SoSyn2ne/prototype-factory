# Assumptions — 심야 방송 등대

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: foreign residents, hard-of-hearing households, older residents, pet owners, and apartment managers.
- The observed workaround is: building text app, neighbor chat, management-office call, door peek, or guessing from tone.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: field-ops.
- Source references are: S03.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: ambient capture lights the puck, preserves verbatim audio locally, displays translation and uncertainty, and offers official confirmation actions.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: timestamped local transcript-and-action card with source and uncertainty.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: apartment management office, foreign-resident onboarding, relocation services, search phrase “아파트 방송 못 알아들음”.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: the household can share a timestamped verbatim card with neighbors while keeping audio local.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
