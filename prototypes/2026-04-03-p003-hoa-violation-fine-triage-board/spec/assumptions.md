# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Violation work is deadline-driven and operationally repetitive.
- Operators need prioritization and communication context more than full resident-history exports.
- Desktop-first review fits multi-property management teams.

## ASSUMPTION
- Fine exposure and escalation risk can be summarized into a reliable triage score.
- Teams will act sooner if the avoided-fines forecast is visible.
- A resolution drawer is more helpful than making users search through scattered notes.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users think by property, resident, or violation type first.
- Which signal best predicts escalation: deadline age, prior outreach, or violation category.
- How much communication history is necessary before the product feels trustworthy.
