# Clause Trap Scanner

A plain-Korean contract red-flag desk that helps renters, freelancers, and subscription buyers spot hidden fees, unfair exits, and liability traps before signing.

## Status
- Phase A scaffolded for Stitch export ingest.

## Candidate
- Type: Practical / Commercial
- Theme: 자산 방어
- Design profile: financial-analyst
- Target user: Korean renters, freelancers, gym members, and service buyers who are worried a contract or membership term will quietly cost them money later.
- Key UX: Paste or review a clause set, see ranked money-risk cards, compare exit options, and leave with a clean negotiation checklist instead of vague fear.
