# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- 2026 Korean comprehensive income tax has May 1-June 1 filing timing, with some June 30 edge cases.
- Freelancers search for simple-ledger forms, tutorials, and receipt organization help.
- Design profile is financial-analyst; theme is 자산 방어.

## ASSUMPTION
- A confidence-first binder will feel more useful than another filing tutorial.
- The first validation artifact can be a checklist/export without direct HomeTax integration.
- Korean freelancers will understand mixed Korean/English prototype labels if the core user language is Korean.

## UNKNOWN
- Which deduction categories create the most confusion for first users.
- Whether the first buyer is the freelancer or the accountant receiving messy records.
- Whether users prefer mobile receipt capture or desktop reconciliation first.
