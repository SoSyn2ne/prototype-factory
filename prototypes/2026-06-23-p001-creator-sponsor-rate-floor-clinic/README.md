# Creator Sponsor Rate Floor Clinic

Creators prove the minimum sponsor rate they can accept before algorithm fear turns into underpriced deals.

## Phase A
- Slot: p001
- Mix label: Practical / Commercial
- Design profile: operator-dense
- Theme: 자산 방어 / 역전 - creator pricing power under algorithm anxiety.
- Expected Stitch drop folder: `/home/sy/Downloads/stitch_drop/2026-06-23/p001/`

## Target user
Newsletter writers, YouTubers, niche creators, and solo media operators negotiating sponsor packages while audience growth feels unstable.

## Key UX
Creator enters audience size, engagement, production time, deliverables, niche proof, and recent distribution effort; the app calculates a rate floor, builds sponsor options, and produces a confidence memo.

## Required UI sections
- Audience proof header with reach, engagement, retention, and niche fit
- Deliverable cost board for newsletter, video, short, podcast, and bundle options
- Production time and opportunity-cost calculator
- Sponsor package ladder with floor, fair, and premium tiers
- Objection simulator for lowball offers and performance-risk pushback
- Distribution grind log showing daily promotion and guest-pitch effort
- Client-ready rate defense memo and invoice terms drawer
