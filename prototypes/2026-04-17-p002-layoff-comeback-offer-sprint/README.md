# Layoff Comeback Offer Sprint

A career rebound command center that helps recently laid-off professionals rebuild their story, target faster-paying roles, and sequence weekly comeback moves before savings run thin.

## Status
- prototype

## Concept snapshot
- Theme: 역전
- Design profile: operator-dense
- Target user: Recently laid-off mid-career workers who need an emotionally tolerable but brutally practical plan to secure their next offer within 30 to 60 days.
- Key UX: Turn layoff panic into a weekly comeback pipeline, showing which applications, narrative fixes, and interview drills are most likely to restore income fast.

## Required UI sections
- runway and urgency strip
- target role pipeline board
- story gap fixer panel
- weekly comeback sprint planner
- interview pressure tracker
- offer recovery plan drawer

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop job-recovery command center called Layoff Comeback Offer Sprint for recently laid-off professionals who need to restore income fast. It should help them see their financial runway, choose which target roles are most realistic, repair weak story gaps, and execute a weekly comeback sprint toward the next offer before confidence collapses. Include a runway and urgency strip, a target role pipeline board, a story gap fixer panel, a weekly comeback sprint planner, an interview pressure tracker, and an offer recovery plan drawer. Design direction: use an operator-dense aesthetic with dark slate structure, crisp white and fog surfaces, one focused electric blue action accent, and highly scannable momentum cues so the product feels urgent, disciplined, and comeback-oriented rather than motivational fluff.
