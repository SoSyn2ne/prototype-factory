# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Recent teacher community posts describe vague phone-ban rules, parent exception pressure, and uneven staff enforcement.
- Schools need low-friction operational views during the school day.

## ASSUMPTION
- School leaders will value a non-punitive consistency board more than another generic behavior tracker.
- Parent-message templates reduce escalation time.
- Staff buy-in gaps can be shown as operational risk without publicly shaming individual teachers.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether schools will enter exception data manually or need SIS/behavior-system integration.
- Whether legal/IEP exception handling requires jurisdiction-specific templates.
