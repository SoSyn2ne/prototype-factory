# Falsification checklist

1. Customers continue ordering at the same speed when the menu is fully hidden.
2. The price rail obscures or contradicts the main menu.
3. Staff cannot trigger the override with one touch during a rush.
4. Signal loss and advertisement takeover are confused.
5. The interruption receipt cannot support a conversation with the ad or signage team.
6. Field-ops controls are too small or low-contrast for counter use.
7. The concept expands into a generic signage dashboard.
