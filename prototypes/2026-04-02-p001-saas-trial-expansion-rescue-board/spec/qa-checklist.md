# QA checklist

## Happy path
- User lands on the rescue board and sees stalled but high-potential trial accounts ranked by forecast value.
- User opens one account and reviews activation friction, buyer signals, and recommended next action in one screen.
- User understands the likely forecast recovery after taking the suggested move.

## Edge cases
- Accounts with strong usage but no clear buyer identified.
- Accounts with many stakeholders but weak product activation.
- Very small accounts that score high on urgency but low on revenue impact.

## Acceptance verification
- Spec is clearly about PLG conversion rescue rather than generic CRM analytics.
- All required UI sections are explicitly documented.
- Design direction is present and consistent with the Stitch prompt.
