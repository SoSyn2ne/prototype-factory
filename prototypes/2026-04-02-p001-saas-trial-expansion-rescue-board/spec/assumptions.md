# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Trial conversion tends to concentrate in a small set of near-ready accounts.
- Revenue and growth teams need a short-listing workflow more than another raw analytics page.
- Desktop-first review is appropriate for operator workflows.

## ASSUMPTION
- Product activation gaps and stakeholder intent can be summarized into a trusted rescue score.
- Teams will take manual intervention if the account payoff is clear enough.
- A recommended next action is more valuable than exposing every underlying event log.

## UNKNOWN
- Which signal matters most for willingness to pay: product usage, stakeholder count, or contract timing.
- Whether users prefer account-based rescue or segment-based campaign planning.
- How much historical context the interface needs before it feels complete.
