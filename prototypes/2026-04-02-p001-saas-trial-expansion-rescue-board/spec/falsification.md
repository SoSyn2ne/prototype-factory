# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do growth teams already handle near-win trial rescue well enough inside existing CRM workflows?
2. Is the real bottleneck outbound capacity rather than account prioritization?
3. Are activation-friction signals too noisy to trust at the individual-account level?
4. Would teams prefer automated nurture recommendations over an operator dashboard?
5. Is expansion intent usually decided too late for a same-week rescue workflow to matter?
6. Does the product only become valuable with deep product-analytics integrations?
7. Would finance leaders value forecast reporting more than intervention UX?
