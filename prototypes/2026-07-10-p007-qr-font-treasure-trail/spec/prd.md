# PRD — QR Font Treasure Trail

## Problem
- A printable party and venue toy that turns plain text into no-image QR clue trails for cafes, classrooms, and popups.

## Target user
- Cafe owners, teachers, event hosts, and makers who want a low-tech interactive trail without building an app or printing image assets.

## Key UX
- Write clues, convert them into font-based QR cards, arrange a route, test scan order, and export printable cards plus a completion stamp.

## Required UI sections
  - Clue text stack
  - Font QR card preview
  - Trail route board
  - Scan-order tester
  - Prize/completion stamp
  - Printable card sheet
  - Venue setup checklist

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- Printable interaction / venue play

## Source signal references
- S4 (GeekNews / Hada front page, captured 2026-07-10)

## Output artifact
- printable QR clue trail pack

## Stitch prompt

```text
Create a playful printable web app called “QR Font Treasure Trail” for cafes, classrooms, and popup hosts making no-image QR clue trails. Show a clue text stack, font QR card preview, trail route board, scan-order tester, prize stamp, printable card sheet, and venue setup checklist. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with bright print-shop energy, tactile card previews, bold scan states, and a clear export-to-print workflow.
```

- Do not claim source signals prove market size. Treat them as problem evidence only.
