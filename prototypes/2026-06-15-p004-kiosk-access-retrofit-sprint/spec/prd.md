## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype called "Kiosk Access Retrofit Sprint" for Korean cafes, franchisees, quick-service restaurants, clinics, venue operators, kiosk vendors, and store managers preparing for kiosk accessibility requirements. The app should audit kiosk access gaps, compare retrofit versus replacement quotes, map staff-assistance fallback, and export a vendor-ready compliance sprint. Required sections: accessibility gap header, kiosk inventory cards, voice/tactile/height/screen-reader checklist, customer journey failure map, retrofit vs replacement quote table, staff-assistance fallback script, inspection and signage readiness lane, and vendor scope-of-work export. Use realistic sample data for a small cafe with two aging kiosks, one wheelchair-height issue, missing voice guidance, and a tight wage/rent budget. Design direction: use the field-ops profile with slate and steel operational surfaces, legible pressure-state panels, a restrained cyan action accent, and controls that feel usable during a store walkthrough. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working retrofit board.
