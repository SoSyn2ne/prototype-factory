# PRD - GLP-1 Comfort Meal Loop

## One-liner
A dose-week meal and symptom planner for people on GLP-1s who need tiny, tolerable defaults instead of another diet dashboard.

## Candidate type
Practical / Commercial

## Problem
The user selects dose day, appetite level, side effects, hydration, constipation risk, and food aversions; the app suggests simple protein-first meal defaults, flags provider-contact moments, and exports a symptom-and-meal summary for the next appointment.

## Target user
GLP-1 users, telehealth weight-care clinics, dietitians, pharmacists, and caregivers helping patients manage daily food tolerance.

## Key UX
The user selects dose day, appetite level, side effects, hydration, constipation risk, and food aversions; the app suggests simple protein-first meal defaults, flags provider-contact moments, and exports a symptom-and-meal summary for the next appointment.

## Required UI sections
- Dose-week status header with day since injection and comfort score
- Tiny meal default planner for breakfast, snack, meal, and emergency option
- Nausea, constipation, fatigue, hydration, and injection-site symptom check-in
- Food aversion and safe-repeat list
- Grocery mini-list for low-effort staples
- Provider-contact flag panel and non-emergency disclaimer
- Appointment summary export
- Trend strip for appetite, energy, and tolerance
