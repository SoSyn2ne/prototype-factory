# Storm Claim Deductible Desk

A homeowner decision console that compares insurance claim math, contractor timing, and out-of-pocket risk before storm damage turns into a bad financial move.

## Status
- prototype

## Theme
- 자산 방어

## Design profile
- financial-analyst

## Key UX
- Compare claim-versus-cash scenarios, see deductible pain against future premium risk, and leave with one defensible next move before contractors or adjusters control the story.

## Required UI sections
- damage cost snapshot
- claim-versus-cash comparison matrix
- deductible and premium risk panel
- contractor timing board
- evidence checklist
- recommended move drawer

## Stitch prompt
```
Design a desktop homeowner decision console called Storm Claim Deductible Desk for people dealing with roof, siding, or water damage right after a storm. It should help them compare insurance claim math, deductible pain, contractor timing, evidence readiness, and likely out-of-pocket exposure before they choose whether to file a claim or pay cash. Include a damage cost snapshot, a claim-versus-cash comparison matrix, a deductible and premium risk panel, a contractor timing board, an evidence checklist, and a recommended move drawer. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with deep navy structure, off-white data surfaces, muted green and restrained amber cues, and premium analytical hierarchy so the product feels fiduciary, calm, and protective under financial stress.
```
