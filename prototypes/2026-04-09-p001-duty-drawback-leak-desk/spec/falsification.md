# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do operators already recover nearly all eligible refund cases with existing broker workflows?
2. Is the missing paperwork rate so high that prioritization does not materially improve recovered cash?
3. Would customs-rule variance make a single queue misleading without heavy localization?
4. Are users unable to trust estimated recoverable cash without line-level audit trails in the first screen?
5. Is the true bottleneck broker turnaround time rather than identifying good cases?
6. Would a spreadsheet plus a broker email template solve the same problem well enough?
7. Are the highest-value cases too infrequent for users to open this product weekly?
