# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Users do not trust a close-probability score without transparent factors.
2. Teams need technician availability data before taking action.
3. Quote priority is driven more by geographic routing than deal value.
4. Financing friction is not a common enough blocker to deserve a dedicated panel.
5. Managers prefer a CRM export over a focused triage dashboard.
6. Weekly forecast context distracts from same-day follow-up decisions.
7. The next-action drawer is too prescriptive for real sales workflows.
