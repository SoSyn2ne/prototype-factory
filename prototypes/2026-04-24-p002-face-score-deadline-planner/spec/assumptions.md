# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Appearance anxiety spikes around interviews, dates, reunions, and formal events.
- Users already track beauty tasks informally in notes, carts, screenshots, and salon bookings.

## ASSUMPTION
- A payoff-ranked routine ladder will reduce panic spending better than generic beauty content feeds.
- Users will trust a planning interface that feels polished and editorial rather than clinical.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether users want strict cost tracking or mostly reassurance and prioritization.
- How much personalization is needed across skin types and styling goals in the first version.
