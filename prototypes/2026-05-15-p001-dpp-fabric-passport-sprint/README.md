# DPP Fabric Passport Sprint

A compliance prep desk that helps small fashion brands turn supplier scraps into EU Digital Product Passport fields before 2027 pressure hits.

## Status
- phase-a

## Candidate type
- Practical / Commercial

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Theme
- 자산 방어

## Target user
Small fashion labels, textile printers, boutique apparel importers, merch brands, and marketplace sellers that need EU-ready product data but do not have a sustainability/compliance team.

## Key UX
Drop supplier PDFs, bill-of-material notes, fabric composition, dye/ink details, country-of-origin, care labels, and packaging data; the product maps missing DPP fields, creates a supplier chase list, shows EU channel risk by SKU, and drafts QR-passport copy for launch review.

## Required UI sections
- supplier evidence intake
- SKU passport completeness grid
- materials and origin field mapper
- missing-data supplier chase queue
- EU channel block-risk meter
- QR passport preview
- audit trail and launch approval memo

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop compliance-operations product called DPP Fabric Passport Sprint for small fashion labels, textile printers, boutique apparel importers, merch brands, and marketplace sellers preparing for EU Digital Product Passport requirements. Users should drop supplier PDFs, bill-of-material notes, fabric composition, dye/ink details, country-of-origin, care labels, and packaging data; the app maps missing DPP fields, creates a supplier chase list, shows EU channel risk by SKU, and drafts QR-passport copy for launch review. Include a supplier evidence intake, SKU passport completeness grid, materials and origin field mapper, missing-data supplier chase queue, EU channel block-risk meter, QR passport preview, and audit trail plus launch approval memo. Design direction: use an operator-dense aesthetic with compact tables, slate and deep navy surfaces, one amber compliance accent, crisp evidence badges, and a no-nonsense factory-console hierarchy that feels like a SKU war room rather than a sustainability brochure.
