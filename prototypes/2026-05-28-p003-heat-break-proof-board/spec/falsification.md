# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Supervisors already use required safety apps from insurers or general contractors.
2. Break logging is seen as worker surveillance and reduces adoption.
3. Heat rules vary too much by location for a generic proof board.
4. Paper logs are faster for small crews than a mobile web workflow.
5. Documentation does not materially help citation reduction or insurance conversations.
6. Field conditions make phone use impractical during hot shifts.
7. The prototype cannot balance urgency with calm proof-building.
