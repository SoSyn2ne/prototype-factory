# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Cleaning operators already manage renewals well enough through spreadsheets and account reviews.
2. Renewal risk is driven more by pricing negotiations than service health.
3. Staffing instability data is too noisy to guide action.
4. Retained ARR is not the metric operators use in daily decisions.
5. A timeline view hides too much location-specific detail.
6. Save-action recommendations vary too much to standardize.
7. Account managers need client communication history more than labor and margin context.
