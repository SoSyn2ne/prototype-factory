# QA checklist

## Happy path
- At-risk queue clearly orders contracts by renewal urgency and value.
- Selected account view connects quality, staffing, and margin signals coherently.
- KPI hero and retained-ARR rail support the same retention story.

## Edge cases
- Low-data accounts still render meaningful fallback states.
- Multiple sites under one contract remain readable.
- Service incidents and margin issues do not visually compete.

## Acceptance verification
- All required UI sections appear in filled metadata and spec docs.
- No scaffold placeholders remain.
- Demo is still a stub, not a custom-built implementation.
