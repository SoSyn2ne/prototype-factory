# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Attendance drop, unused classes, and payment friction are strong churn-warning signals in membership businesses.
- Operators want an intervention queue tied to revenue impact, not just a list of inactive members.
- Coach notes matter because save actions work better when outreach feels context-aware.

## ASSUMPTION
- A daily queue with recommended actions will outperform generic monthly churn reporting.
- Staff can manually execute save actions if the system clearly explains why each member is at risk.
- Showing recovered revenue will make the dashboard legible to owners, not just front-desk staff.

## UNKNOWN
- Which signal weights vary most by membership type, PT package structure, or local gym culture.
- Whether members respond better to pricing saves, class nudges, or coach outreach in this segment.
- How much historical data is needed before operators trust the risk ranking.
