# PRD — Shift Interview Heatmap

## Problem
An hourly-hiring cockpit that predicts applicant no-show risk and tells managers who needs a text, call, alternate slot, or same-day offer.

## Target user
Restaurant, QSR, retail, and franchise managers hiring hourly workers under staffing pressure.

## Key UX
The manager sees every candidate by shift need, distance, response speed, interview time, no-show risk, and next nudge; one tap sends the right reminder or reschedule offer.

## Required UI sections
- Staffing gap strip by role, shift, and store.
- Candidate heatmap with risk, distance, availability match, and reply velocity.
- Interview calendar with no-show probability and backup candidates.
- Nudge composer for SMS, call, alternate slot, and document reminder.
- Same-day offer readiness checklist.
- Source quality report by job board/referral/channel.
- Manager handoff notes for shift leads.

## Assigned design profile
- operator-dense

## Theme

## Source signal references

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive B2B web app prototype called "Shift Interview Heatmap" for restaurant and retail managers hiring hourly workers. Show the active hiring cockpit first. Include a staffing gap strip, candidate risk heatmap, interview calendar with no-show probability, nudge composer, same-day offer readiness checklist, source quality report, and manager handoff notes. Use realistic QSR sample data across cashier, cook, shift lead, and delivery prep roles. Design direction: use the operator-dense profile with compact operational tables, crisp status badges, one sharp accent, 40px controls, and no decorative hero sections.
