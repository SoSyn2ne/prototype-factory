# PRD — Neighborhood Vacancy Pulse

## Problem
Turn a block walk into a dated sequence of storefront openings, closures, and gaps.

## Target user
Korean would-be shop owners, landlords, business groups, and local reporters.

## Core loop / key UX
Capture storefront status with privacy crops and replay changes along the physical street.

## First screen
Make the core action and its evidence immediately understandable in three seconds.

## Required UI sections
walk recorder; storefront capture; privacy crop; street sequence; timeline; gap hypotheses; block brief.

## Design
- Assigned profile: `calm-consumer`
- Theme: 지역 상권

## Source signals
S6, S12 from `daily/2026-09-02/ideas.md`.

## Non-goals
- No production integrations or custom demo in Phase A.

## QA / screenshot criteria
Mobile capture protects faces and plates; stale, duplicate, temporary, and conflicting states are visible.
