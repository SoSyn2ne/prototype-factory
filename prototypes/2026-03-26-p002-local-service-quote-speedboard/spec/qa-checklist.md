# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Open the board and identify which new lead should be quoted first.
- Build a fast first-pass estimate with labor, materials, and optional upsell lines.
- Review send readiness and understand expected revenue before sending the quote.

## Edge cases
- Leads with missing photos or unclear scope should remain actionable without blocking the queue.
- Emergency jobs need strong urgency treatment without overwhelming normal leads.
- Low-ticket jobs should not dominate visual attention when larger quotes are pending.

## Acceptance verification
- All required sections from today's idea are represented in the spec.
- Scaffolded files contain actual product language rather than blank checklist items.
- The Stitch prompt is directly reusable and aligned with the target user/problem.
