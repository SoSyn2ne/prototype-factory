# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Fast response time is a key conversion lever in local service businesses.
- Users need lightweight quoting help before rolling into a full CRM or dispatch suite.
- A desktop quote-prep board can combine inbox urgency and estimate assembly in one view.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users are comfortable with estimated ranges rather than exact parts pricing on first response.
- Neighborhood and urgency cues matter enough to prioritize the queue visually.
- Win probability framing is useful even when based on simple heuristics.

## UNKNOWN
- How much map context is needed before the screen feels too busy.
- Whether trade-specific terminology should be generalized or customized per service type.
- What level of quote detail users expect before sending a first-pass estimate.
