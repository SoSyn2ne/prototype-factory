# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. The first screen feels like a marketing page instead of the working app.
2. The prototype reduces the idea to a generic dashboard/board/planner without the unique mechanic: Operator chooses cost shocks by ingredient, sees margin and review backlash scenarios, then generates menu changes, staff talking points, and a price-test plan.
3. Required sections are missing: Cost shock header for coffee, beef, beer, labor, and shipping; Menu item margin table with old price, new cost, target margin, and guest sensitivity; Backlash simulator showing review risk, reorder risk, and perceived value; Portion/ingredient swap board for shrink, bundle, premiumize, or hold price; Staff script composer for explaining price changes without sounding defensive; A/B test calendar for lunch, dinner, delivery, and happy-hour segments; Owner decision memo with top three price moves and watch metrics.
4. The design does not match the operator-dense profile.
5. Source signals S3 are not visible in the problem framing or sample data.
6. The UI lacks concrete sample data and therefore cannot be judged quickly.
7. The output cannot fit the pNNN drop contract with code.html and screen.png.
