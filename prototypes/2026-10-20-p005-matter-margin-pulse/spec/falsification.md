1. Partners still need manual timers to trust the margin signal.
2. Duplicate events inflate the scope ruler and cannot be corrected.
3. Source confidence is not understood.
4. The client card exposes internal cost or privileged notes.
5. A scope conversation starts no earlier than the current month-end process.
6. Financial-analyst styling implies fake precision.
7. The core turns into another generic matter-management dashboard.
