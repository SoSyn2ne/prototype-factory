# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Receipt scanning is slower than manually reading the receipt.
2. The fairness score feels arbitrary or accusatory.
3. Users do not want to share fee scorecards.
4. The app cannot explain advertised-vs-final deltas clearly.
5. Dynamic-pricing examples are too broad for one workflow.
6. The playful visual style makes fee details less legible.
7. The concept overlaps too much with generic budgeting apps.
