# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Recent policy signals point to more importer-of-record requirements, certifications, and ownership/control monitoring.
- Small importers often coordinate across suppliers, brokers, and internal finance leads.

## ASSUMPTION
- A binder-style readiness interface is easier to sell than a full compliance system.
- SKU/shipment risk queues can make compliance work feel urgent and concrete.
- Founder/CFO memo generation is useful for small operators who need signoff.

## UNKNOWN
- How often data changes enough to justify alerts.
- Whether customs brokers or importers are the better first buyer.
