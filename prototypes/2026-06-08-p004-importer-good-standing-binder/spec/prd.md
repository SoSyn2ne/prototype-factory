# PRD - Importer Good-Standing Binder

## Problem
- Small importers face tighter importer-of-record scrutiny, supplier documentation demands, bond exposure, and disclosure obligations without a compact readiness workbench.

## Target user
- Small ecommerce importers, customs brokers, 3PL operators, and compliance leads preparing for tighter importer-of-record scrutiny.

## Key UX
- A compliance binder that turns messy supplier, bond, ownership, shipment, and certification data into a readiness score and next-action queue.

## Required UI sections
- IOR good-standing checklist.
- Shipment risk queue.
- Disclosure and certification tracker.
- Supplier ownership/control watchlist.
- Broker task handoff.
- De minimis transition timeline and SKU impact table.
- Executive readiness memo generator.

## Design profile
- financial-analyst: institutional workbench, premium data-forward comparisons, muted confidence and warning cues.

- 자산 방어: preventing customs holds, surprise bonds, duty exposure, and compliance-driven cash lockups.

## Stitch prompt
Build an institutional compliance web app prototype called "Importer Good-Standing Binder" for small importers preparing for tighter customs and importer-of-record scrutiny. The main screen is an analytical workbench with an IOR good-standing checklist, shipment risk queue, disclosure/certification tracker, supplier ownership watchlist, broker task handoff, de minimis transition timeline, SKU impact table, and a readiness memo generator. Use realistic sample rows for apparel, electronics accessories, beauty goods, HS codes, country flags, bond exposure, missing supplier certification, and founder signoff. Design direction: use the financial-analyst profile with ink/graphite/off-white surfaces, muted green confidence cues, restrained warning tones, and premium data-forward comparisons. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.

## Non-goals
- Legal advice.
- Full customs brokerage platform.
- Automated duty filing.

## Success metrics
- User can see missing IOR proof and high-risk shipments immediately.
- Broker handoff tasks have owners and due dates.
- Readiness memo can be generated from current risk state.
