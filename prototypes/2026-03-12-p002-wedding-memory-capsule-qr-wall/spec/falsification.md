# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. If guests scan the QR before the page has ever loaded on that device and the venue has no usable internet, does the experience still start quickly enough to matter?
2. Do couples actually want a public message wall during the reception, or do they mainly want a private digital guestbook viewed after the event?
3. Is one moderator enough, or does the approval queue become a distraction during peak moments like speeches, dinner service, and dancing?
4. Are guests comfortable sharing a message on a semi-public screen when their name, relation, or photo memory might be visible to other tables?
5. Does local-only storage break the concept because each table or phone becomes an isolated island instead of contributing to one shared keepsake?
6. Is JSON export useful to planners and couples in practice, or do they immediately ask for a prettier output such as PDF, slideshow, or print-ready album pages?
7. On older iPhones and budget Android devices, do localStorage persistence and file download work reliably enough for a wedding-night workflow?
