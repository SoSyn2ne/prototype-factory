# PRD — Supplier Rebate Missed-Claim Console

## Problem
- Brands often earn supplier rebates but fail to claim them because program rules, thresholds, and filing proof live across email, ERP exports, and distributor portals.
- High-value rebate windows quietly expire while teams chase lower-impact admin work.

## Target user
- Brand operators, wholesale finance managers, and ecommerce operations teams managing vendor rebate programs.

## Constraints
- Time: A finance or ops lead should understand today's best claim opportunities in under 10 minutes.
- Budget: The concept must work before direct integrations exist, using imported operational data.
- Platform: Desktop-first internal tool used in weekly margin review and month-end close workflows.

## Non-goals
- Automated rebate submission to supplier portals.
- Full contract parsing or legal review of rebate terms.
- General ledger reconciliation across all finance systems in phase one.

## Success metrics
- Users can rank rebate opportunities by payout, deadline pressure, and documentation readiness in one session.
- Teams can spot missing proof before claims expire.
- The interface makes recoverable cash obvious enough to drive weekly action.

## Notes
- Design profile: financial-analyst.
- Core UI sections: rebate dollars-at-risk KPI strip, supplier program priority table, threshold progress chart, missing-proof checklist panel, claim deadline timeline, action drawer with owner assignment.
