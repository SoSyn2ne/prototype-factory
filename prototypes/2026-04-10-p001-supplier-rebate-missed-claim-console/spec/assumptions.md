# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Supplier rebate programs often combine threshold logic, claim windows, and proof requirements.
- Operators care more about net recoverable dollars than raw purchase volume.
- Claim delay usually comes from missing proof or unclear ownership, not lack of opportunity.

## ASSUMPTION
- A single prioritized rebate queue will outperform spreadsheet-based monthly review.
- Operators will manually resolve proof gaps if the likely payout is obvious.
- Deadline visibility tied to payout size will improve claim completion.

## UNKNOWN
- How standardized supplier rebate rule structures are across small and mid-sized brands.
- Whether teams trust estimated payout calculations without audited back-office data.
- How much manual data cleanup is acceptable before the product feels burdensome.
