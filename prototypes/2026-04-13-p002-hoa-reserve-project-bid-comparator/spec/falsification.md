# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do most HOA boards make too few capital decisions per year to adopt a dedicated comparison tool?
2. Are reserve studies and contractor bids too inconsistent for reliable normalized scoring?
3. Does the board still need full consultant interpretation, limiting product value?
4. Is resident disruption too qualitative to represent credibly in a dashboard?
5. Would community managers resist duplicating data from existing association software?
6. Is budget approval driven more by politics than by clearer analysis?
