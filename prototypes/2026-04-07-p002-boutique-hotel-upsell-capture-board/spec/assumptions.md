# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Room upgrades and add-ons are easier to sell when timed before arrival.
- Reservation context and guest history affect conversion likelihood.
- Staff need a concise opportunity queue rather than a long arrivals list.

## ASSUMPTION
- A premium but data-clear desktop UI will fit boutique hotel operators better than a generic revenue dashboard.
- Teams can act effectively with guest signals and recommended offers on one screen.
- Daily captured-revenue framing will help justify use by managers.

## UNKNOWN
- Which pre-arrival signals matter most across different hotel sizes.
- Whether teams want segment-based offers or fully manual offer composition.
- How much guest-experience staff will trust upsell scoring without explanation.
