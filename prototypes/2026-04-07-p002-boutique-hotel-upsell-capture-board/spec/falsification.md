# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Hotels already handle upsells well in their PMS or CRM tools.
2. Staff prefer manual guest judgment over ranked opportunity scoring.
3. Offer timing matters less than price or package design.
4. Premium visual styling reduces operational scan speed.
5. Add-ons and upgrades need availability logic to be credible.
6. Daily revenue framing is less persuasive than occupancy or ADR metrics.
7. The action drawer oversimplifies how guest teams coordinate offers.
