# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. The target user cannot recognize their trigger moment in the first screen: the buyer realizes the ticket may be overpriced, duplicated, delayed, or fake.
2. The core interaction feels like a generic dashboard instead of gate check ritual.
3. The assigned design profile (playful-experimental) is not visible in layout, density, palette, and interaction tone.
4. The source signals only describe curiosity, not existing workaround behavior.
5. The required UI sections do not produce a useful artifact for the user: packet, pass, ledger, receipt, or decision sheet.
6. The product crosses into authoritative legal, medical, tax, immigration, or financial advice rather than organizing facts and next questions.
7. The Stitch output hides the pressure theme (자산 방어) behind bland SaaS styling.
