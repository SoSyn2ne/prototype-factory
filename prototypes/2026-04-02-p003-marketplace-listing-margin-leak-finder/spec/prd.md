# PRD — Marketplace Listing Margin Leak Finder

## One-liner
An ecommerce merchandising desk that flags listings losing profit through coupon stacking, ad overspend, and fee drag before the week closes.

## Problem
- Marketplace operators often notice listing-level margin damage only after weekly finance reviews.
- Promo discounts, ad spend, fees, and return pressure live in separate tools, hiding the true leak driver.
- Teams need a triage desk that ranks fixable losses, not another retrospective performance report.

## Target user
- Primary: marketplace operators, ecommerce analysts, and brand-side merchandising managers.
- Secondary: founder-led ecommerce teams protecting contribution margin.

## Why now
- Paid marketplace growth is increasingly sensitive to fee and ad inflation.
- Brands rely on promos more aggressively, which makes leakage harder to see.
- Operators need tighter weekly control loops to protect cash generation.

## Key UX
- Rank listings by margin leakage and focus on the biggest recovery opportunities first.
- Open one SKU cluster and see exactly how coupons, ads, fees, and returns interact.
- Compare corrective moves and choose the highest-impact fix without leaving the screen.

## Required UI sections
- Margin leak hero
- Listing watchlist table
- Leakage driver breakdown
- Promo vs ads comparison panel
- Fix recommendation drawer
- Weekly profit recovery rail

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no custom demo implementation.
- Budget: should feel viable for small brand-side marketplace teams.
- Platform: desktop ecommerce operations dashboard.

## Non-goals
- Real-time marketplace API sync.
- Full pricing automation.
- Order-level forensic accounting.

## Success metrics
- Increase weekly recovered contribution margin.
- Reduce time spent diagnosing underperforming listings.
- Improve intervention rate on fixable promo and spend leaks.

## Stitch-ready design direction
Use a high-contrast commerce-ops visual style with graphite, off-white, and vivid coral accents; keep hierarchy decisive, tables and comparison cards extremely legible, and the overall product feel commercial, profit-minded, and action-oriented.
