Notice of Adverse Benefit Determination

Dear Dr. Jenkins,

We have received your request for prior authorization for the following services:

  • CPT 22551: Arthrodesis, anterior interbody, including disc space preparation, discectomy, osteophytectomy and decompression of spinal cord and/or nerve roots; cervical below C2.
  • Diagnosis: M50.12 - Cervical disc disorder with radiculopathy, mid-cervical region.

After careful review of the clinical documentation submitted by a Medical Director in the same specialty, we have determined that the requested service does not meet the medical necessity criteria outlined in our commercial policy guidelines (Policy ID: SP-0023).

REASON FOR DENIAL

Specifically, the documentation fails to demonstrate:

  • Failure of conservative therapy. The records do not show completion of at least 6 weeks of structured physical therapy within the last 6 months.
  • Current imaging confirming severe central canal stenosis or high-grade foraminal narrowing correlating with the reported radicular symptoms. The submitted MRI report from 02/2022 is outdated.

You have the right to appeal this decision. Please submit relevant clinical records, progress notes, and current imaging reports that address the specific criteria gaps identified above within 180 days of this notice.
