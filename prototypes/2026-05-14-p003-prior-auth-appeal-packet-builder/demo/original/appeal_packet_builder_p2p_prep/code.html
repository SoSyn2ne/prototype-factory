I am writing on behalf of my patient, John Doe, to formally appeal the denial of coverage for the requested Lumbar Spinal Fusion (CPT 22630). As detailed in Exhibit B (Physical Therapy Flowsheets), the patient attended 24 sessions focusing on core stabilization and pain management, concluding on September 30, 2023, with no functional improvement noted.
