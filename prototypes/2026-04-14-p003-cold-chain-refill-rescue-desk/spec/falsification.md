# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do existing specialty pharmacy systems already cover refill rescue well enough?
2. Is patient contact quality a staffing issue rather than a workflow visibility issue?
3. Are cold-storage confirmations too unreliable or informal to structure cleanly in software?
4. Would teams need outbound communication tools embedded before they adopt the dashboard?
5. Is the route map visually appealing but operationally low value compared with a tighter case queue?
6. Are failed deliveries too infrequent in a given clinic to justify a dedicated product?
7. Does the concept break if shipment timing changes multiple times per day?
