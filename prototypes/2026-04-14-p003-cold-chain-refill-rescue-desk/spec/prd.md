# PRD — Cold-Chain Refill Rescue Desk

## Problem
- Temperature-sensitive medication refills fail when shipment windows, patient contact, and home storage readiness are managed in separate systems or informal checklists.
- Care teams discover risk too late, after a patient misses delivery, cannot receive the package, or cannot confirm proper cold storage.

## Target user
- Specialty pharmacy coordinators, infusion clinic staff, and care teams managing temperature-sensitive therapies.

## Constraints
- Time: Staff should triage the day's highest-risk refill cases in under 10 minutes.
- Budget: The concept should work with imported shipment data and manual contact updates before deeper logistics integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first operational desk used by coordinators during refill planning windows.

## Non-goals
- Route optimization for the carrier itself.
- Clinical assessment of medication suitability.
- Replacing the pharmacy management system or patient chart.

## Success metrics
- Teams can identify refill cases where timing, contact, or storage confirmation puts delivery at risk.
- Users can open one patient lane and see the exact missing operational step immediately.
- The interface reduces same-day scramble by making intervention ownership explicit.

## Notes
- Theme: 건강.
- Design profile: field-ops.
- Core UI sections: at-risk refill KPI row, shipping-window queue, patient contact timeline, cold-storage confirmation checklist, route risk map, intervention assignment drawer.
