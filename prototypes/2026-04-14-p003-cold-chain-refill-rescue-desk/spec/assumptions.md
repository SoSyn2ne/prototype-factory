# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Temperature-sensitive therapies have narrow delivery and storage constraints.
- Patient contact failure is a major operational risk for successful refill delivery.
- Coordinators need a single screen that combines logistics and patient-readiness signals.

## ASSUMPTION
- Shipping-window pressure, therapy value, and storage confirmation are enough to drive useful prioritization.
- Teams will accept a dedicated refill-rescue desk if it lowers failed-delivery incidents.
- Map-based route context adds confidence for intervention planning.

## UNKNOWN
- Which data source is easiest to integrate first: carrier feeds, pharmacy system exports, or CRM tasks.
- Whether clinics require SMS or call tooling inside the same surface.
- How often weather or regional disruption data materially changes workflow decisions.
