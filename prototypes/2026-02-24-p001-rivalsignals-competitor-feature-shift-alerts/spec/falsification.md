# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1) **Alert relevance fails:** In a 1-week pilot, the user marks >60% of alerts as “irrelevant / no action,” even after tuning thresholds.

2) **Critical misses happen:** A meaningful competitor move occurs (pricing change, major integration, packaging shift) and RivalSignals does not alert within 24h, *despite the source being public*.

3) **Impact scoring is not better than baseline:** Compared to plain RSS/changelog alerts, RivalSignals does not reduce total alerts while maintaining (or improving) perceived usefulness.

4) **Inputs are too sparse/unclear:** For >50% of monitored updates, the system cannot extract a concrete “what changed” statement (too vague, too marketing-y), making triage unreliable.

5) **Personalization doesn’t work:** Even with a clear ICP/thesis provided, users report the “why it matters” rationale is generic and not meaningfully tailored.

6) **Operational burden is too high:** Source maintenance (broken pages, format changes) requires >30 minutes/week per 10 competitors to keep monitoring working.

7) **No willingness to pay / low retention:** After a 2-week pilot, users would not pay at least $19/mo (or churn immediately) because perceived value is too low.
