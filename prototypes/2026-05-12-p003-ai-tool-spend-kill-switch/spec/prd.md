# PRD — AI Tool Spend Kill Switch

## Problem
- A ruthless AI subscription audit board that decides which tools earn their seat in a small-business workflow.
- Search/community evidence suggests this is a live pain, not a generic dashboard rename.

## Target user
- Local business owners, solo founders, agencies, coaches, creators, and small teams paying for too many AI tools without proof of saved time or revenue.

## Key UX
- List AI tools, monthly prices, owners, actual workflows, usage proof, time saved, quality risk, replacement options, and renewal dates; score keep/kill/consolidate decisions, schedule cancellation windows, and create a before/after operating stack.

## Required UI sections
- AI stack inventory
- monthly spend waterfall
- workflow proof board
- usage-versus-promise scorecard
- keep/kill/consolidate decision queue
- renewal cancellation calendar
- lean stack export

## Design profile
- `financial-analyst` — reflected in the Stitch prompt and visual direction.

## Stitch prompt
Design an analytical small-business finance product called AI Tool Spend Kill Switch for local business owners, solo founders, agencies, coaches, creators, and small teams paying for too many AI tools without proof of saved time or revenue. Users should list AI tools, monthly prices, owners, actual workflows, usage proof, time saved, quality risk, replacement options, and renewal dates, then score keep/kill/consolidate decisions, schedule cancellation windows, and create a before/after operating stack. Include an AI stack inventory, monthly spend waterfall, workflow proof board, usage-versus-promise scorecard, keep/kill/consolidate decision queue, renewal cancellation calendar, and lean stack export. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with ink-and-muted-green charts, sober ROI tiles, confidence bands, spend waterfalls, and decision memos so the app feels like a disciplined cost-cutting workbench for AI sprawl, not another shiny AI tool directory.

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A spec/scaffold only; do not implement custom demo yet.
- Budget: Use Stitch export workflow for Phase B.
- Platform: Web prototype, desktop-first unless consumer flow benefits from responsive cards.

## Non-goals
- Do not claim legal, medical, tax, or financial certainty.
- Do not integrate live paid APIs in Phase A.

## Success metrics
- A user understands the painful trigger within 5 seconds.
- The main workflow is clear from intake to export/share/action.
- The UI can be built from the Stitch prompt without additional explanation.
