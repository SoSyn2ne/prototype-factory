# PRD — Office Camouflage Photo Hunt

## Problem / target
Remote teams want a one-minute no-install social game with direct manipulation and funny failure. Target: teams, classrooms, streamers, and friend groups.

## Core UX
Daily safe room photo → choose silhouette → sample/paint colors → hide → 20-second seek → tap heatmap/miss replay → mini-league. Sections: seed, tray, paint canvas, timer, reveal, replay, league.

## Design / theme / evidence
- Profile: `playful-experimental`; theme: 외로움/사회적 놀이.
- Sources: S14 https://store.steampowered.com/app/4704690/MECCHA_CHAMELEON/; S11 https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49212848.
- QA: rule understood in 15 seconds; round under one minute; built-in assets only; funny miss is shareable without exposing user photos.

## Stitch prompt
Design a responsive playful web game named “Office Camouflage Photo Hunt.” Use a safe built-in office photo as the daily shared seed. In Hide mode, the player chooses one of six office-object silhouettes, drags it onto the scene, samples nearby colors with a chunky eyedropper, and paints it to blend in. In Seek mode, friends get a 20-second countdown and one tap; reveal a tap heatmap and a short funny miss replay, then update a tiny team mini-league. Show hide, seek, reveal, and daily-complete states. Design direction: follow the playful-experimental profile with bold cobalt/lime, tactile paint controls, oversized countdown type, lively but readable cards, and celebratory micro-motion only at reveal.
