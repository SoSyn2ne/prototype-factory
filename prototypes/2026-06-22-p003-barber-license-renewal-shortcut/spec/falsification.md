# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Reject if users cannot tell old steps from new steps within 10 seconds.
2. Test whether the renewal packet feels calmer than a state portal.
3. Verify the prototype does not imply official filing.
4. Confirm expiry and business-interruption risk are visible.
5. Reject if the flow requires too much legal reading.
6. Check mobile readability for solo workers.
7. Validate that source signal is policy-driven, not a generic checklist clone.
