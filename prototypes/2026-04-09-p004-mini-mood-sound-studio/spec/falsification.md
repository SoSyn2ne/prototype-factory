# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Are existing ambient tools already good enough at audio coordination for the target user?
2. Is vacancy delay driven mostly by market demand rather than operational lag, reducing product value?
3. Would teams refuse to maintain another board because vendors still communicate by phone and text?
4. Are audio workflows too varied across portfolios to support a coherent default information architecture?
5. Does move-in readiness require too much visual proof to express effectively in a lightweight phase-one product?
6. Is the real bottleneck vendor availability, not coordination quality?
7. Would an owner only care about weekly summaries, making a daily scheduler unnecessary?
