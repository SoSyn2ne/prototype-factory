# PRD — Mini Mood Sound Studio

## Problem
- People who want a quick focus ritual often bounce between separate ambient sound apps, playlist tabs, and aesthetic inspiration boards instead of landing in a single playful tool.
- The result is too much setup friction for a tiny emotional reward, so the mood-making ritual gets skipped entirely.

## Target user
- Remote workers, students, and cozy desk-setup fans.

## Constraints
- Time: A new user should be able to assemble and preview a mood in under 60 seconds.
- Budget: The product should feel delightful and expressive before any audio-engine complexity exists.
- Platform: Desktop-first consumer-style productivity toy for daily focus rituals.

## Non-goals
- Professional DAW-grade audio editing.
- Streaming music catalog integration.
- Team collaboration or real-time multiplayer sessions.

## Success metrics
- Users can build and preview a satisfying mood scene in under one minute.
- Users can understand sound layers and scene objects without explanation.
- The interface feels charming enough that a finished mood feels worth saving or sharing.

## Notes
- Design profile: playful-experimental.
- Core UI sections: mood-scene hero, sound-layer mixer, room object picker bar, session preview canvas, saved-scene carousel, share action panel.
