# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Layoff fear is rising even among workers who still appear stable on paper.
- Households need to compare timing scenarios, not just one static monthly budget.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users will value pre-crisis planning if the tool feels protective rather than doom-heavy.
- A partner-facing note area will help convert analysis into coordinated action at home.

## UNKNOWN
- What level of detail users will disclose about debt, severance, and side income in an early prototype.
- Whether users prefer layoff planning as a one-time drill or an always-on household dashboard.
