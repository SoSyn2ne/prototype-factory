# Layoff Buffer Action Map

A household cash defense workspace that helps salaried families stress-test layoffs, severance timing, debt pressure, and spending cuts before a job shock turns into a forced panic sale.

## Status
- prototype

## Theme
- 자산 방어

## Design profile
- financial-analyst

## Target user
- Dual-income households and mid-career workers who worry that one bad quarter, restructuring rumor, or surprise dismissal could wreck savings momentum.

## Key UX
- Simulate a layoff month in minutes, see how long the household can breathe, and leave with a ranked sequence of protective cash moves.

## Core UI sections
- household runway snapshot
- severance timing scenarios
- bill pressure waterfall
- cut-now versus cut-later board
- emergency cash move planner
- partner alignment notes drawer

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop household cash defense workspace called Layoff Buffer Action Map for salaried families worried about layoffs, severance delays, debt pressure, and protecting savings before panic hits. It should help users simulate a layoff month, compare runway outcomes, and choose the next protective cash moves without spiraling. Include a household runway snapshot, severance timing scenarios, a bill pressure waterfall, a cut-now versus cut-later board, an emergency cash move planner, and a partner alignment notes drawer. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with precise comparison panels, restrained navy and graphite tones, careful warning accents, and premium decision-workbench structure so the product feels rigorous, calm, and protective of household assets.
