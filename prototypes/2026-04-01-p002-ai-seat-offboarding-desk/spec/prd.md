# PRD — AI Seat Offboarding Desk

## One-liner
A security and IT operations workspace that closes former employees' AI tool access before data leaks through forgotten seats and shared workspaces.

## Problem
- Companies are adopting many AI SaaS tools faster than their offboarding processes can keep up.
- Departing employees may retain access to AI workspaces, uploaded files, browser-linked seats, or shared API projects.
- IT teams need a clear offboarding control center without checking each vendor console manually.

## Target user
- Primary: IT admins and operations managers at small-to-mid-sized companies.
- Secondary: compliance, security, or finance teams auditing SaaS seat hygiene.

## Why now
- AI tool sprawl is increasing across teams.
- Layoffs, contractor churn, and role changes increase offboarding volume.
- Audit and privacy risk rises when AI tools hold sensitive prompts, docs, and connectors.

## Key UX
- Triage all active departures in one queue.
- Identify which AI seats and shared spaces remain exposed.
- Complete revocation, transfer, export, and signoff from one checklist-driven flow.

## Required UI sections
- Offboarding risk hero
- Departing-user queue
- AI seat inventory panel
- Shared workspace exposure card
- Revocation checklist
- Audit-ready timeline

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A only; no live admin integrations.
- Budget: suitable for lean internal IT teams.
- Platform: desktop web admin workspace.

## Non-goals
- Full identity-provider replacement.
- Employee device management.
- Legal retention policy automation.

## Success metrics
- Reduce unrevoked AI seats after employee departure.
- Cut manual admin-console checks per offboarding event.
- Improve audit completeness for access-revocation evidence.

## Stitch-ready design direction
Use a sober security-first design direction with graphite, off-white, and restrained crimson accents; emphasize crisp status hierarchy, calm compliance cues, and an overall product feel that is trustworthy, modern, and audit-ready.
