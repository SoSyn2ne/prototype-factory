# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Many companies now use multiple AI products with separate admin surfaces.
- Offboarding gaps create security and privacy risk.
- Audit trails matter for security operations purchases.

## ASSUMPTION
- AI seat cleanup is painful enough to justify a dedicated workflow.
- Teams care about shared workspaces and uploaded files, not only license revocation.
- A product can start with checklist orchestration before deep integrations.

## UNKNOWN
- Which buyer feels the pain most: IT, security, finance, or people ops.
- Whether the must-have feature is automation, evidence capture, or exposure discovery.
- How much vendor-specific complexity users will tolerate in a unified UI.
