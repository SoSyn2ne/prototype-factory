# PRD — Riff Relay

## Product intent
Turn beginner music imitation into a fast social game where a riff mutates through motion-only clues.

## Arrival / validation
A friend share link, music clubs, or streams. Measure completed three-player chains and instant rematches.

## Core loop
Play three seconds → hide audio → pass rhythm pulses → imitate → reveal stacked waveforms → rematch.

## First screen / key screens
Six luminous keyboard strings and a three-second record ring. Instrument, cue, waiting room, reveal, share card.

## Interaction rules
No microphone required; synthesize locally. Provide muted-device and reduced-motion modes.

## QA / risks
Latency, mute, disconnect, wild miss, close match. Browser audio permission is the principal risk.

## Development start prompt
```text
Build a playful browser instrument using keyboard/touch strings, three-second recording, motion-only relay cards, local synthesized sound, waveform reveal, and instant rematch.
```
