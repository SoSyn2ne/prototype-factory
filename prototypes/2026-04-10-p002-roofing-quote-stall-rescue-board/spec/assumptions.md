# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Home-service deals frequently stall because paperwork and financing readiness are uneven.
- Owners care most about quote-to-close speed on high-value jobs.
- Operational follow-up quality can materially change close rate on already-estimated work.

## ASSUMPTION
- A stall-focused rescue board is more valuable than a broad pipeline view for small roofing operators.
- Staff will follow recommended next actions if blockers are clearly labeled.
- Insurance and financing blockers can be simplified into a few high-signal categories.

## UNKNOWN
- How different sales workflows are between insurance-heavy and retail-heavy roofing businesses.
- Whether teams have enough structured data to populate blocker states automatically.
- What level of homeowner communication history is required to make the tool trustworthy.
