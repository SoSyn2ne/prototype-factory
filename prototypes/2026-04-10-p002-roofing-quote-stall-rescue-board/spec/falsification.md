# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do roofing companies already manage stalled deals effectively inside their existing CRM workflows?
2. Is the true bottleneck contractor capacity or weather, not quote follow-up?
3. Are financing and insurance blockers too nuanced for standardized triage categories?
4. Would owners resist another dashboard if staff still need to work from phone calls and SMS?
5. Is stall volume too low in the target segment to justify dedicated software?
6. Does job close rate depend more on local reputation and pricing than on rescue workflow quality?
