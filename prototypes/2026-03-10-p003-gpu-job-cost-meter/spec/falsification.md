# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users reject average-power inputs as “too hand-wavy” and stop using it?
2. Does the cost estimate fail to correlate with real bills enough to break trust?
3. Do presets feel wrong for common workloads (fine-tune vs inference vs SD)?
4. Are runbook notes ignored because they are obvious or not actionable?
5. Does utilization-driven guidance cause people to increase power for speed, raising cost unexpectedly?
6. Would users rather have a “cost per output” metric (per image/token) than total cost?
7. After one month, does anyone actually change behavior (shorter runs, better utilization) because of this?
