# PRD — Third Place Budget Quest

## Problem
- Young people want offline social rituals, but many public or semi-public hangouts now feel expensive, hostile to lingering, car-dependent, or awkward.
- Hobby and lifestyle communities can also turn social participation into status-driven spending.

## Target user
- Gen Z and millennial friend groups, student clubs, community organizers, local creators, and people trying to rebuild offline social rituals on tight budgets.

## Key UX
- Friends enter budget, transport limits, vibe, weather, and tolerance for awkwardness.
- The app generates a low-cost route with linger-friendly stops, mini-challenges, hidden-cost warnings, and a shareable recap card.

## Required UI sections
- Friend group budget and transport intake
- Vibe selector for cozy, chaotic, quiet, flirty, maker, and outdoors
- Third-place route board with free/cheap stops
- Linger score and hidden-cost warning
- Micro-challenge deck for conversation, making, exploring, and people-watching
- Awkwardness safety valve and bail-out plan
- Shareable quest card and recap poster

- playful-experimental

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web app prototype for "Third Place Budget Quest," a playful social planner that helps broke friend groups turn low-cost local places into offline adventures. Include: friend group budget and transport intake, vibe selector, third-place route board, linger score and hidden-cost warning, micro-challenge deck, awkwardness safety valve, bail-out plan, and shareable quest card/recap poster. Design direction: use the playful-experimental profile with bold social-game energy, tactile quest cards, local map snippets, expressive but readable colors, and shareable poster moments; it should feel like a friend-group adventure generator, not a conventional event calendar.
