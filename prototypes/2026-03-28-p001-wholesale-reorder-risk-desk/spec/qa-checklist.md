# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Scan the watchlist and identify the top at-risk accounts immediately.
- Open one account and understand why reorder confidence declined.
- Choose a recovery play and see the expected revenue impact quickly.

## Edge cases
- Seasonal buyers should not be mislabeled as urgent leaks.
- Large but healthy accounts should remain visually distinct from small urgent ones.
- Multiple warning signals must stay legible without cluttering the main flow.

## Acceptance verification
- Core sections from the idea brief are present and clearly labeled.
- The demo stub reflects title/one-liner correctly and the spec explains the product without placeholders.
- The Stitch prompt can be pasted directly into Stitch with no extra editing.
