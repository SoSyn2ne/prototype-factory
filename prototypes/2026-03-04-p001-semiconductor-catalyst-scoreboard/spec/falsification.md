# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Can an operator complete one full daily update (4-12 tickers) in <=10 minutes for 5 consecutive days?
2. Do >=90% of logged catalysts include all required evidence fields (source link, confidence, horizon), or does quality degrade immediately?
3. When two people independently score the same 10 catalyst events, do they agree on direction and confidence in at least 70% of cases?
4. Does a single low-confidence rumor ever push a ticker to top rank; if yes, is the weighting model too fragile to noise?
5. On a no-news day, does the scoreboard stay stable (no artificial score churn) and still provide useful "no action" confirmation?
6. After 2 weeks of use, can the operator cite at least 2 concrete "research priority changed" decisions attributable to the scoreboard?
7. Do users still interpret the score as near-term price prediction despite repeated disclaimers; if yes, is the concept fundamentally mispositioned?
