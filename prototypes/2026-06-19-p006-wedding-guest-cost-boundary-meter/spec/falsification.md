# Falsification checklist (5-7)

1. The first screen feels like a marketing page instead of the working app.
2. The prototype reduces the idea to a generic dashboard/board/planner without the unique mechanic: User models guest expenses, couple subsidies, lodging awkwardness, and RSVP pressure; the app produces boundary scripts, fairness scores, and a revised invite plan.
3. Required sections are missing: Guest burden header with travel, lodging, attire, gifts, PTO, and per-person fee; Social-risk meter for resentment, embarrassment, and group-chat blowback; Cost split canvas for couple pays, guest pays, optional events, and subsidy tiers; Invite wording lab with tactful alternatives to mandatory fees; Friendship boundary script composer for declining, negotiating, or apologizing; Scenario cards for destination, Airbnb weekend, local ceremony, and microwedding; Shareable fairness receipt for partner/planner/family review.
4. The design does not match the playful-experimental profile.
5. Source signals S11 and S12 are not visible in the problem framing or sample data.
6. The UI lacks concrete sample data and therefore cannot be judged quickly.
7. The output cannot fit the pNNN drop contract with code.html and screen.png.
