# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. The concept feels too niche compared with broader wedding planning pain.
2. The humor risks escalating conflict instead of softening it.
3. Guests do not want to paste private wedding details into a tool.
4. Couples reject anything that challenges their aesthetic requirements.
5. Outfit acceptability cannot be judged without cultural context.
6. The product is shareable but not monetizable.
7. The interaction overlaps too much with generic etiquette advice.
