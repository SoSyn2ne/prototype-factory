# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Option OI/volume clusters often matter around round strikes.
- Near-term expiration often carries more tactical sentiment.

## ASSUMPTION
- A small set of manually entered strikes is enough to validate the UX.
- A simple C/P OI ratio is useful as a first pass.

## UNKNOWN

- Whether this should be per-expiry or aggregated.
