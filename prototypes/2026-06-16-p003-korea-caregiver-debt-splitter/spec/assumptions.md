# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- S5: Private caregiver cost averages about 3.7M won per month, above the average monthly wage, making eldercare a sibling debt and fairness problem rather than only a health issue.
- S6: US private home care is quoted hourly, with 2026 median rates around the mid-$30/hour range and large state variation, showing families need schedule-to-monthly-burn translation.

## ASSUMPTION
- A focused prototype can be built around the trigger moment: A parent needs a private caregiver after hospitalization or decline.
- Users already have enough raw material to enter into the product: family calls, hospital caregiver lists, cash pooling, debt.
- The assigned design profile (calm-consumer) matches the decision pressure and intended buyer.

## UNKNOWN
- Exact willingness to pay for this narrow wedge.
- Whether Stitch will render all sections cleanly without manual correction.
- Whether local/legal details need professional review before production use.
