# PRD — AI Blackout Ledger

## Problem and target user
Founders and finance leads see rising AI spend but cannot separate essential operational value from habit.

## Arrival, business route, and core loop
Arrive from a monthly cost review. Select five jobs, rehearse manual fallback, record time/quality/dependency, classify keep/cut/test, and export a decision receipt. Validate as a quarterly paid audit.

## First screen and key screens
Monthly AI bill beside five critical jobs and “Rehearse an AI-off day.” Screens: spend snapshot, job deck, blackout timeline, fallback recorder, classification, receipt.

## Interaction and output
Each job requires a manual result and confidence note. Output is a finance-ready keep/cut/test ledger.

## Design profile, theme, and sources
- Profile: `financial-analyst`
- Theme: 자산 방어
- Sources: S6, S2

## Constraints and non-goals
Use mock costs; do not claim universal ROI or connect billing accounts.

## Success and QA
Users can explain one essential, one replaceable, and one habitual use after a single rehearsal; all decision states render clearly.
