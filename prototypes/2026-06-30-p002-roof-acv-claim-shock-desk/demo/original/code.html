Analyst Interpretation

Your policy utilizes a strict Actual Cash Value (ACV) settlement provision for roofing structures exceeding 10 years of age.

Applying a 15-year straight-line depreciation schedule against a presumed 25-year architectural shingle lifespan results in a 60% reduction of the replacement cost.

"This calculation results in a $12,400 out-of-pocket gap to complete full roof replacement at current market material rates."

However, the adjuster's report notes exceptional granular adhesion prior to the wind event. This presents a strong basis to argue for Observed Condition Depreciation rather than strict straight-line age depreciation.
