# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Owners default to lawyers/brokers and do not want self-serve analysis.
2. Commercial lease terms vary too widely for a compact prototype to feel credible.
3. Users lack relocation-cost data, making scenarios feel speculative.
4. Landlords ignore counteroffer memos from tenants.
5. Product risk overlaps too much with legal-advice boundaries.
6. Existing accounting tools already handle enough of the cost math.
7. A one-time renewal pain does not create repeat usage.
