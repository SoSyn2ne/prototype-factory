Formal Notice

The Action Script

[Date]

To: Customer Success / Dispute Resolution Department
Subject: Formal Complaint Regarding Deceptive Billing Practices - Account #[Your Account Number]

To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing to formally dispute a charge of [$Amount] levied on my account on [Date of Charge]. This charge represents a significant departure from the agreed-upon terms of service and was initiated without transparent consent.

"The user interface deliberately obfuscated the auto-renewal clause, employing 'dark patterns' that violate standard consumer protection expectations."

I request an immediate reversal of this charge. Furthermore, I require written confirmation that my account has been fully decoupled from any auto-enrollment programs. Failure to resolve this matter within five (5) business days will result in immediate escalation to the relevant regulatory bodies outlined below, as well as a formal dispute filed through my financial institution.

I expect a prompt and satisfactory resolution.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your Contact Information]
