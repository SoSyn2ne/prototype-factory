# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. In a 5-day trial, do users skip daily triage on more than 2 days because setup/scoring feels too heavy?
2. When given the same 12 events, do two users disagree on top-3 priority picks in more than half of cases?
3. Does the ranked view fail to change any research action compared with a plain chronological calendar?
4. Do users interpret the triage rank as a short-term price prediction despite explicit positioning?
5. Can users explain why one event outranks another within 30 seconds, or is the method too opaque?
6. Does event volume above 25 items/day collapse usability (slow review, abandoned scoring, ignored list)?
