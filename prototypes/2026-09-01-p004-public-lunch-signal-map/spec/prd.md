# PRD — Public Lunch Signal Map

## Problem and target user
Korean diners distrust sponsored reviews and struggle to turn raw public expense records into local lunch choices.

## Arrival, business route, and core loop
Search a station, inspect repeat agency visits and source records, add places to a walkable trail, and share. Validate via local SEO, ads, and reservation affiliates.

## First screen and key screens
Lunch-radius map with repeated-visit badges. Screens: search, map, visit timeline, context card, provenance drawer, trail builder.

## Interaction and output
Every ranking opens its source evidence and caveat. Output is a three-stop lunch trail with public-record links.

## Design profile, theme, and sources
- Profile: `calm-consumer`
- Theme: 발견
- Sources: S4

## Constraints and non-goals
Use representative mock records; do not imply public spending proves quality or legality.

## Success and QA
Users understand the repeat-visit signal and caveat in 3 seconds; sparse, one-off, repeat, closed, and source-error states render.
