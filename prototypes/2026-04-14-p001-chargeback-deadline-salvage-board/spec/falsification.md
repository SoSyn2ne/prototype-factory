# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do billing leads already get enough deadline prioritization from their existing PM or RCM system?
2. Are the highest-dollar recovery cases too few in number to justify a dedicated product?
3. Does missing evidence depend so heavily on external staff that the dashboard cannot materially improve outcomes?
4. Are payer filing rules too inconsistent to normalize without expensive maintenance?
5. Would teams reject one more queue unless it also handles actual appeal submission?
6. Is the recoverable amount too uncertain early in the workflow to support confident prioritization?
7. Does the concept fail if outsourced billing vendors control the underlying process and data?
