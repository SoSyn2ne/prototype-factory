## Happy path
- The landing view communicates the one-liner and theme within the first screen.
- The user can identify body debt snapshot and move naturally toward recovery action priority ladder.
- The design profile calm-consumer is visible without needing supporting copy.

- Dense information still feels scannable on a standard laptop viewport.
- Empty or low-data states do not break the emotional promise of the concept.
- Secondary modules do not overpower the core workflow around Turn scattered health guilt into a ranked recovery sequence that shows which small habit or appointment will reduce the most physical damage first.
