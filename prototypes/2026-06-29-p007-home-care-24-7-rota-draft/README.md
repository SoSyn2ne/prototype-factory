## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "Home Care 24-7 Rota Draft" for families hiring round-the-clock elder caregivers to test agency coverage, turnover gaps, tasks, and backup shifts before burnout wins. The first screen must be a working care operations console with a 24/7 coverage header, weekly caregiver rota, task matrix, agency-vs-independent comparison, turnover risk board, family escalation ladder, and print-friendly house guide. Use realistic sample data: parent with dementia, daughter 9 minutes away, three agency aides, two independent caregivers, uncovered Sunday 2am-6am shift, medication reminders, fall-risk note, and monthly budget ceiling. Design direction: use the field-ops profile with reliable handoff rows, large status blocks, calm steel neutrals, and one safety-orange accent for uncovered care gaps. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.
