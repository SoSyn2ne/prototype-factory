Fire Drill: Suspicious Caller

This is a safe practice space. We will simulate a common scam scenario so you can practice your response in a low-pressure environment.

info

Practice Reminder

This is only a drill. The person calling you will be a Family Guard automated assistant acting as a suspicious caller. Nothing you say here will affect your real accounts. Take your time and feel free to end the drill at any point.

description The Scenario

You will receive a call from someone claiming to be your bank. They will say there is an urgent problem with your account and ask for your "Safe Word" to verify your identity.

Your Script

"Before we continue, please tell me the Family Safe Word we established."

"If you cannot provide it, I will hang up and call the bank directly at the number on the back of my card."
