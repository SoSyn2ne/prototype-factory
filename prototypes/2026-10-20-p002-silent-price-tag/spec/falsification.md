# Falsification checklist

1. Shoppers prefer asking the seller and do not tap visible tags.
2. Anonymous tapping still creates a login, chat, or social obligation.
3. Sellers cannot update twelve tags faster than replacing paper labels.
4. Offline tags show stale prices without a visible timestamp.
5. Bundle pricing is mistaken for the single-item price.
6. The premium-editorial styling weakens contrast or price legibility.
7. S03 does not recur in local craft-market interviews.
