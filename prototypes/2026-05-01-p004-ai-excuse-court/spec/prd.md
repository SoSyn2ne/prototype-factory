# PRD — AI Excuse Court

## Problem
- A playful classroom dispute simulator where teachers rehearse parent/student AI-cheating conversations and turn drama into a fair paper trail.

## Target user
- Teachers, department leads, and school trainers navigating AI-writing accusations, parent emails, detector uncertainty, and admin pressure for phone calls.

## Key UX
- Choose a scenario, cross-examine the student/parent/admin, collect evidence cards, test policy-safe wording, and export a neutral communication trail that avoids false certainty while still protecting the teacher.

## Required UI sections
- case docket scenario picker
- evidence card table: assignment, drafts, detector note, rubric
- parent/student argument simulator
- policy-safe response composer
- paper-trail export preview
- absurd verdict badge wall

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- 사교육

## Stitch prompt
Design a playful web prototype called AI Excuse Court for teachers rehearsing AI-cheating disputes with students, parents, and admins. The user should pick a classroom scenario, inspect evidence cards such as assignment instructions, draft history, rubric notes, and AI-detector caveats, cross-examine a parent/student argument simulator, compose policy-safe wording, and export a neutral paper trail. Include a case docket scenario picker, evidence card table, parent/student argument simulator, policy-safe response composer, paper-trail export preview, and absurd verdict badge wall. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with courtroom-game metaphors, expressive but readable cards, witty microcopy, dramatic stamps, and bright accent colors while keeping the final export calm, fair, and professional.
