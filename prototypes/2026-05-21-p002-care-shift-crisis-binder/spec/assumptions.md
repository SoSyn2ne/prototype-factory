# Assumptions - Care Shift Crisis Binder

## KNOWN
- Community caregivers repeatedly describe sudden crisis logistics and paperwork overload.
- The first 72 hours after a fall or move require many calls and documents.
- Assigned design profile: calm-consumer.
- Assigned theme: 건강.

## ASSUMPTION
- Families will share a simple binder link before buying complex care-management software.
- Reducing sibling ambiguity is a major perceived value.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether caregivers trust a lightweight app with sensitive health/admin information.
- Which export format facilities and siblings actually use.
