# Falsification checklist - Care Shift Crisis Binder

1. The app feels like medical advice instead of logistics support.
2. Users cannot complete the first safety snapshot quickly under stress.
3. Sibling assignment creates more conflict than clarity.
4. Paperwork checklist is too generic for real facilities.
5. Caregivers refuse to enter medication/contact data.
6. Printable/export packet is not useful at appointments.
7. The design feels childish or too soft for crisis moments.
