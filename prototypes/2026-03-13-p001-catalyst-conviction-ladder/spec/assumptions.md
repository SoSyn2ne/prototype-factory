# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Investors routinely track catalysts informally (notes, screenshots, chat).
- Ranking changes decision behavior: people act more confidently when tradeoffs are explicit.
- Time horizon materially affects how you should evaluate the same catalyst.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple 0–5 impact and 0–5 confidence scale is "good enough" to start.
- A coarse horizon penalty (longer horizon → lower near-term conviction) improves prioritization.
- The user will tolerate a small amount of structure if the UI is fast.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether the weighting should favor confidence over impact for most users.
- Whether a ladder metaphor (vs table) increases clarity.
- Which falsification prompts actually change behavior (vs being ignored).
