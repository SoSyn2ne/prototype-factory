## Stitch prompt

Create a responsive web app called "Mom-and-Pop Rent Squeeze Court" for Small landlords with one to four units who are squeezed by insurance renewals, rent caps, repairs, and tenant relationship risk.. The app is based on observed user pull from S14: Small landlords report steep insurance renewal jumps and compare multi-year premium increases. The app should use a Tradeoff Court around the trigger moment "Enter premium changes, local rent limits, repair reserve, vacancy estimate, and tenant history; the app stages a tradeoff court with evidence, options, and a respectful renewal explanation." and make the first screen communicate "A small-landlord tradeoff court that compares insurance spikes, rent caps, repairs, and vacancy risk before a renewal decision becomes a fight.". Build the core workflow around user-entered evidence and produce renewal decision docket and explanation letter. Required sections: Cost evidence docket; Rent-cap rule panel; Repair reserve calculator; Vacancy risk witness; Tenant impact note; Renewal options bench; Exportable explanation letter. Emotional pressure to make visible: 자산 방어. Design direction: use the field-ops profile with field-ops clarity, sturdy forms, practical green accents, and printable evidence blocks. Avoid generic dashboard framing; make the main interaction feel like Tradeoff Court.
