# PRD — HOA Premium Shock Town Hall

## Problem
- A meeting-room projector prototype that helps condo boards explain insurance non-renewal, dues hikes, reserves, and owner tradeoffs without chaos.

## Target user
- Condo HOA board members, property managers, and homeowners facing insurance non-renewal or surprise premium hikes.

## Key UX
- Board imports the renewal shock, then presents a projector-friendly town hall flow: why it happened, options, dues impact, owner Q&A, and vote-ready tradeoffs.

## Required UI sections
- Town-hall projector mode
- Premium shock explainer
- Carrier quote and non-renewal timeline
- Dues/reserve impact slider
- Roof/claim/risk evidence wall
- Owner question queue
- Vote-ready tradeoff summary
