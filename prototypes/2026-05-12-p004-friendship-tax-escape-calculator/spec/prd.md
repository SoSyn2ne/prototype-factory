# PRD — Friendship Tax Escape Calculator

## Problem
- A playful social-budget simulator that shows the true cost of weddings, trips, outfits, gifts, and the emotional price of saying no.
- Search/community evidence suggests this is a live pain, not a generic dashboard rename.

## Target user
- Wedding guests, bridesmaids/groomsmen, friend groups in their late 20s and 30s, and budget-constrained people afraid to look cheap or unsupportive.

## Key UX
- Enter upcoming weddings, trips, roles, flights, hotels, outfits, gifts, PTO, partner costs, and relationship closeness; visualize the friendship tax, rank obligations by emotional risk, suggest graceful boundary scripts, and generate a shareable budget card.

## Required UI sections
- event obligation intake
- total friendship-tax calculator
- closeness versus cost map
- emotional-risk meter
- boundary script generator
- alternative support menu
- shareable budget card

## Design profile
- `playful-experimental` — reflected in the Stitch prompt and visual direction.

## Theme
- 외로움

## External signal references
- Reddit r/AmIOverreacting wedding guest cost anxiety — https://www.reddit.com/r/AmIOverreacting/comments/1quzxw2/aio_overwhelmed_by_the_cost_of_being_a_wedding/
- Reddit r/weddingplanning bridesmaid/wedding participation frustration — https://www.reddit.com/r/weddingplanning/

## Stitch prompt
Design a playful social-budget product called Friendship Tax Escape Calculator for wedding guests, bridesmaids and groomsmen, friend groups in their late 20s and 30s, and budget-constrained people afraid to look cheap or unsupportive. Users should enter upcoming weddings, trips, roles, flights, hotels, outfits, gifts, PTO, partner costs, and relationship closeness, then visualize the friendship tax, rank obligations by emotional risk, suggest graceful boundary scripts, and generate a shareable “I love you, but here is my budget” card. Include an event obligation intake, total friendship-tax calculator, closeness versus cost map, emotional-risk meter, boundary script generator, alternative support menu, and shareable budget card. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with bold receipt-collage visuals, soft social-anxiety humor, colorful obligation tokens, expressive meters, and a memorable share card so the app feels like a portfolio-worthy confession booth for friendship money stress rather than a boring budget spreadsheet.

## Constraints
- Time: Phase A spec/scaffold only; do not implement custom demo yet.
- Budget: Use Stitch export workflow for Phase B.
- Platform: Web prototype, desktop-first unless consumer flow benefits from responsive cards.

## Non-goals
- Do not claim legal, medical, tax, or financial certainty.
- Do not integrate live paid APIs in Phase A.

## Success metrics
- A user understands the painful trigger within 5 seconds.
- The main workflow is clear from intake to export/share/action.
- The UI can be built from the Stitch prompt without additional explanation.

## Notes
- Candidate category: Fun / Experimental.
