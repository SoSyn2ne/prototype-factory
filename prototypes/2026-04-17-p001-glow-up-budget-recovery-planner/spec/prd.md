# PRD — Glow-Up Budget Recovery Planner

## Problem
- A cosmetic-treatment planning workspace that helps appearance-conscious users compare procedures, downtime, financing pain, and event deadlines before they overspend for a glow-up.
- Most beauty or clinic decision flows push aspiration and urgency without giving users a grounded way to compare visible payoff, downtime, and financing pain.

## Target user
- Adults preparing for weddings, reunions, job changes, or dating re-entry who want to improve appearance fast without getting trapped in overpriced aesthetic packages.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should understand the best-fit plan in under 7 minutes.
- Budget: Version one should rely on manual inputs and static guidance instead of live clinic integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning product for focused comparison before a consult or purchase.

## Non-goals
- Full treatment marketplace or online booking engine.
- Medical diagnosis or personalized clinical advice.
- Social feed, influencer content, or cosmetic community features.

## Success metrics
- Users can narrow from many treatment ideas to one realistic plan in a single session.
- Users can see deadline, downtime, and spend tradeoffs without opening separate notes.
- The interface reduces insecure impulse buying by making tradeoffs explicit and calm.
