# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do users still choose based on clinic charisma rather than explicit comparisons?
2. Is aesthetic-package pricing too opaque for a planner to feel actionable?
3. Does discussing financing make the concept feel uncomfortable instead of empowering?
4. Will users reject desktop planning for a mobile-first beauty workflow?
5. Does the concept fail if visible payoff cannot be estimated credibly?
6. Are deadlines like weddings and interviews too seasonal for repeat use?
