# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Investors already receive too much information; volume is not the bottleneck.
- If a system cannot explain *why* something matters for a specific thesis, it will be ignored.
- Telegram is a fast, low-friction channel for daily briefs.

## ASSUMPTION
- Users can write a “good enough” thesis in <3 minutes using a template.
- A hard cap (3–7 cards/day) increases trust and long-term retention.
- “Incident response” framing (severity, impact, next action) will feel clearer than typical market newsletters.
- Simple feedback (relevant/not relevant) is sufficient to improve future briefs.

## UNKNOWN
- What sources are best as reliable anchors for different thesis types (company-specific vs macro)?
- Whether users prefer thesis organized by positions (ticker) or by themes (e.g., ‘AI infra’).
- How often deltas are truly actionable on a daily cadence vs weekly cadence.
- Whether summarization + delta detection can be done cheaply without drifting into genericness.
- What minimum “baseline memory” is needed (yesterday’s brief, last 7 days, or thesis change log).
