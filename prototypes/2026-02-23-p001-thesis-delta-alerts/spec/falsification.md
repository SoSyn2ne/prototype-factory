# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. **If users can’t write a usable thesis quickly**, does onboarding exceed 5 minutes and lead to drop-off?
2. **If most daily cards are marked “not relevant”** after 1 week (e.g., <50% relevance), is the core promise (thesis linkage) failing?
3. **If severity ratings don’t correlate with user action** (no follow-up tasks even on “action/thesis-risk”), is the format not actionable?
4. **If the cap still feels noisy** (users complain even at ≤5 cards/day), does daily cadence need to be weekly by default?
5. **If “delta” requires too much historical context**, do we need a heavier baseline store that breaks the “cheap + simple” constraint?
6. **If credible sources are paywalled/limited**, does the product collapse without broker integrations or premium news feeds?
7. **If the system frequently misses obvious thesis-breakers** (user points to a major event it ignored), is the data acquisition approach inadequate?
