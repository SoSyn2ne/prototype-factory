# PRD - Answer Engine Product Visibility Lab

## Problem
- A GEO/AEO rehearsal bench that lets small e-commerce brands see whether AI answers mention their product, competitor, or nobody.
- Source evidence: S4 - Business Insider on Lantern pivoting to e-commerce GEO/AEO: E-commerce brands worry that AI-driven search and shopping answers fail to mention them, pushing a new visibility problem beyond classic SEO. S3 - Reddit r/Entrepreneur discussion on real AI business use cases beyond hype: Commenters describe social listening and lead qualification as practical AI use, catching intent in Reddit, Quora, X, and niche forums.

## Target user
- Niche Shopify and DTC operators whose sales depend on being named in AI shopping answers, buyer guides, and product comparisons.

## Key UX
- Enter product category, competitor names, proof claims, and buyer questions; the app simulates answer-engine queries, maps mention gaps, and outputs a claim/evidence patch list.

## Required UI sections
- Query rehearsal wall
- Competitor mention matrix
- Missing proof checklist
- Product claim evidence cards
- FAQ/schema patch plan
- Content brief queue
- Before/after AI-answer preview

## Assigned design profile
- operator-dense
- Design direction: dark operator workspace, dense matrices, clear query states, and sharp orange action markers.
