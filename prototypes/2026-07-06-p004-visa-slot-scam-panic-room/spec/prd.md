# PRD — Visa Slot Scam Panic Room

## Problem
- A recovery checklist for applicants who used a slot broker and need to protect accounts, money, and evidence fast.
- Source evidence: S9 - Reddit r/Scams: US/Canada visa appointment scam: Applicants report slot brokers holding accounts hostage and removing victims from large WhatsApp groups.

## Target user
- Visa applicants and families who paid a broker, lost portal access, or suspect a WhatsApp/Telegram appointment scam.

## Key UX
- Answer what happened, then get a 30-minute lock-down checklist, evidence folder, official-contact map, and warning post template.

## Required UI sections
- Incident triage
- Account lock-down checklist
- Evidence vault
- Official-contact map
- Payment dispute notes
- Community warning draft

## Design profile
- calm-consumer
- Design direction must be tailored inside the Stitch prompt and avoid generic dashboard styling.

## Theme
- 자산 방어 / 불안

## Source signal references
- S9: Reddit r/Scams: US/Canada visa appointment scam — https://www.reddit.com/r/Scams/comments/1s9u1fx/uscanada_us_visa_appointment_scam/

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive web prototype for "Visa Slot Scam Panic Room". Target user: visa applicants who paid a slot broker, lost portal access, or suspect a WhatsApp/Telegram appointment scam. Core UX: the user answers a short incident triage, then receives a prioritized account lock-down checklist, evidence vault, official contact map, payment dispute notes, and a community warning draft. Required sections: panic summary, incident triage cards, 30-minute checklist, evidence vault mock, official channel map, payment dispute notes, and shareable warning draft. Use the calm-consumer design profile: reassuring hierarchy, gentle accent, clear recovery steps, high-legibility forms, and no sensational scam graphics.

## Success metrics
- A Stitch operator can paste the prompt without rewriting it.
- The first screen shows the actual workflow, not a landing page.
- The output artifact is visible before the fold or immediately after the primary input.
