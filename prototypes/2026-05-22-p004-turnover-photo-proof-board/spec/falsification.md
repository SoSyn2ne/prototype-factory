# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Cleaner mobile flow takes too many taps under time pressure.
2. Hosts already use a property-management tool with adequate checklists.
3. Photo proof storage is more liability than value.
4. Same-day timeline does not adapt to late checkout or early check-in.
5. Damage-claim packet lacks enough evidence to be useful.
6. The field-ops UI is not readable on a phone.
7. Hosts will not pay unless integrated with booking calendars.
