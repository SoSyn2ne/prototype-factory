# PRD — Creator Invoice Shield

## Problem
- A sponsor-deal invoice checker that helps creators avoid brand finance rejections, tax confusion, and late-payment chaos.
- Inspired by external problem signals: Signal 6 — Reddit r/CreatorEconomy invoice/TDS questions: https://www.reddit.com/r/CreatorEconomy/; Signal 9 — Reddit r/contentcreation creator-business mess complaint: https://www.reddit.com/r/contentcreation/comments/1rsgpxf/why_is_managing_a_creator_business_still_such_a/

## Target user
- Micro-creators, newsletter writers, YouTubers, Instagram/TikTok creators, and small talent managers sending brand invoices without a finance team.

## Key UX
- Enter a sponsor brief, deliverables, jurisdiction/tax hints, payment terms, and brand finance requirements; the app checks missing invoice fields, tax-deduction questions, proof links, due-date risk, and sends a clean finance-ready packet.

## Required UI sections
- sponsor deal intake card
- invoice rejection checklist
- tax/TDS confusion explainer
- deliverable proof attachment tray
- payment-term risk meter
- brand finance packet preview
- late-payment nudge composer

## Design profile
- premium-editorial

## Theme

## Stitch prompt
Design a polished creator-business product called Creator Invoice Shield for micro-creators sending sponsorship invoices to brand finance teams. The product should let users enter sponsor name, campaign deliverables, proof links, tax or TDS questions, payment terms, invoice fields, and due dates, then flag why the invoice might be rejected and assemble a finance-ready packet. Include a sponsor deal intake card, invoice rejection checklist, tax/TDS confusion explainer, deliverable proof attachment tray, payment-term risk meter, brand finance packet preview, and late-payment nudge composer. Design direction: use a premium-editorial aesthetic with warm charcoal, cream, muted brass, refined typography, elegant proof cards, and calm money-status cues so creators feel more professional and powerful without drowning in accounting software.
