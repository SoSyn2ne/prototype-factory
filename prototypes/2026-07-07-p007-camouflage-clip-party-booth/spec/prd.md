# PRD - Camouflage Clip Party Booth

## Problem
- A friendslop-style party toy where players draw camouflage rules on a room photo, hide, fail loudly, and export a shareable challenge card.
- Source evidence: S15 - Windows Central on Meccha Chameleon selling 15M copies in under a month: A simple hide-and-seek camouflage rule, low price, funny failure clips, and streamer-friendly play created a viral friendslop loop.

## Target user
- Friends, streamers, and event hosts who want a 15-second rule party game that produces funny failure clips.

## Key UX
- Upload or choose a room photo, paint legal hiding zones and forbidden colors, start a round timer, then grade how visible each player is and export a clip-card challenge.

## Required UI sections
- Room photo stage
- Paintable camouflage zones
- 15-second rule card
- Round timer
- Visibility judge meter
- Fail replay caption
- Shareable challenge poster

## Assigned design profile
- playful-experimental
- Design direction: playful-experimental energy, high-contrast stage lighting, bold timers, and tactile paint controls.

## Theme
- 외로움 / 역전
