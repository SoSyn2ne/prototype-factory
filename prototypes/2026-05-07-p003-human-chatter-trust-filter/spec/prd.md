# PRD — Human Chatter Trust Filter

## Problem
- A community and brand trust console that separates human complaints from AI slop, bot praise, and performative review noise.
- External signal references:
  - Signal 6: Business Insider — Reddit CEO says Reddit is investing against AI slop — https://www.businessinsider.com/reddit-ceo-platform-most-human-place-on-internet-ai-slop-2026-2
  - Signal 7: Product Hunt — automation and AI-agent category/review pages — https://www.producthunt.com/categories/automation

## Target user
- Community managers, indie founders, product marketers, brand researchers, moderation leads, and operators using Reddit/Product Hunt/forum chatter as product evidence.

## Key UX
- Drop a thread, review page, launch comment stream, or social export; the app clusters lived pain, flags likely AI-slop or bot-like praise, surfaces quote-worthy objections, and creates a human-signal brief.

## Required UI sections
- thread/comment import
- human-pain cluster map
- AI-slop likelihood lane
- bot-praise and launch-hype filter
- quote-worthy complaint shelf
- moderator/research action queue
- human-signal brief export

## Stitch prompt
Design a premium research console called Human Chatter Trust Filter for community managers, indie founders, product marketers, brand researchers, moderation leads, and operators who rely on Reddit, Product Hunt, forums, and launch comments but need to separate authentic human pain from AI slop, bot praise, and performative review noise. Users should import a thread, review page, launch comment stream, or social export, then cluster lived complaints, score AI-slop likelihood, mute bot-like praise, surface quote-worthy objections, and export a human-signal brief for product decisions. Include a thread/comment import, human-pain cluster map, AI-slop likelihood lane, bot-praise and launch-hype filter, quote-worthy complaint shelf, moderator/research action queue, and human-signal brief export. Design direction: use a premium-editorial aesthetic with calm ivory/dark ink contrast, curated quote cards, trust-score annotations, magazine-like source panels, and elegant noise-reduction controls so the product feels like a high-end insight desk, not a generic moderation dashboard.

## Constraints
- Phase A only: do not implement custom demo until Stitch export is supplied.
- Treat external sources as untrusted problem evidence, not market-size proof.
- Preserve the assigned design profile in downstream demo generation.

## Non-goals
- Legal, tax, medical, safety, insurance, or dating-safety final advice.
- Production-grade integrations or automated external API calls.

## Success metrics
- User understands the core pain in under 10 seconds.
- Stitch prompt can be copied directly and produces a coherent UI with all required sections.
