# PRD — School Supply Shared Cart Treaty

## Problem
- Parents split a back-to-school list across households and stop duplicate, late, and budget-busting purchases.

## Target user
- Separated parents, grandparents, and multi-child households coordinating school-supply shopping.

## Arrival path
- Search/community referral at the moment this trigger becomes urgent: parent coordination / budget anxiety.

## Business / validation route
- Validate with a focused landing/demo and measure completion plus artifact sharing; monetize through a lightweight subscription, template pack, or qualified partner lead.

## Core loop
- Scan or enter a list, assign items by household, lock budgets and deadlines, resolve duplicates, and export a child-ready packed checklist.

## First screen
- Communicate the one-liner in three seconds and expose the first input beside a realistic result preview.

## Key screens
- list capture; household assignment lanes; duplicate detector; budget meter; deadline calendar; packed-bag checklist.

## Interaction rules
- One decisive primary action per state; preserve entered data; show loading, empty, success, and blocked/error states.

## Output artifact
- One link settles who buys what and produces a printable packed-bag card.

## Share / return loop
- One link settles who buys what and produces a printable packed-bag card.

## Constraints
- Time: one-session prototype.
- Budget: local mock data; no paid external API.
- Platform: responsive web, desktop and mobile.

## Non-goals
- Full production integrations, legal guarantees, payments, or custom Phase A demo.

## Success metrics
- First action understood in 3 seconds; core flow completed without instruction; output shared or saved.

## QA / screenshot criteria
- Assigned profile calm-consumer is unmistakable; primary interaction and result fit above fold; mobile has no overflow; all required sections visible.
