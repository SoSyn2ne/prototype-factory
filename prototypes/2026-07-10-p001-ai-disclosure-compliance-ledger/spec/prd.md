# PRD — AI Disclosure Compliance Ledger

## Problem
- Small businesses prove where AI touches customers, contracts, hiring, and support before a state rule or angry customer asks.

## Target user
- Owners and ops leads at small firms using AI chatbots, hiring tools, contract generators, meeting bots, or customer-service agents.

## Key UX
- Inventory every AI touchpoint, classify customer/employee impact, attach approved-tool status, and export a disclosure plus review packet.

## Required UI sections
  - AI touchpoint inventory
  - Customer vs employee impact lanes
  - Approved tool and sensitive-data checklist
  - Disclosure text builder
  - Human oversight proof log
  - Risk review calendar
  - Exportable compliance ledger

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Theme
- AI compliance / trust defense
