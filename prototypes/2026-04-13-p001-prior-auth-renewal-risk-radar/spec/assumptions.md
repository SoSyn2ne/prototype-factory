# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Prior authorization renewals often depend on payer deadlines, updated chart notes, and clear ownership.
- Therapy interruption risk rises quickly when documentation is incomplete near refill dates.
- Coordinators need to triage by both urgency and medication value, not by date alone.

## ASSUMPTION
- A unified renewal risk queue will outperform status tracking across payer portals, spreadsheets, and inboxes.
- Teams will act faster if the missing item is framed as a single next step rather than generic case status.
- Escalation visibility across patient cohorts will reduce preventable therapy gaps.

## UNKNOWN
- How much payer status data is realistically available without direct integrations.
- Whether clinics and pharmacies can share enough workflow context inside one product.
- How tolerant users are of manual document-state updates before automation exists.
