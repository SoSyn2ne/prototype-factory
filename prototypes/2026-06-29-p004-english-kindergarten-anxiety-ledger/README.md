## Stitch prompt
Build a production-grade web app prototype called "English Kindergarten Anxiety Ledger" for Korean parents comparing English-kindergarten cost, policy uncertainty, commute fatigue, and status pressure without drowning in mom-chat panic. The first screen must be a working family decision surface with a pressure header, cost stack, policy uncertainty lane, child fatigue check-in, sibling fairness calculator, mom-chat claim parking lot, and family decision card. Use realistic sample data: KRW 1,545,000 monthly tuition, 38-minute commute, shuttle fee, five-year-old child, sibling entering daycare, policy rumor flagged as unverified, and grandparent pressure note. Design direction: use the calm-consumer profile with reassuring step cards, warm neutral surfaces, gentle blue-green accents, and copy that lowers shame while keeping the decision concrete. Do not make a marketing landing page; the first screen must be the working app.
