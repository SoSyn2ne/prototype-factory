# Pet Premium Shock Triage

A calm decision board for pet owners facing sudden insurance hikes, showing switch risks, claim history tradeoffs, and complaint options.

## Status
- phase-a

## Candidate type
- Practical / Commercial

## Design profile
- calm-consumer

## Theme

## Target user
Pet owners who just received a steep renewal increase and need to decide whether to keep coverage, raise deductibles, switch insurers, self-insure, or file a state complaint without accidentally losing pre-existing-condition protection.

## Key UX
Enter current premium, renewal jump, pet age/breed, claim history, deductible, reimbursement rate, quoted alternatives, and known conditions; the app compares scenarios, warns about switch traps, prepares insurer/state complaint notes, and produces a family-safe decision memo.

## Required UI sections
- premium shock intake
- pet health and claim history timeline
- keep/switch/self-insure scenario cards
- pre-existing-condition trap warnings
- deductible and reimbursement slider
- state complaint note builder
- family decision memo

## Stitch prompt
Design a responsive consumer decision product called Pet Premium Shock Triage for pet owners who just received a steep insurance renewal increase. Users should enter current premium, renewal jump, pet age and breed, claim history, deductible, reimbursement rate, quoted alternatives, and known conditions; the app compares keep, switch, deductible-change, and self-insure scenarios, warns about pre-existing-condition traps, prepares insurer or state complaint notes, and produces a family-safe decision memo. Include a premium shock intake, pet health and claim history timeline, keep/switch/self-insure scenario cards, pre-existing-condition trap warnings, deductible and reimbursement slider, state complaint note builder, and family decision memo. Design direction: use a calm-consumer aesthetic with warm neutral cards, gentle teal accents, low-stress comparison language, soft pet-health timelines, and clear next-step buttons so the product feels protective and emotionally steady during a money panic.
