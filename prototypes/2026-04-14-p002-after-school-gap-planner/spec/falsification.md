# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do parents already solve this well enough with shared calendars and notes?
2. Is the real pain not planning but lack of trustworthy outcome data from tutors and academies?
3. Would users abandon setup because entering schedules and spend feels like more homework?
4. Are recommendations too subjective to feel actionable without expert guidance?
5. Does the concept become much less useful for families with only one recurring activity?
6. Will parents reject any framing that appears to score or judge the child rather than the schedule?
7. Is mobile-first actually required despite the desktop planning assumption?
