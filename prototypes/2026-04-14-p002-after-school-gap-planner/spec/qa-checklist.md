# QA checklist

## Happy path
- Parent lands on the planner and sees one child's weekly load, spend, and goal alignment at a glance.
- Parent spots an overloaded day or a low-ROI activity immediately from the heatmap and outcome panel.
- Parent adjusts the timetable and saves a cleaner recommended schedule.

## Edge cases
- One child has sparse schedule data but high monthly spend.
- Two activities claim to improve the same skill but on different timelines.
- Family values emotional balance over measurable score improvement.

## Acceptance verification
- The schedule heatmap communicates overload without anxiety-heavy visuals.
- Spend and outcome comparison feels understandable to non-expert parents.
- Recommendation UI supports practical decisions instead of abstract analysis only.
