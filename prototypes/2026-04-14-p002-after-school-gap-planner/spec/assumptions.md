# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Parents commonly juggle multiple overlapping after-school commitments with limited visibility into ROI.
- Schedule overload and duplicated tuition are emotionally charged decision points.
- A weekly visual planner is easier to reason about than a spreadsheet of classes and payments.

## ASSUMPTION
- Parents will tolerate light manual data entry if the output feels clarifying and calmer.
- Simple progress tags and confidence scoring are enough for an initial concept.
- The product value is highest when it reframes guilt-heavy decisions into structured tradeoffs.

## UNKNOWN
- Whether parents prefer child-by-child planning or a whole-household view first.
- What outcome metrics feel credible without school-grade integrations.
- Whether academies or tutors might become a second customer type later.
