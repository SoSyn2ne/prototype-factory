# Falsification checklist

1. Users cannot distinguish simulated disable from deletion.
2. Protected essentials can be turned off.
3. Apply and restore QR codes are easily confused.
4. The before/after preview overpromises unsupported model behavior.
5. Parents cannot recover without the adult child repeating the full process.
6. Calm-consumer styling hides compatibility risk.
7. The product becomes an ADB terminal instead of a family support flow.
