A structured guide to communicating with billing departments effectively. Use evidence-based phrasing to contest inaccuracies.

phone_in_talk Official Call Script

"Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I am calling regarding account number [Account #] for a date of service on [Date]."

"I am reviewing my Explanation of Benefits alongside the corresponding bill, and I have found discrepancies regarding the coding applied to my visit."

"Specifically, I am requesting a formal review of the CPT code billed. Can you confirm if the clinical documentation supports a Level 5 complex visit for a standard 15-minute consultation? Please add a note to my file that this charge is currently under patient dispute."

checklist Pre-Call Verification Checklist

Verify these details before initiating contact to ensure a productive conversation.

  • Check that the group number and member ID match your current card exactly.

  • Verify the facility and the specific provider were in-network on the date of service.

  • If the procedure required PA, ensure the authorization number is noted on the bill.
