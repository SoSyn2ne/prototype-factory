# PRD — Gasp Choir Boss Fight

## Problem
- Many phone party games feel like passive trivia instead of embodied cooperative chaos.

## Target user
- Party groups, streamers, and families.

## Arrival path
- Live source signal, operator search, or peer share.

## Business / validation route
- Test five target users completing the core loop with realistic local data.

## Core loop
- Join → calibrate sound → take fish role → sustain lane → wipe out or win → replay.

## First screen
- QR join and four absurd sound-role cards.

## Key screens
- Room join; calibration; role cards; breath lanes; boss arena; wipeout replay; rematch.

## Interaction rules
- Each microphone feeds one sound lane; silence or wrong pitch triggers visible boss retaliation.

## Output artifact
- Six-second waveform-face wipeout replay.

## Share / return loop
- Funny failure clip plus one-tap rematch.

## Constraints
- Time: one-session prototype loop under 15 minutes.
- Budget: local mock data; no paid APIs.
- Platform: responsive web/mobile concept appropriate to the interaction.

## Non-goals
- No production integration, automated external action, or custom demo in Phase A.

## Success metrics
- 80% of five testers explain the core rule in 15 seconds and finish the main loop without help.

## QA / screenshot criteria
- Show noisy room, shy-player tap alternative, breath break, perfect chord, wipeout, and rematch safely.
