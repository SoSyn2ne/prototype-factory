# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Guests often delay RSVP even if they intend to attend.
- Segment matters: family and coworkers respond to different wording and timing.
- Clear options (meal, +1, kids) reduce back-and-forth.

## ASSUMPTION
- A simple funnel view (Viewed → Started → Submitted) plus a “Maybe” bucket is enough to prioritize follow-ups.
- Hosts want *suggested copy* more than raw metrics.
- Showing “reason tags” (busy, unsure, question about +1) increases empathy and reduces awkwardness.

## UNKNOWN
- Which segments users actually use (family branches, workplace teams, friend groups).
- Whether hosts prefer to nudge individually vs. group messages.
- What timing rules feel respectful (days since invite vs. days to deadline).
