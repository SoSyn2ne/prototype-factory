# PRD — AI Citation Revenue Leak Map

## Problem
- A publisher survival console that shows which pages are being cited by AI search while losing clicks, sponsors, and affiliate revenue.
- External signal references:
  - Signal 6: Press Gazette — global publisher Google traffic dropped by a third in 2025 — https://pressgazette.co.uk/media-audience-and-business-data/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/
  - Signal 8: Reddit r/seogrowth — review sites lose SEO traffic while AI still quotes them — https://www.reddit.com/r/seogrowth/comments/1qtx65f/review_sites_lost_up_to_90_of_seo_traffic_so_why/

## Target user
- Independent publishers, niche review sites, affiliate operators, SEO consultants, creator newsletters, and local media teams facing AI-search traffic erosion.

## Key UX
- Import search-console rows, top content URLs, AI citation sightings, sponsor/affiliate value, and update cadence; the app maps click leakage by page, separates citation visibility from revenue, ranks defensive rewrites, and creates sponsor-safe evidence briefs.

## Required UI sections
- traffic/citation import
- page-level click-leak heatmap
- AI citation without click lane
- sponsor and affiliate exposure table
- defensive rewrite priority queue
- community complaint quote shelf
- weekly revenue-risk brief

## Design profile
- financial-analyst

## Theme
- 자산 방어

## Stitch prompt
Design a financial analyst style publisher console called AI Citation Revenue Leak Map for independent publishers, niche review sites, affiliate operators, SEO consultants, creator newsletters, and local media teams facing AI-search traffic erosion. Users should import search-console rows, top content URLs, AI citation sightings, sponsor and affiliate value, and update cadence, then map page-level click leakage, separate citation visibility from actual revenue, rank defensive rewrites, and create sponsor-safe evidence briefs. Include a traffic/citation import, page-level click-leak heatmap, AI citation without click lane, sponsor and affiliate exposure table, defensive rewrite priority queue, community complaint quote shelf, and weekly revenue-risk brief. Design direction: use a financial-analyst aesthetic with crisp tables, dark-mode revenue charts, citation-vs-click deltas, scenario toggles, and CFO-readable risk summaries so the prototype feels like a money-defense terminal for publishers, not an SEO vanity dashboard.
