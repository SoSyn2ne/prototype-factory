# Clone Deal Devil Mirror

A playful creator contract scanner that shows what an AI-likeness clause could turn you into before you sign.

## Status
- phase-a

## Candidate type
- Fun / Experimental

## Design profile
- playful-experimental

## Theme
- 역전

## Target user
UGC creators, micro-influencers, actors, models, voice creators, and creator managers reviewing brand contracts that mention likeness, synthetic media, derivative works, paid ads, whitelisting, or perpetual usage.

## Key UX
Paste a contract clause and campaign brief; the app turns rights language into a dramatic “future clone” storyboard, flags dangerous duration/channel/AI-training terms, suggests negotiation swaps, and creates a shareable red-flag card.

## Required UI sections
- contract clause paste stage
- AI likeness rights highlighter
- future clone storyboard
- usage-duration and channel danger meter
- negotiation swap cards
- manager/legal handoff notes
- shareable red-flag poster

## Stitch prompt
Design a bold experimental web product called Clone Deal Devil Mirror for UGC creators, micro-influencers, actors, models, voice creators, and creator managers reviewing brand contracts with likeness, synthetic media, derivative works, paid ads, whitelisting, or perpetual usage clauses. Users paste a contract clause and campaign brief; the app turns rights language into a dramatic future-clone storyboard, flags dangerous duration, channel, AI-training, and sublicensing terms, suggests negotiation swaps, and creates a shareable red-flag poster. Include a contract clause paste stage, AI likeness rights highlighter, future clone storyboard, usage-duration and channel danger meter, negotiation swap cards, manager/legal handoff notes, and shareable red-flag poster. Design direction: use a playful-experimental aesthetic with theatrical mirror motifs, neon magenta and acid green accents on dark velvet surfaces, comic red-flag badges, and a clear main workflow so it feels shareable and portfolio-worthy without losing contract readability.
