1. Does the prototype avoid a generic dashboard and make the Risk Radar mechanic obvious?
2. Are the source signals visible as user pull, not just trend decoration?
3. Is the first screen a working decision surface rather than a landing page?
4. Does the output artifact (apply-or-skip evidence card) appear clearly?
5. Is the assigned design profile (calm-consumer) visible in density, controls, and tone?
6. Is this materially different from the last seven PF daily batches?
7. Can the Stitch prompt be pasted without extra context?
