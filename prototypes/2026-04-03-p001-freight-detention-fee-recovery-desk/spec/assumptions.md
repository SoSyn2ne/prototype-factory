# Assumptions (KNOWN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN)

## KNOWN
- Fee recovery depends on fast evidence gathering and filing discipline.
- Operators need prioritization more than raw shipment history.
- Desktop review is appropriate for claims and audit workflows.

## ASSUMPTION
- Timeline evidence can be summarized into a trusted recovery score.
- Teams will act faster if the expected recovered cash is explicit.
- A structured claim action panel is more useful than exposing every back-office detail.

## UNKNOWN
- Which evidence pattern most strongly predicts reimbursement success.
- Whether users think in shipment cases, carriers, or terminals first.
- How much document preview detail is required before the tool feels complete.
