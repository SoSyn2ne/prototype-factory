# Falsification checklist (5–7)

1. Do teams already recover these fees well enough with spreadsheets and email templates?
2. Is the real bottleneck contract language rather than operator workflow?
3. Are recovery probabilities too uncertain to rank cases confidently?
4. Would users prefer carrier-level reporting over shipment-level triage?
5. Does value collapse without direct TMS and document-system integrations?
6. Are disputed dollars too small or too infrequent for a dedicated desk to matter?
7. Would finance leaders care more about post-mortem reporting than an active queue?
