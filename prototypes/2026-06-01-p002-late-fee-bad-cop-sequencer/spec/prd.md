# PRD - Late Fee Bad Cop Sequencer

## One-liner
A small-business AR desk that turns awkward overdue invoices into staged notices, pause rules, and evidence logs.

## Problem
- Small businesses know invoices are late but hesitate to escalate because they fear damaging client relationships.
- Community signals S4 and S5 show operators struggling with ignored late fees, unclear pressure, and wasted follow-up time; S6 frames overdue invoices as a survival-level cash-flow issue.

## Target user
- Freelancers, agencies, contractors, and small service businesses.

## Key UX
- Import invoices and terms, choose a relationship stance, then generate a staged escalation sequence from reminder to formal notice, pause rule, late-fee ledger, and evidence packet.

## Required UI sections
- Overdue invoice queue.
- Terms interpreter.
- Escalation timeline.
- Late-fee collectability meter.
- Evidence log.
- Client policy board.
- Cash-impact strip.

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive B2B web app called "Late Fee Bad Cop Sequencer" for freelancers and small businesses. Build an accounts-receivable command center that imports overdue invoices, interprets payment terms, recommends staged escalation, tracks late-fee collectability, stores evidence, and shows when to pause service or require deposits. Required sections: overdue invoice queue, terms interpreter, escalation timeline, late-fee meter, evidence log, client policy board, and cash-impact strip. Design direction: use the `operator-dense` profile with compact tables, sharp urgency accents, disciplined charcoal/slate surfaces, crisp status chips, and practical no-nonsense controls.
