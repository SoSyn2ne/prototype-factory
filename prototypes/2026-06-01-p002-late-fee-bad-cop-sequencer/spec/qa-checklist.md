# QA checklist

## Happy path
- User sees overdue queue, terms, escalation, fees, evidence, policy, and cash impact.
- A sample invoice can move through reminder, formal notice, pause, and packet stages.
- Visual style is compact, practical, and operator-dense.

## Edge cases
- No late-fee clause.
- Client disputes invoice.
- Partial payment.
- Owner wants friendly tone only.

## Acceptance verification
- Includes design profile, theme, and signal references.
- Stitch prompt is present and copy/paste ready.
- Phase A remains spec/scaffold only.
