# Falsification checklist

1. The first screen reads like a generic dashboard instead of subscription spend management + usage guardrails.
2. A target user cannot identify the next action within 5 seconds.
3. The required UI sections are missing: AI spend queue by vendor and team; Hidden-cost alerts: overage, seat creep, token spikes; Human-touch risk notes for customer-facing automations; Daily cap and owner approval rules; Savings receipt and next billing calendar.
4. The design direction does not match operator-dense.
5. The prototype depends on source claims as market-size proof instead of treating them as problem evidence.
6. The workflow duplicates one of the last 7 PF batches without a new user, trigger, data source, or interaction mechanic.
7. The final screen lacks a shareable output, packet, receipt, or result tied to: Owners can share the savings receipt with partners or staff to justify which tools stay and which get capped.
