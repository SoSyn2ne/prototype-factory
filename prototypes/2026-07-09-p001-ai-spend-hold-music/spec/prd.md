# PRD - AI Spend Hold Music

## One-liner
Small business owners see every AI subscription, token spike, and human-touch risk before the monthly bill lands.

## Problem
Owners/operators of teams under 50 who already pay for ChatGPT, agent tools, AI receptionists, CRM automations, or AI writing apps. are reacting to fresh market/community signals but still solve the work with scattered screenshots, invoices, threads, or ad hoc spreadsheets. The prototype should turn that repeated pain into one focused first-screen workflow.

## Target user
Owners/operators of teams under 50 who already pay for ChatGPT, agent tools, AI receptionists, CRM automations, or AI writing apps.

## Key UX
A monthly “hold music” queue groups every AI cost into keep, cap, downgrade, or replace-with-human-touch decisions, with a simple before-bill intervention workflow.

## Required UI sections
- AI spend queue by vendor and team
- Hidden-cost alerts: overage, seat creep, token spikes
- Human-touch risk notes for customer-facing automations
- Daily cap and owner approval rules
- Savings receipt and next billing calendar

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Theme
- AI cost control / small-business margin defense

- subscription spend management + usage guardrails

## Twist
- Focused only on AI tools and the owner’s fear of losing the personal service edge.

## Small group fit
- Small businesses adopting AI fast but without finance staff can try this immediately from invoices and card statements.

## Spread trigger
- Owners can share the savings receipt with partners or staff to justify which tools stay and which get capped.

## Copy/paste Stitch prompt
Create a B2B operator console called “AI Spend Hold Music” for small business owners managing AI subscription and usage costs. Show a compact spend queue, vendor rows, token/seat spike alerts, human-touch risk notes, monthly cap controls, and a savings receipt panel. Design direction: use the operator-dense profile with crisp compact tables, charcoal/navy surfaces, tabular numbers, one sharp action accent, and no decorative hero section.

## Non-goals
- Do not implement a custom local demo in Phase A.
- Do not present source signals as proof of market size.
- Do not turn the product into a generic dashboard without the specific trigger and workflow.

## Success metrics
- A viewer understands the target user and core action within 5 seconds.
- The primary workflow can be inferred from the first screen without explanatory marketing copy.
- The Stitch output preserves the assigned design profile and includes all required sections.
