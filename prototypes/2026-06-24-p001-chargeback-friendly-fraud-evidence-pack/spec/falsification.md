1. The first screen looks like a generic dashboard instead of the specific workflow: Merchant selects a disputed order, connects payment, shipping, customer messages, refund history, and descriptor context; the app scores evidence gaps and produces a bank-ready response packet.
2. The prototype omits any required UI section from the PRD.
3. The design does not visibly match the operator-dense profile.
4. The workflow cannot be understood in 10 seconds by the target user.
5. The source signal references are not reflected in the sample data or product framing.
6. The idea is too close to one of the last seven PF batches without a new user, trigger, data source, or mechanic.
7. The Stitch output becomes a marketing landing page instead of a working app surface.
