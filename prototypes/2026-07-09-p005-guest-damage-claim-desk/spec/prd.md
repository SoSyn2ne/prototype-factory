# PRD - Guest Damage Claim Desk

## One-liner
Short-term rental managers turn messy cleaner photos, invoices, and platform replies into a calm damage-claim timeline.

## Problem
Airbnb/VRBO hosts, property managers, cleaners, and co-hosts documenting damage while protecting the next booking. are reacting to fresh market/community signals but still solve the work with scattered screenshots, invoices, threads, or ad hoc spreadsheets. The prototype should turn that repeated pain into one focused first-screen workflow.

## Target user
Airbnb/VRBO hosts, property managers, cleaners, and co-hosts documenting damage while protecting the next booking.

## Key UX
A claim desk aligns room photos, cleaner notes, invoices, platform responses, and next-guest risk into a single reimbursement story.

## Required UI sections
- Property and booking header
- Photo evidence board by room
- Cleaner/task invoice lane
- Platform claim status and excluded-cost warnings
- Next booking recovery checklist

## Design profile
- premium-editorial

## Theme
- Hospitality trust / claims recovery

## Source signal references
S13: Short-term rental hosts report damage-claim admin work not reimbursed and listings restricted after claims (Reddit r/AirBnBHosts) https://www.reddit.com/r/AirBnBHosts/new/
S16: Travelers still struggle with cancelled-flight refund evidence and escalation scripts (Reddit r/travel, 2026 discussions) https://www.reddit.com/r/travel/comments/1t18gtt/spirit_airlines_will_my_flight_be_cancelled/

## Reference pattern
- insurance claim room + hospitality operations board

## Twist
- Makes damage claims feel composed and host-ready rather than legalistic or panic-driven.

## Small group fit
- Small property managers can use it after every messy turnover without buying enterprise PMS tooling.

## Spread trigger
- The before/after claim timeline is easy to forward to owners, cleaners, or platform support.

## Copy/paste Stitch prompt
Create a polished hospitality claim workspace called “Guest Damage Claim Desk” for short-term rental managers. Show a property header, room-by-room photo evidence, cleaner notes, invoices, platform claim status, excluded-cost warnings, and a next booking recovery checklist. Design direction: use the premium-editorial profile with warm charcoal/stone tones, refined spacing, curated evidence cards, and one quiet CTA color.

## Non-goals
- Do not implement a custom local demo in Phase A.
- Do not present source signals as proof of market size.
- Do not turn the product into a generic dashboard without the specific trigger and workflow.

## Success metrics
- A viewer understands the target user and core action within 5 seconds.
- The primary workflow can be inferred from the first screen without explanatory marketing copy.
- The Stitch output preserves the assigned design profile and includes all required sections.
