# Falsification checklist — Hagwon Spend Triage Board

1. If parents refuse to remove any class even when fatigue and budget clearly spike, the product may not change behavior.
2. If perceived academic payoff cannot be represented simply enough, the board will feel fake or judgmental.
3. If spouses or caregivers disagree on goals too often, single-user planning may be insufficient.
4. If child schedule complexity is low in many households, the product may be overbuilt for the common case.
5. If parents mainly want tutor recommendations rather than triage, the value proposition is misframed.
6. If the weekly rebuild takes too long, usage will collapse into occasional panic sessions.
