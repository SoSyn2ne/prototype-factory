# PRD — Hagwon Spend Triage Board

## Problem
- A parent decision desk that turns scattered private education bills, subject anxiety, and child energy limits into a weekly study spend plan with clear keep, cut, or replace calls.
- Parents often keep stacking classes because each individual cut feels risky, but they rarely see cost, fatigue, and real learning benefit in one decision surface.

## Target user
- Korean parents managing multiple hagwon, tutor, worksheet, and test-prep decisions who feel trapped between fear of falling behind and rising monthly costs.

## Constraints
- Time: Users should understand the top action path in under 5 minutes.
- Budget: The first version should work with manual input, lightweight imports, and no deep enterprise integrations.
- Platform: Desktop-first planning product designed for a single high-focus session.

## Non-goals
- Full back-office system replacement.
- Heavy automation that requires hidden AI decisions.
- Multi-role admin tooling beyond the core operator or end-user workflow.

## Success metrics
- Users can understand risk, upside, or chemistry state in one screen.
- Users can identify the next action without reading a long manual.
- The interface makes the emotional driver of the product feel obvious rather than abstract.

## Notes
- Theme: 사교육.
- Design profile: operator-dense.
- Core UI sections: monthly spend scoreboard, subject confidence grid, child fatigue timeline, keep-cut-replace board, weekly timetable rebuilder, parent decision memo drawer.
