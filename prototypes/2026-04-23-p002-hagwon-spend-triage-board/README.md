# Hagwon Spend Triage Board

A parent decision desk that turns scattered private education bills, subject anxiety, and child energy limits into a weekly study spend plan with clear keep, cut, or replace calls.

## Status
- prototype

## Theme
- 사교육

## Design profile
- operator-dense

## Target user
- Korean parents managing multiple hagwon, tutor, worksheet, and test-prep decisions who feel trapped between fear of falling behind and rising monthly costs.

## Key UX
- Compare academic payoff, stress load, and schedule friction in one place so a parent can defend one realistic study plan instead of endlessly adding more classes.

## Core UI sections
- monthly spend scoreboard
- subject confidence grid
- child fatigue timeline
- keep-cut-replace board
- weekly timetable rebuilder
- parent decision memo drawer

## Stitch prompt
Design a desktop parent decision desk called Hagwon Spend Triage Board for families trying to control private education costs without feeling like they are sacrificing a child's future. It should help users compare hagwon bills, subject anxiety, and child energy limits so they can make clear keep, cut, or replace decisions for the coming week. Include a monthly spend scoreboard, a subject confidence grid, a child fatigue timeline, a keep-cut-replace board, a weekly timetable rebuilder, and a parent decision memo drawer. Design direction: use an operator-dense aesthetic with compact planning surfaces, disciplined hierarchy, dark slate neutrals, and one sharp action accent so the product feels practical, high-signal, and made for hard budget decisions.
