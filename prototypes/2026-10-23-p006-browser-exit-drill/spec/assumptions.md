# Assumptions — Browser Exit Drill

## KNOWN

- The final Phase A title and one-liner are fixed in daily/2026-10-23/ideas.md.
- The target user is: Arc users, browser-dependent freelancers, research teams, customer-support teams, and IT administrators.
- The observed workaround is: bookmark HTML export, handwritten extension checklist, and memory-based recreation.
- Exactly one PF design profile is assigned: financial-analyst.
- Source references are: S11.
- Phase A intentionally contains no custom demo.

## ASSUMPTION

- The target user will understand the mechanic without a guided onboarding tour: choose one recurring task, capture its old-browser path, preview import/restore, then complete the task in the candidate browser under the same checkpoints.
- The concrete output will be worth saving or sharing: browser exit passport with verified tasks, unresolved blockers, fallback, and retest date.
- The selected profile makes the high-pressure moment easier to read, not merely prettier.
- The arrival path can recruit an initial test group: shutdown notice, browser communities, IT offboarding, search phrase “Arc shutdown migrate workflow”.
- The proposed spread trigger is socially acceptable: a task-level exit card shows coworkers exactly which blocker remains and which browser is ready.

## UNKNOWN

- Real willingness to pay and the final economic buyer.
- Whether the source pain repeats often enough outside the observed community.
- Which integrations or hardware constraints would dominate production.
- Whether the first validation test produces a meaningful behavior change.
