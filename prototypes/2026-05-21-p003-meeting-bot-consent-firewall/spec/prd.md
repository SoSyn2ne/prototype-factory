# PRD - Meeting Bot Consent Firewall

## One-liner
A pre-call control room that blocks risky AI notetakers, collects consent, and creates a defensible share policy for sensitive meetings.

## Category
Practical / Commercial

## Target user
Agencies, consultants, HR teams, board secretaries, healthcare operators, and legal-adjacent teams that use AI meeting notes but worry about consent, auto-sharing, and client trust.

## Problem
- The user has a timely, stressful workflow exposed by the referenced external/community signals.
- Existing tools are either too generic, too fragmented, or not shaped around the immediate decision moment.

## Key UX
Before each sensitive meeting, the host chooses meeting type and risk level, sees allowed/blocked bot behavior, sends consent language, and gets a share-safe summary policy.

## Required UI sections
- Meeting risk classifier
- Bot/recorder detection and allowed tools list
- Consent language generator
- Participant opt-out tracker
- Transcript retention and sharing rules
- Audit log and client-safe summary preview

## Design profile
operator-dense

## Theme
자산 방어

## Signal references
- Cyber Unit: AI meeting recorders collect data from people who may not have agreed
- Yahoo Finance / Canadian Press: AI meeting tools carry privacy and distribution risks

## Stitch prompt
Create a responsive B2B control room called "Meeting Bot Consent Firewall" for teams that need to manage AI notetakers before sensitive calls. The product classifies meeting risk, detects or lists AI bots, shows allowed/blocked recorder behavior, generates consent language, tracks participant opt-outs, sets retention and sharing rules, and produces a client-safe summary preview plus audit log. Required screens/sections: meeting queue, risk score strip, bot policy matrix, consent composer, participant tracker, retention settings, audit trail, and safe-summary preview. Design direction: use the operator-dense profile with crisp compliance-oriented tables, compact controls, subdued risk colors, and a serious enterprise workflow feel.
